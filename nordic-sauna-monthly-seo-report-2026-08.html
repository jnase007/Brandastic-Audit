Opportunity

Nordic Sauna holds 1.2% AI share of voice (California locale) with 25 mentions (4.6% mention share), 25 own-domain citations (0.9%), 92% favorable sentiment, and average answer position 3.28. Finnleo leads at 6.5% SOV / 132 mentions; Harvia is close at 6.0% / 127. ChatGPT share is only 0.18% — far behind the multi-platform SOV and a clear voice gap. AI platforms recognize Nordic's heritage, engineering rigor, custom space optimization, durability, and design-team collaboration, but do not consistently place the brand on California's first-call shortlist. Nordic is often framed as premium or expensive rather than as the specialist for complex, code-sensitive, architect-led, long-life projects. Third-party citation weight sits with Vulcon, YouTube, Tahoe Sauna Company, and YourSaunas — not nordicsauna.com.

Recommendations
  • Codify a focused promise around California-proven engineering for complex custom rooms, multi-room suites, and risk-sensitive projects.
  • Publish a Fit/Not-Fit decision guide so architects, GCs, homeowners, and AI platforms know where Nordic is the strongest fit — and where simple or budget builds should go elsewhere.
  • Build third-party proof via regional builder lists, architect features, SweatDecks coverage, and Preferred Builder partnerships to lift SOV beyond the 1–2% band.
  • Close the ChatGPT gap (0.18% → 1% target) with California-focused reference guides and structured FAQs, plus a visible service charter (1–5-year commitments, installation roadmaps, satisfaction metrics).
