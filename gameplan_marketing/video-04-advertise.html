How to Advertise Your Agency

~59 min. You already watched it — this is the documented deep briefing for the team and for action.

What this video is

How he advertises the agency itself. History (SEO → gift-card customer generation at huge LinkedIn scale → commoditization → evolved cheaper plays), then the OS: TAM, offer, sales process, financial model, channels, measurement.

Beat-by-beat

Credentials

  • SEO first, then gift-card-for-demo “customer generation.”
  • ~$270K/mo LinkedIn; 10K accounts; 6,800 calls / 18 mo; $6M→$22M / 18 mo.
  • Tactic commoditized after he taught it; still works evolved + cheaper plays. No templates (IP).

TAM first

  • Not outbound junk. Ads aren’t free. Positioning first.
  • Account list, verify manually (10× time). Audience + offer forever.

Before ads

  • Proposal-held win rate ~20–40% (over 40% often underpriced).
  • CRM stages + Gong-class AE visibility required.

Channels

  • Meta crushes webinars / upmarket. Monthly webinar with delivery leads.
  • LinkedIn webinar CAC often breaks; use content clips + ABM.
  • Gift cards manufacture social intent; show-up required; activation > acquisition.
  • “Unlock audit” non-incentive retarget never worked for him.

What to advertise

  • Not capability to threatened practitioners — division/outcome to change-agent buyers.
  • Only TAM slices with CAC room (tech/industrial; skip low-spend services).
  • Parent + division sub-brands.

Measurement

  • CAPI on Meta + LinkedIn. Influence revenue, not last-click (CTR ceiling ~4–6%).

Brandastic should

  1. Verified TAM after ICP lock.
  2. Sales hygiene before scale.
  3. One offer (webinar or show-up incentive).
  4. Meta first for webinars.
  5. Economic-buyer creative.
  6. Influence pipeline monthly; CAPI on.
  7. New-biz ad cap separate from client media.

Full actions →

One sentence: Advertise only after TAM, offer, sales process, and influence metrics are real — Meta webinars and activation incentives, not spray capability ads.
