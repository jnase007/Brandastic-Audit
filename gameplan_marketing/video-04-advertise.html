How to Advertise Your Agency — copyable playbook

You watched it. This is the operator manual: tools, ICP targeting, offers, channels, math.

Results he quoted (why listen)

  • Customer generation / gift-card-for-demo pioneer.
  • ~$270K/mo LinkedIn at peak.
  • 10,000 target accounts → 6,800 sales calls in 18 months → $6M to $22M in 18 months.
  • Then commoditized; evolved; still uses incentives; lower spend plays now.

Step 0–1: Positioning + TAM (with Primer)

  1. If positioning boils the ocean, ads can’t.
  2. Build a real account-level TAM — not outbound email junk (emails free, ads not).
  3. Verify every account (week of work; “10× under” your estimate). He built AI agents to open websites and verify list fit; still human-owned.
  4. Primer (and similar) to upload/match the list into ad platforms — he tests list-based vs native algo every time (“non-native and impersonative/native”).
  5. First principle forever: Audience + Offer.

ICP / who the ads talk to

  • Do not advertise SEO to an SEO manager (threat). Advertise to a VP of Marketing who wants to fire/replace the current setup.
  • Advertise division/department + outcome, not raw capability laundry lists.
  • Only advertise TAM slices with CAC room: technology + industrial; skip low-spend services TAM even if the website covers it (use other motions there).
  • After saturating tech TAM with performance for years, re-enter same TAM with communications offer.

Sales + finance codependencies (tools)

  • HubSpot marketing · Salesforce sales · Gong for AE management/visibility.
  • Stages disciplined or capital underperforms.
  • Win-rate model from proposal held → close: not <20%, not >40% (over 40% ≈ underpriced).
  • Live hygiene example he showed: intros, strats, proposals, SOWs, legal amounts moving.

Channel playbook

Meta

  • Best for webinars — crushing; upmarket CMOs possible.
  • Monthly webinar with people client will work with (not only CEO face as you go upmarket).
  • Integrates thought leadership + lead gen; raises brand experience for higher ACV (drop “book a control call / explore partnership” cheapness).

LinkedIn

  • Home of old gift-card machine at $270K/mo.
  • Webinar CAC often fails vs Meta CPM/CPC math — don’t force it.
  • Mix content clips so not pure DR.

Incentives

  • Search = intent, weak graphics for upmarket services story.
  • Social = graphics, weak intent → gift cards manufacture intent.
  • No card without attending pitch → lower cost per held intro even with gift cost.
  • Activation > acquisition.
  • Non-incentive retarget (“unlock your audit”) — never worked for him (“dog shit”) even with good creative.

Brand system in ads

  • Parent brand + division sub-brands (performance / communications / commerce) with own colors/guidelines.
  • Unity without looking like Power-style “everything / master of none.”

Measurement

  • Set up CAPI on LinkedIn and Meta.
  • Optimize to influence revenue, not last-click/first-touch vanity.
  • Best Meta CTR ~4–6% ⇒ ~94% of exposed people never click — last-click will make you cut spend wrongly.

Brandastic implementation order

  1. ICP lines written (titles, firmographics, exclusions) — VP/Director marketing pattern from V02 unless we prove otherwise.
  2. Verified TAM spreadsheet + Primer (or equivalent) ready for match.
  3. CRM stages + proposal-held win rate dashboard.
  4. One offer path: Meta webinar or incentive show-up offer.
  5. CAPI + influence reporting before scaling.
  6. Division/outcome messaging only; no practitioner-threat ads.
  7. New-biz budget cap separate from client media.
