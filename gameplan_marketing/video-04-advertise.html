How to Advertise Your Agency — full playbook

You watched it. This is the operator manual with tools (including Primer), ICP/VP targeting, offers, channels, and math — so the team can execute.

1. Proof points he stated

  • Early growth: SEO guest posts; ranked agency terms; Clutch/review sites
  • Commercial SEO harder → advertising
  • Pioneer of gift card for a demo ads (SaaS + own agency)
  • Method name: Customer Generation
  • Peak LinkedIn ~$270,000/month
  • 10,000 target accounts
  • 6,800 sales calls in 18 months
  • $6M → $22M in 18 months off these ads
  • Taught publicly → commoditized; still works evolved; lower-spend plays added
  • No creative templates shared (Directive IP) — copy the structure

2. First principles (in order)

  1. Build a real TAM — account list you actually want
  2. Not outbound junk lists — emails free, ads not free
  3. Positioning first — boiling-ocean agency cannot be fixed by ads
  4. Advertising always = Audience + Offer (he repeats this)
  5. Account-level list, manually verify (time estimate ×10; can take a week)
  6. He built AI agents to open websites and verify list fit; humans still own final quality
  7. Upload/match lists with Primer (and similar) into ad platforms
  8. Always test list-based vs native algorithm audiences

3. ICP / who ads talk to (critical)

  • Talk to VP of Marketing / economic buyer who wants change
  • Example: advertise SEO to a VP who wants to fire/replace current SEO person or agency
  • Do not advertise SEO to an SEO manager — they see you as a threat; conversion dies
  • Advertise division/department + outcome, not capability laundry lists as primary
  • Prefer macro / AOR positioning when possible (“value is buying it together starting with marketing”)

Which TAM gets paid spend

  • Macros again: technology / industrial / services
  • Do not advertise Services TAM — CAC will not clear (they do not spend enough)
  • Advertise Technology + Industrial
  • Website can show more than you advertise
  • After saturating tech TAM for years with performance, re-enter same TAM with communications offer

When a service is ready for ads

  • Nacent services he still advertises if he believes he can sustain them (comms/PR)
  • Legacy he cannot monetize (example RevOps) → do not advertise
  • Gauge: MVD required vs real scalable division

4. Before ads: sales + finance codependencies

Tools

  • HubSpot — marketing
  • Salesforce — sales
  • Gong — AE management / truth (“managing AEs without it is pouring salt in your eyeballs”)

Process

  • Stage discipline required
  • Spreadsheet + no CRM/stage hygiene → capital underperforms once not only founder selling

Financial model

  • From proposal held → close (pitch given, not merely SOW signed)
  • Win-rate band:
    • Below ~20% → positioning/pricing problem
    • Above ~40% → often underpricing
    • Directive aims roughly 30–40%
  • Know spend capacity from model before media plan

Free product incentives he ranked (do not skip)

  1. AirPods (#1)
  2. Kindle (#2)
  3. Amazon gift card (#3) — live ad uses $105 (charm: $105 not $100; 29 minutes not 30)

Non-cash lead magnets: he has not found one that works. “Unlock your audit” fails. Full ad copy, convo-ad name trick, and 3-line rule on the Channels page.

5. Channel playbooks

Meta

  • Meta is for webinars — absolutely crushing
  • Can go upmarket (CMO-quality examples)
  • Monthly webinar with people client will actually work with if they hire you
  • Big-agency face is not only CEO; design experience for higher ACV
  • Thought leadership + lead gen together
  • Moved off cheap CTAs (“book a control call / explore partnership”)

LinkedIn

  • Historic home of gift-card customer generation at $270K/mo
  • Webinar CAC often fails first-principles math vs Meta → webinars on Meta
  • Still run LinkedIn; include content clips so not pure DR
  • List match via Primer-class tools; test vs native

Incentives / offers

  • Search: intent, weak graphics for upmarket services story
  • Social: graphics, weak intent → gift cards manufacture intent
  • No gift unless they show up to the pitch
  • Even with gift cost, cost per held intro fell
  • Invest in activation more than acquisition
  • Non-incentive retarget (“unlock your audit”) — never worked for him (“dog shit”) despite good creative

6. Brand architecture in paid

  • Parent brand (e.g. purple)
  • Sub-brands per division: Performance / Communications / Commerce (own logo/colors/guidelines)
  • Unity in AOR positioning + differentiation in GTM
  • Constraint so you do not look like you do everything and nothing

7. Measurement

  • Set up CAPI on LinkedIn and Meta
  • Primary lens: influence revenue, not last-click / first-touch vanity
  • Best Meta CTR maybe ~4–6% → ~94% of exposed audience never clicks → last-click will make you cut spend wrongly
  • Report revenue ads influenced, not only directly drove

8. Media organization

  • Organize by divisions on site/media plan
  • Do not stop/start ads chaotically — build continuously
  • Industries may exist hidden; macros are the paid spine

9. Brandastic ticket list (execute in order)

  1. Write ICP one-pager: titles (VP/Dir Marketing default), company size, industries, exclusions
  2. Build account TAM; verify (human + optional AI site checks)
  3. Load to platforms via Primer (or equivalent); test list vs native
  4. CRM stages + proposal-held win-rate dashboard (20–40% band)
  5. Call visibility (Gong-class discipline)
  6. Choose ONE primary offer path:
    • Meta webinar with delivery leads, OR
    • Incentive show-up offer (gift card rules)
  7. Do not lead with free-audit retarget as primary
  8. Creative to change-agent buyers; division + outcome language
  9. CAPI on; weekly/monthly influence pipeline + revenue report
  10. Cap Brandastic new-biz ads; separate from client media (Video 01 rule)
  11. Only spend against TAM slices with real ACV/CAC room
