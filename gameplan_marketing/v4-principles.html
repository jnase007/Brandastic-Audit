First principles: TAM, Primer, verify, Audience + Offer

1. Positioning first

  • If agency boils the ocean, ads cannot fix it.
  • Positioning must allow a defined paid audience.

2. Real TAM

  • Account-level want-list — not outbound junk.
  • Emails free; ads not free.
  • Verify every account; time estimate ×10; can take a week.
  • AI agents can click sites to help verify; humans own quality.

3. Primer

  • Primer uploads TAM into ad platforms.
  • Always test list-based vs native algorithm audiences.
  • List still matters for control of accounts you care about.

4. Audience + Offer

  • Advertising = Audience and Offer every time.
  • Creative is downstream.
  • Scaled offer: gift-card customer generation (see Channels).
  • Peak: 10K accounts; 6,800 calls; $6M→$22M; ~$270K/mo LinkedIn.

5. Brandastic tickets

  1. Positioning one-pager.
  2. TAM spreadsheet + verification sprint.
  3. Primer (or equivalent) + list vs native tests.
  4. Define Offer before creative scale.
