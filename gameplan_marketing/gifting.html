Client gifts (Goody)

When something real happens for a client or contact — new job, hard week, big win — we send a thoughtful gift fast. We use Goody so we don’t chase shipping addresses or shop Amazon for an hour.

What this is

Goody is a business gifting tool. You pick a gift (or a price and let them choose). They enter their address. It ships. You can send a nice note with it.

Why it matters

The friend agency said letting the team “love on clients” without red tape was one of the best growth habits they had. Not expensive gifts. Personal ones. Under about $100.

How Brandastic will do it

  1. Create a Goody for Business account with our logo and company card.
  2. Make three gift “collections” (preset lists): about $50, $75, and $100.
  3. Open a Slack channel called #client-love.
  4. Anyone who works with clients can post: who, why, which collection, how urgent.
  5. Erin or Aimme sends the Goody the same day or next morning.
  6. We keep a monthly gift budget about $750–$1,000. Sandy checks the total monthly — we do not need Justin to approve every $60 gift.
  7. When the person says thank you, paste it back in the Slack thread so the teammate feels the win (that keeps people doing it).

When to send a gift

  • They start a new job
  • They launch something big
  • They had a brutal week
  • They referred us
  • Contract anniversary / personal milestone (baby, etc.)

You’re done when

Goody is live, three collections exist, Slack channel exists, and we’ve sent at least 5 gifts in the first 30 days.
