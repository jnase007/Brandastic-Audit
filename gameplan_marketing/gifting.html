Revenue driver

Client gifts with Goody

When something real happens in a client’s life or business, we send a thoughtful gift quickly. We use Goody so the team does not waste time shopping or hunting addresses.

What this is

Goody (ongoody.com) lets you send a gift by email/text. The person picks/confirm and enters their address. You can also set a budget and let them choose.

Why we are doing it

The friend agency found that letting the team take care of clients without approval theater built trust and referrals. Cheap personal gifts beat expensive generic ones. Keep most gifts under $100.

Who does what

PersonJob
Anyone on a client (AM, Justin, Melissa)Notices the moment and posts in Slack
Erin or AimmeSends the Goody gift
SandyWatches monthly spend (not each gift)

Monthly pool to start: $750–$1,000.

Step-by-step

Open Goody for Business

Sandy or Erin

Create the account at ongoody.com. Add Brandastic logo and company card. Send a test gift to yourself once.

Build three collections

Erin

Make three saved lists (or budget tiers):

  • ~$50 — small thank-you / hard week
  • ~$75 — job change / congrats
  • ~$100 — big win or important relationship

Or use Goody’s “choose your gift” at those dollar amounts when you do not know their taste.

Create Slack #client-love

Sandy

Pin this format so requests are complete:

Who: Jane at Acme (email) Why: She started a new CMO job today Collection: $75 job change Note tone: proud / warm Needed by: tomorrow

Teach the team when to post

Justin in a 10-min huddle

Post when: new job, launch, brutal week, referral given, personal milestone, anniversary of working together. Do not post random “just because” spam.

Send and close the loop

Erin/Aimme

Send in Goody same day or next morning. Log in CRM. If they reply thanks, paste it in the Slack thread so the requester sees it.

Done looks like this

Account live, three tiers ready, channel live with pinned format, five real gifts sent in 30 days, monthly spend visible to Sandy.
