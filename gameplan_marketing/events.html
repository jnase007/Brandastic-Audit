Revenue driver

Client events (Costa Mesa)

Once a month we host a small, fun night for clients and possible clients — cooking class or wine tasting. No sales pitch. People bring a guest. The goal is real relationships that later turn into meetings and referrals.

What this is

A private group experience in Orange County (near us). Think pasta class or wine night, not a conference booth and not a stiff private dining room with slides.

Why it matters

The friend agency found fancy dinners had no-shows and high cost. Hands-on nights + allowing a plus-one got people to actually show up. They got renewals, referrals, and warmer relationships — not mostly cold brand-new logos from the night itself.

What success looks like

  • People show up
  • Within 30 days, a few real meetings happen
  • Clients introduce us to someone later

Places to call first (Costa Mesa area)

  1. Sur La Table at South Coast Plaza — cooking classes. Good first event because people do something with their hands and talk naturally.
    surlatable.com cooking classes
  2. Argaux — private wine tasting in Costa Mesa. Feels special without being a stiff sales dinner.
    argaux.com private venue
  3. La Mesa Wine Works — private tasting room option.
    lamesawineworks.com

Backup: host at our office with wine dropped off + simple food if venues are booked.

First things to do

  1. Sandy emails Sur La Table and Argaux for a private group quote (14–18 people).
  2. Pick a date and put it on the company calendar.
  3. Make a simple spreadsheet: name, company, client or prospect, plus-one, RSVP, follow-up.
  4. AMs list 12 clients. Melissa lists 12 prospects.
  5. Justin sends the invite (see Invite email example). Allow plus-ones. No pitch language.
  6. After the night: thank-you next day. Personal note from Justin within 3 days to prospects. Log everything.

You’re done when

Date is booked, deposit is paid, at least 12 people confirmed, and follow-up tasks exist for after the event.
