Video 01 · Events
Revenue driver

Client nights (detailed)

Private experiential nights — not conference sponsorships.

Numbers: ~$8,500/mo all-in · up to ~25 seats · 50/50 VP+ prospects/customers · plus-one · pre intro call · book ~90 days out · no pitch.

What failed

  • Conference sponsorships hard to monetize
  • Fancy dinners: no-shows + price jumps after ~10
  • Sports suites ~50% no-show

What works

  • Experiential (pasta, wine, activity + dinner)
  • Plus-one kills no-shows
  • Event person does pre-call (not AE pitch)
  • Anchor 1–3 VIPs first
  • Outcomes: renewals, cross-sell, referrals

Brandastic SOP

  1. OC venue + date
  2. 20 prospects + 20 clients
  3. Short invite
  4. Intro calls
  5. Follow-ups next day
