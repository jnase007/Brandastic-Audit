Brandastic GTM / 1. Client nights
Revenue driver

Client nights in Costa Mesa

Once a month we host a small hands-on night (cooking or wine) for clients and people we want as clients. Guests can bring someone. We do not pitch Brandastic. We build relationships on purpose so meetings and referrals happen later.

What this is

A private group experience near our office — not a conference booth, not a webinar, not a stiff dinner with slides. People cook or taste wine, talk, and leave liking us.

Why we are doing it

A friend agency learned that big sponsorships and fancy private dining often wasted money (no-shows, high cost per seat). Hands-on nights plus allowing a plus-one got people to show up. They saw better renewals, cross-sell, and referrals from relationship time — not a hard sell at the table.

At our size, one solid night a month is enough to start.

Rules of the night

  • Budget to start: $2,500–$4,000 all-in
  • About 12–20 people
  • Half clients / half prospects (owners, marketing directors, CMOs)
  • Plus-one always allowed — this is how no-shows drop
  • No pitch, no deck, no “quick Brandastic overview”
  • Success = show-ups + real meetings in the next 30 days — not Instagram photos

Where to host (call these first)

  1. Sur La Table cooking class — South Coast Plaza, Costa Mesa. Best first event because people are busy with their hands and conversation is easy.

  2. Argaux private wine night — Costa Mesa venue built for private groups.

  3. La Mesa Wine Works — private tasting room option.
    Open La Mesa private events

If dates are full: host at the Brandastic office with wine service dropped off and simple catering.

Step-by-step (do in order)

Get prices for two venues

Sandy

Email Sur La Table and Argaux. Ask for a private group for 14–18 guests on two possible weeknights in the next 6–8 weeks. Ask what is included (food, drinks, tax, gratuity) and plus-one policy.

Subject: Private group cooking class / wine tasting quote — Brandastic Hi — We’re Brandastic, a marketing agency in Costa Mesa. We want to host a private hands-on night for about 14–18 clients and friends (plus-ones welcome). No presentation. Looking at weeknights in the next 6–8 weeks. Can you send package options and total all-in cost?

Lock the date

Sandy + Justin

Pick the venue and date. Put it on the company calendar. Pay the deposit. Create a folder in Drive: Events / YYYY-MM.

Make the guest spreadsheet

Aimme

Columns: Name, Company, Email, Type (Client or Prospect), Plus-one name, RSVP (Yes/No/Maybe), Invited by, Follow-up owner, Notes.

Fill the list

AMs + Melissa

AMs add 12 clients who like us and would enjoy a night out. Melissa adds 12 prospects who can buy (owner or marketing lead). Better to invite ~24 to seat ~15.

Justin sends invites

Justin

Use the invite on the Invite email page. Email or LinkedIn. One follow-up only. No PDF agenda.

Confirm and remind

Aimme

Three days before: address, parking, end time, “bring your plus-one.” Day of: short reminder at noon.

Host the night

Justin + one teammate

Be a good host. Introduce people. Take a couple photos if natural. Do not talk packages.

Follow up

Justin + Aimme

Next day: thank-you text/email. Within 3 days: Justin personal note to each prospect about something they said. Log meetings that come from it. Tag CRM with event_YYYY_MM.

Done looks like this

Venue booked, deposit paid, 12+ confirmed guests, night happened, every prospect has a follow-up note or clear “not a fit,” and we know if any meetings came from it within 30 days.

This is a working handbook. Each page should be clear enough that someone can do the job without a meeting.
