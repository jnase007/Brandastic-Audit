Revenue driver

Client nights (private experiential events)

From the bookings masterclass: stop burning money on conference sponsorships you can’t monetize. Run one private experiential night a month.

Their numbers: ~$8,500 all-in per event · 1× / month · up to ~25 people on experiential · mix 50% prospects (VP+) / 50% customers · plus-one required for show rates.

What works (their words)

  • Pasta making, wine tasting, activity + dinner — not only private-room steak houses
  • Experiential keeps cost/seat down at higher headcount
  • Fancy dinners: no-shows + price jumps after ~10 seats
  • Plus-one = date night commitment → attendance jumps
  • Intro call before the night so dinner isn’t a pitch
  • No agenda theater, no hard sell at the table
  • Success so far: renewals, cross-sell, referrals (not mainly cold new logos)
  • One internal owner runs the whole motion

SOP — Brandastic client night (pilot)

  1. Pick city / room: OC / SoCal first (we can travel later)
  2. Budget: set a pilot budget (scale toward $8.5K only when process works; first night can be smaller)
  3. List: 50% ICP prospects (VP/Dir/Owner from Dental/Med, CalOPS peers, or Aero as relevant) + 50% happy clients
  4. Invite: short screenshot-able text + plus-one (use invite template)
  5. RSVP → intro call: 15 min, notes shared with attending team; no business pitch at dinner
  6. Night: activity + dinner; execs hang; capture follow-ups same night / next morning
  7. Follow-up: personal thank-you + one useful next step (audit, reopen, referral ask) — not a deck dump
  8. Score: show rate, meetings created, expand/referral pipeline, cost per attendee
