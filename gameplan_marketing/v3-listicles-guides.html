Listicles, guides, Clutch, AI overview — full recipe

Listicle recipe (do in order)

  1. Every entry on anchor tags.
  2. Duplicate anchors in sidebar and body — must match exactly.
  3. Jump links to each entry.
  4. Modules: services · stand out · clients · best for · why choose.
  5. Near top: commercial intent block — “finding a paid media partner — what to look for.”
  6. TL;DR / top 5, then full list.
  7. AI draft + editorial pass.
  8. Internally link the whole cluster (Video 01 8-piece: guide + listicle + buyer guide + 5 TL posts).
  9. Weak first versions often too few entries (~9); expand.

Guides

  • Actually valuable long asset.
  • Strong internal links can rank #1.
  • AI writing OK if quality is real.
  • Rehab Legion example: low DA still #1 when execution got serious.

SERP + AI overview

  • Goal: own listicle + appear on others’ lists + ad + AI overview cite.
  • If AI cites Clutch → pay to win (~$800 example to #1).
  • Prefer 1:1 B2B lists.
  • Share-serve: ads ~4% CTR max → be everywhere on commercial intent.
  • Some systems ~15–25 form fills/day at their scale.

Pay / production

  • $500 employee moonlight / $350–500 freelance per article.
  • Boat sprint ~$65–68K example.
  • Freelancers for commodity; internal for methodology/brand POV.
