Listicles, guides, Clutch, AI overview — full recipe

Listicle recipe (do in order)

  1. Every entry on anchor tags.
  2. Duplicate anchors in sidebar AND body — must match (e.g. Web Mechanics #15 both places).
  3. Jump/click-down navigation to each entry.
  4. Modules: services · stand out · clients · best for · why choose.
  5. Near top: commercial intent block — “finding a paid media partner — what to look for.”
  6. TL;DR / top 5, then full list (“meat and potatoes”).
  7. Heavy AI draft + editorial pass.
  8. Internally link everything; take it seriously.
  9. Weak first versions often too few entries (~9); expand properly.
  10. Connect to Video 01 8-piece: guide + listicle + buyer guide + 5 thought leadership around each node; TL posts link to each other with in-copy anchors.

Guide recipe

  • Actually valuable long educational asset.
  • Strong internal linking can take a guide to #1.
  • AI writing OK if quality high (they said this explicitly).
  • Example: rehab “complete guide” style assets that rank #1.
  • Rehab Legion: low DA, lesser-known brand — same advice worked once execution got serious.

SERP + AI overview market share

  • On commercial-intent queries for services/subservices, show up as: own listicle + others’ listicles + ad + AI overview cite.
  • Inspect AI overview citations; if pulling Clutch → pay to win (~$800 example to hit #1).
  • When AI re-scrapes → overview + organic helped.
  • Prefer 1:1 B2B lists over bloated generic lists unless chasing macro query (best B2B marketing agencies).
  • Share-serve theory: ads max ~4% CTR on a query → ~96% of searches still go elsewhere — be everywhere on commercial queries.
  • Some listicle systems: ~15–25 form fills/day at their scale.
  • Prioritize genuine commercial intent for services sold; don’t only chase non-commercial volume.

Pay / production context

  • $500 employee moonlight / $350–500 freelance per article.
  • Boat sprint ~$65–68K example.
  • Freelancers for commodity; internal for methodology/brand POV.
