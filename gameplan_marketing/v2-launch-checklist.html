VIDEO 02

30-day launch kit — every artifact

Copy into Asana/Notion. This is the checklist from the launch training.

People

  1. Hire craft depth (example: Head of PR from NP Digital — performance-shop background + deepest LLM/PR POV).
  2. Aim for two people in a new competency.
  3. Existing leader may get one expert hire under them.
  4. Wedge offer first if a related engine exists (their paid social under comms: ~15 people, ~$3M P&L; ~$15K/mo “rank better on LLMs / digital PR” easier than brand theater first).
  5. No middle manager on day one.
  6. Trigger: after ~five strategists or ~five accounts → manager training + onboarding.
  7. Commerce lead waited longer without paid-social revenue.
  8. Resource ask = timeline trade (“hit goal 3 months faster?”).
  9. Philosophy: protect people from expectations.

Research (when weak in category)

  1. Interview heads of marketing (examples: Grainger, Snap-on, Schneider-class industrial).
  2. Weeds questions: agency problems, tools like Feedonomics, what they’d do different, why.
  3. Same question set every interview + recorder.
  4. Do not invent methodology until research finishes.
  5. CEO doesn’t micromanage how interviews are booked — requires research outcome.

Methodology and sales assets

  1. Build POV from research.
  2. Discoverability OS decks — two parallel versions: non-ecommerce vs B2B ecommerce (contribution margin differs; one deck creates sales friction).
  3. Integrate new division into performance methodology where needed (comms as earned + owned).
  4. Decks: overall division deck + channel decks (paid social, influencer, PR, etc.).
  5. SOWs for each offer.
  6. North Star Metric (NSM) tracker — if you take money, show whether it works.
  7. Proposal pages.

Website + brand

  1. Service pages.
  2. Methodology pages.
  3. Proposal pages.
  4. Division logo.
  5. Sub-brand guidelines.
  6. Content pack: listicle(s) + guide(s) + ~5 thought-leadership pieces (or two guides) — same family as Video 01’s 8-piece system.
  7. Bring technology/content from existing stack.
  8. PR tech note: Meltwater over Precision.

Systems

  1. Salesforce: parent/child so multi-service form fills don’t break SF; new division field; children under performance/commerce/comms; keep product names; report pipe by division.
  2. Legal: contracts assignable to any of your businesses (multi-brand + future asset sale).
  3. Pricing: post-intro debrief → hours × cost rate × regional multiplier × fully weighted overhead → price by division/capability.
  4. Intro call redesign: meet them where they are; expand to the outcome, not only the named service; open adjacent offers that serve that outcome.

Timing + GTM dependencies

  • Target: new department live ~30 days when kit exists.
  • CEO = what/why macros; leaders = how (must own it emotionally).
  • Also roadmap: PR motion, budget allocation by channel/TAM/offer, creative briefs, videos, brand campaigns, organic rank content, return-POC messaging for new offers, client-strategy reporting, titles/JDs/comp/hiring, white space mapping, finance bonuses/budgets/KPIs/banking, HR rate cards/taxes, training/onboarding/SOPs as you go.
