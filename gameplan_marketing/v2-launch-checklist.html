30-day launch kit — every artifact

Copy into project tickets. Full checklist from the launch training.

People

  1. Hire craft depth (example: Head of PR from NP Digital — performance background + LLM/PR depth).
  2. Aim for two people in a new competency.
  3. Existing leader may get one expert hire under them.
  4. Wedge offer first if related engine exists (their paid social under comms: ~15 people, ~$3M P&L; ~$15K/mo LLM/digital PR style offers easier than brand theater).
  5. No middle manager day one.
  6. Trigger: ~five strategists or ~five accounts → manager training/onboarding.
  7. Resource ask = timeline trade (“3 months faster?”).

Research (if weak)

  1. Interview heads of marketing (Grainger / Snap-on / Schneider-class examples).
  2. Weeds questions including Feedonomics, agency pain, what they’d do different.
  3. Same questions + recorder every time.
  4. No methodology until research done.

Assets

  1. POV from research.
  2. Discoverability OS decks — two versions: non-ecommerce vs B2B ecommerce.
  3. Overall division deck + channel decks (paid social, influencer, PR, etc.).
  4. SOWs.
  5. North Star Metric tracker.
  6. Service pages, methodology pages, proposal pages.
  7. Division logo + sub-brand guidelines.
  8. Content pack: listicle(s) + guide(s) + ~5 thought leadership (or two guides).
  9. PR tech note: Meltwater over Precision.
  10. Bring existing stack tech/content over.

Systems

  1. Salesforce parent/child + division field; report pipe by division.
  2. Contracts assignable across entities.
  3. Pricing: hours × cost rate × region multiplier × overhead → price by division/capability.
  4. Intro call expands to outcome, not only named service.

Timing: ~30 days when kit exists. CEO = what/why; leaders = how.
