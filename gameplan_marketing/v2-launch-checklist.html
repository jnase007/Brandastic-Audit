Launch checklist (their build list)

How they stood up a new service/division. Sequential guidelines — not micromanaging every sub-task. They said a new department can roll in about 30 days when this is ready.

Their order (from the talk)

  1. Market research if you lack depth — interview real buyers (heads of marketing). Same questions. Record. Example: Granger / industrial ecommerce buyers. Don’t invent methodology before research finishes.
  2. POV + methodology deck — they parallel-pathed “Discoverability OS” for ecommerce vs everyone else so sales doesn’t pitch the wrong story.
  3. Service pages on the site.
  4. Methodology pages.
  5. Proposal pages / decks — overall division deck + channel decks (e.g. social, influencer, PR) as needed.
  6. Sub-brand guidelines for the division.
  7. Content pack — listicle + guide(s) + about five thought-leadership pieces (same family as Video 01’s 8-piece idea).
  8. SOWs (scopes of work).
  9. North Star Metric tracker — if you take money, you must show whether it works.
  10. Tech stack choices for delivery.
  11. Training / onboarding.
  12. Middle manager trigger — not day one. Example they used: after roughly five strategists/accounts, then manager training. Too many resources too early shortens the time you give someone to succeed.

How they manage the work

  • CEO gives macro what + why (and maybe ~80% of a high-level checklist).
  • Division leader fills the how so they own it emotionally — otherwise rollout dies in 12 months.
  • Don’t micromanage every sub-bullet.

Done looks like this (for one new Brandastic offer)

Research or clear POV done · methodology deck · service page · proposal/SOW · NSM defined · content pack started · named owner · no extra hire until revenue trigger hit.
