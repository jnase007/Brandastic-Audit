VIDEO 02

Brandastic application — launch services

Turn Video 02 into tickets. Execute in order.

Week 1–2

  1. ICP one-pager: default personas VP/Director Marketing (or our proven buyers); list what we will NOT sell because it sits outside their KPIs.
  2. Service audit sheet: columns = Service | Could we win best RFP? (Y/N) | Only cross-sell today? (Y/N) | Keep / MVD / Kill | Owner.
  3. Be brutal on video/email/random extras that fail the RFP test.

Week 2–3

  1. Fix no-brainers (SEO ↔ paid honesty at our stage).
  2. Pick 0 or 1 incubation this half — only after vertical/ICP lock from Video 01 SEO work.
  3. If 0: stop. Improve depth on existing offers.
  4. If 1: name owner; Justin writes what/why macro one-pager.

If launching one offer — clone their kit

  1. Research interviews (if weak) with same question set + recorder.
  2. POV + methodology deck (split if ecommerce vs not, like Discoverability OS).
  3. Overall + channel decks, SOWs, NSM tracker.
  4. Service/methodology/proposal pages + sub-brand rules if needed.
  5. Content pack: listicle + guide(s) + ~5 thought leadership (Video 01 8-piece).
  6. CRM division/field hygiene so reporting doesn’t break.
  7. Contract assignability review with counsel.
  8. Hire trigger written: no manager until X accounts (their pattern ~5).
  9. Mon/Thu roadmap meeting while standing it up.

Ongoing

  1. NRR behaviors for AMs: upsell, cross-sell, referral, return POC after losses.
  2. Default: build, don’t buy agencies for capabilities.
  3. Multi-service accounts over a $ threshold get one owner (their >$20K AD pattern — set our number).
