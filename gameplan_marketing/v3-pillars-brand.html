Three pillars + founder brand — full detail

1. Three pillars of content

  1. Ego — authority / brand (“we’re the ones you’d hire”). Trust us.
  2. Methodology — how you work differently. For them: Discoverability OS / Customer Generation depth. Deliverable differentiation because you serve a defined buyer deeply in one industry/motion.
  3. Results — clients like your ICP who trusted you and got outcomes.

Depth of positioning must translate to depth and differentiation of the deliverable. If you only serve one type of person well, say so and prove it.

2. Founder brand / distribution mix

  • Historical grind that built domain authority: post every day, write for industry blogs, events, speaking, podcasts, YouTube.
  • When you write for everyone else first, your own site can start to rank.
  • Shift later: less pure LinkedIn combo DR dependence; more video/broader TAM + content system (ads $270K → ~$50K story).
  • LinkedIn is harder now; he historically blocked ~1 hour/day; team linking LinkedIn → blog is fine but not the whole system.

3. ICP jealousy hypothesis

  • Best way to grow the agency: make your ICP jealous of the work.
  • Only works if content, creative, and strategy are actually excellent — not performative.
  • Obsess micro moments (example: waiting-room video on Zoom) — becomes the #1 thing clients notice/feedback.

4. Campaigns → company POV flywheel

  • Example: Renaissance campaign promoting Communications division — “everything else evolved, your B2B comms didn’t.”
  • Motion ads of outdated approaches you would not do anymore.
  • When something works on their own marketing, he: builds training → puts in new-hire onboarding → writes into SOPs/SLPs → becomes client methodology.
  • Gift cards for demo pioneered on himself, then rolled to SaaS clients the same way.
  • He still runs marketing as CMO of Directive to stay at the bleeding edge.

5. Brandastic tickets

  1. Map all public content to ego / methodology / results — kill orphan posts with no pillar.
  2. Write methodology pages that match how we actually deliver (not generic agency speak).
  3. Proof library: results stories for our real ICP only.
  4. One high-craft campaign only when quality can create jealousy — not daily noise.
  5. Any tactic that works on Brandastic itself → document into training/onboarding for client work.
