VIDEO 04

What/who to advertise — full detail

Who

  • VP of Marketing / change agent.
  • SEO example: VP who wants to fire/replace current SEO — not the SEO manager (threat → conversion dies).

What

  • Division/department + outcome.
  • Not capability laundry lists as primary.
  • Macro/AOR positioning when possible.
  • Advertise nascent divisions you can sustain; don’t advertise legacy you can’t monetize (e.g. RevOps for them).

Which TAM gets spend

  • Technology + Industrial — yes.
  • Services — no paid (CAC won’t clear); other motions only.
  • Website can show more than you advertise.
  • Re-enter saturated TAM with new division offers (performance → then comms on same tech TAM).

Brand architecture

  • Parent brand + division sub-brands (Performance / Communications / Commerce).
  • Own colors/guidelines per division.
  • Unity without “we do everything / master of none.”

Brandastic tickets

  1. Who-we-advertise-to one-pager + exclusions.
  2. Rewrite angles as division + outcome.
  3. Pick paid TAM slices with real ACV.
  4. Sub-brand rules if multi-offer.
