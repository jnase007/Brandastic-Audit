How to Build a Sales Team

Exact AE hiring scorecard from the course file — what they screen for and the interview prompts.

Source: Copy_of_Scorecard_for_AE.xlsx

Who they’re hiring

AE with strong SME in performance marketing and SaaS. Skilled at prospecting, sales development, building presentations, pitching, negotiating, winning new business.

Scorecard objectives (rate 1–10)

ObjectiveInterview probe (exact)
SME for Performance MarketingRank yourself 1–10 on Paid, SEO, MarketingOps, Creative, Strategy. Have you done the work? Which campaigns, what did you do, deal size?
Proposal building + presentationWhat makes a great presentation? With client info, can you build strategy yourself or need ops support?
Hitting goalsTell me about a time you missed a number — what did you do to fix it?
Follow-upTell me about a deal you won that nobody thought you could — what was your follow-up process?
TechnologyExperience with Salesforce, Gong, Outreach, HubSpot?
Sales developmentHave you done SDR work? How will you always hit your proposal numbers here?

Brandastic translation

  • Same scorecard shape — swap SaaS SME for Dental / Edu / Aero curiosity + vertical proof
  • Keep tools: HubSpot (or our CRM), Gong-class call review if we add it
  • Must-build: proposal without heavy ops hand-holding on standard deals
