Video 03 FULL · Content Marketing 2026

Content Marketing 2026 — full playbook

Long-form. Print it. This is what they did with dollars, recipes, tools, meetings, and ICP logic.

1. Context numbers

  • Peak ads ~$270K/mo (2021–22 LinkedIn combo-style direct response)
  • Later ~$50K/mo while making more money + more TAM penetration via video/broader targeting + content system
  • Initial content “boat” sprint ~$65K–$68K
  • Pay per article: $500 employees moonlighting after hours; $350–$500 freelancers
  • Freelancers = commodity topics; internal = methodology/brand POV uniqueness
  • Claim at their scale: not under ~$2M/mo new business since the system
  • He books himself as a $20K account on the SEO consultant’s book (two $10K / ~20-hour units) so it counts toward their bonus / revenue under management
  • Default packaging reference: ~$10K ≈ 20 hours style units

2. Information architecture (exact)

  1. SEO/content IA starts with services and subservices
  2. Each page type has a defined use case
  3. Vertical/fit pages: still design to rank, but primary job is cognitive fit — “these guys do exactly what I need”
  4. Not endless thin industry pages as the spine
  5. B2B macros covering entire TAM:
    1. Technology
    2. Industrial
    3. Services
  6. Industries can exist hidden deeper — do not run industry spam as primary ad/content spine
  7. Spend map example ($50K): thread by division × TAM bucket × funnel stage × channel × audience × dollars (example $40K + $10K)
  8. IA and GTM must be one unified narrative

Capital allocation rules

  • Do not put advertising/content capital into Services TAM (CAC headroom too low)
  • Services TAM: email via Instantly VIP
  • Put capital into Industrial + Technology
  • Do not over-invest into Performance if the market already knows you for performance — push new divisions (Commerce, Communications)
  • Parent brand colors (purple/black) on home/global; each division gets own color + sub-brand on its pages

3. Production system

  • Mode A: employee moonlight @ $500/live article after hours
  • Mode B: freelancers $350–500 for non-POV commodity
  • Mode C: internal for methodology/brand-unique pieces
  • Boat sprint example: him + SEO consultant + FT content marketer; ~$65–68K
  • Quality bar: following the map without execution detail fails (Rehab Legion: methodology present, execution weak → fixed → #1 ranks despite lower DA)

4. Listicle recipe (copy step by step)

  1. All entries on anchor tags
  2. Duplicate anchors in sidebar AND body — must match (e.g. Web Mechanics #15 both places)
  3. Jump links to each entry
  4. Modules: services · stand out · clients · best for · why choose
  5. Near top: commercial intent block — “finding a paid media partner — what to look for”
  6. TL;DR / top 5 then full list
  7. AI draft + editorial pass
  8. Internally link everything seriously
  9. Weak first versions often too few entries (~9); expand

Guide recipe

  • Actually valuable long educational asset
  • Strong internal linking
  • AI OK if quality high (they said this explicitly)
  • Example: rehab complete guide style assets ranking #1

Interlink with Video 01 8-piece system

  • Per service/subservice/category node: ultimate guide + listicle + buyer guide + 5 thought leadership
  • Thought leadership links to each other with in-copy anchors and back to service hub

5. SERP + AI overview market share plan

On commercial-intent queries for services/subservices, show up as:

  1. Your own ranking listicle
  2. Mentions on others’ ranking listicles
  3. An ad in the SERP journey
  4. Citation in AI overview

Clutch / directory play

  • Example query: best B2B PR agencies
  • Inspect AI overview citations
  • If pulling Clutch → pay to win Clutch (~$800 example to hit #1)
  • When AI re-scrapes → overview + organic helped
  • Prefer 1:1 B2B lists over bloated generic lists unless chasing macro query
  • Share-serve theory: ads max ~4% CTR on a query → ~96% of searches still go elsewhere — be everywhere on commercial queries
  • Some listicle systems: ~15–25 form fills/day at their scale

Commercial intent focus

  • Prioritize queries with genuine commercial intent for services sold
  • Do not only chase non-commercial volume
  • Take market share on services + subservices

6. Three pillars

  1. Ego — authority/brand
  2. Methodology — Discoverability OS / Customer Generation depth; deliverable differentiation because you serve a defined buyer deeply
  3. Results — clients like your ICP who got outcomes

Depth of positioning must equal depth/differentiation of deliverable.

7. Founder brand + campaigns

  • Historical: post daily, write for everyone, events, speaking, podcasts, YouTube — built DA so own content could rank
  • Hypothesis: grow by making ICP jealous — only if quality is real
  • Obsess micro moments (waiting-room video on Zoom) — becomes #1 feedback
  • Renaissance campaign for Communications: “everything else evolved, your B2B comms didn’t” — motion ads of outdated approaches
  • Self-proven tactics (gift cards for demo) → training → onboarding → SLPs → client POV
  • He still runs marketing as CMO of his firm
  • LinkedIn harder now; historically ~1 hour/day; LinkedIn→blog links OK but not the whole system

8. Marketing team OS

  • Treat own company as a client
  • ~6 FT people: content, paid, customer marketing, creative (static+motion), revops/Salesforce hygiene; some also touch clients
  • No approval theater
  • Public walkthrough of ads/work; public accountability + public praise
  • Weekly SEO meeting since March 2025 (stop → slide)
  • Weekly paid + creative + lead with him; lead shadows for future marketing leadership
  • Still ~3 personal strategy sessions/day with COO/MD/VP — keep soul at scale
  • Proximity is CEO superpower

9. Brandastic ticket list

  1. IA rebuild from services/subservices on brandastic.com
  2. Lock 3 macros or 3–5 verticals — stop scatter
  3. Produce 8-packs with listicle recipe + editorial gate
  4. Commercial-query market share matrix (own list, 3rd-party lists, ads, AI overview sources)
  5. Clutch/directory budget where AI overviews cite them
  6. Weekly SEO/content meeting on calendar
  7. One high-craft campaign only when quality warrants jealousy
  8. Document content budget model ($ and/or AI+edit)
