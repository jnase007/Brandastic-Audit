Content Marketing 2026 — copyable playbook

How they wire content/SEO so you can duplicate the machine — tools, dollars, page recipes, meetings.

Context numbers

  • Ads: ~$270K/mo (2021–22 LinkedIn combo DR) → ~$50K/mo now; more money + broader TAM via video/broader targeting + content system.
  • Initial content boat sprint cost cited ~$65K–$68K.
  • Pay: $500/article employees moonlight after hours; $350–500 freelancers; internal POV pieces preferred for methodology/brand; freelancers for commodity topics.
  • Claim: not less than ~$2M/mo new business since the content system (their scale).
  • He treats himself as a $20K account (two $10K / 20-hr units) on the SEO consultant’s book so it counts to their bonus/RUM.

Information architecture (exact)

  1. Start with services + subservices.
  2. Vertical/fit pages: rank if possible; job = cognitive fit (“they do exactly what I need”).
  3. B2B macros: technology · industrial · services (entire TAM).
  4. Spend map example: $50K total → e.g. $40K + $10K threaded by division × TAM × funnel stage × channel × audience.
  5. Capital allocation: no ad/content spend on “services” TAM (too low CAC headroom) — use Instantly VIP email there; put capital into industrial + technology.
  6. Don’t over-spend content/ads on Performance division if market already knows you for it; push new divisions (commerce/comms).
  7. Parent brand colors (purple/black) on home; each division gets own color/sub-brand on its pages.

Listicle recipe (copy this)

  1. Anchor tags duplicated in sidebar + body (must match — “Web Mechanics at 15” both places).
  2. Jump links to each entry.
  3. Modules: services · stand out · clients · best for · why choose.
  4. Commercial-intent block near top: “finding a paid media partner — what to look for.”
  5. TL;DR / top 5, then full list.
  6. AI draft + editorial pass (their listicles).
  7. Guides: can be AI-only if structure/pro tips strong; internal links → can rank #1 (Rehab Legion example; low DA worked when execution got serious). First listicle versions failed when too few entries (~9).
  8. Internally link all 8 pieces around a node to each other + service page (thought leadership 1↔2 with in-copy anchors).

SERP + AI overview market share

  • Goal: show up on commercial-intent queries for services/subservices — own listicle + appear on others’ listicles + ad + AI overview mention.
  • Not only “beat Clutch organically” — take share everywhere (even ~4% max CTR on ads leaves 96% to other results).
  • Example: “best B2B PR agencies” → AI overview cites Clutch → paid ~$800 to #1 on Clutch → wait for AI rescrape + organic help.
  • Prefer 1:1 B2B lists over vague huge lists unless chasing macro “best B2B marketing agencies.”
  • Form fills cited ~15–25/day on some listicle systems at their scale.

Three pillars

  1. Ego — authority/brand (who you’d hire).
  2. Methodology — Discoverability OS / Customer Generation depth (deliverable differentiation because you only serve one type of buyer well).
  3. Results — clients like you who trusted us + outcomes.

Team / tools / cadence

  • ~6 FT marketing people as if Directive is a client: content, paid, customer marketing, creative (static+motion), revops/Salesforce hygiene, etc.; some also touch clients.
  • He still personal-audits strategy ~3 strategy sessions/day with COO/MD/VP — keep soul at scale.
  • No approvals; public walkthrough of ads; public praise.
  • Weekly SEO meeting since March 2025; paid+creative+lead weekly; lead shadows him.
  • When a tactic works on self (gift cards for demo), it becomes training → onboarding → SLPs → client POV.
  • Campaign example: Renaissance for communications (“everything else evolved, your B2B comms didn’t”) — motion ads; ICP jealousy hypothesis.
  • LinkedIn: harder now; he blocked ~1 hour/day historically; still posts; team linking LinkedIn→blog is fine but not the whole system.
