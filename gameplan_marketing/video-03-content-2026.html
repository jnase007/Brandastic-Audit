How To Use Content Marketing To Grow Your Agency In 2026

~45 min. Briefing written so you understand the whole training without rewatching. Detail pages under it match Video 01 depth.

What this video is

Same operator. He explains how he grew (and kept growing) while cutting pure direct-response ad dependence — moving from huge LinkedIn-combo style spend toward content + SEO architecture + video/broader reach.

It is not “post more on social.” It is a website information architecture + listicle/guide machine + SERP/AI overview tactics + founder-brand flywheel + weekly marketing team operating system.

What he said, beat by beat

1. Ads context

  • Old world: very heavy direct response (he again references the ~$270K/mo era).
  • Newer world: closer to ~$50K/mo, more motion/video, broader TAM targeting, more distribution — while still making money.
  • Point: content system + smarter media beats living only on combo ads.

2. Information architecture

  • Start with services and subservices.
  • Vertical/fit pages: try to rank, but main job is buyer cognition — “these guys do exactly what I need.”
  • Instead of endless industry pages: three B2B macros — technology, industrial, services — to cover the TAM.
  • Media plans thread: division × bucket × funnel stage × channel × audience × spend (example ~$50K mapped).

3. Guides

  • Must be genuinely useful.
  • Internal links can push a guide to #1.
  • AI writing is fine if quality bar is real (he said this outright).

4. Listicles (their recipe)

  • Matching anchor lists in sidebar and body.
  • Jump links to each entry.
  • Sections: services, standout, clients, best for, why choose.
  • Commercial intent block: “what to look for in a partner.”
  • TL;DR / top picks, then the full list.
  • AI draft + editorial.
  • Nothing magical — seriousness + internal linking.
  • Example: rehab vertical guide + “best SEO agency for rehabs” listicles (fixed after a weak first version).

5. SERP + AI overview

  • Pick query e.g. “best B2B PR agencies.”
  • See what AI overview cites (Clutch etc.).
  • Win that source (they paid ~$800 to hit #1 on Clutch) so AI re-scrape + organic both improve.
  • Plan for ads in the SERP journey too.

6. Three pillars of content

  1. Ego — trust / authority
  2. Methodology — how we work
  3. Results — proof

7. Founder brand + campaigns

  • Events, speaking, podcasts, YouTube, consistent personal content.
  • Hypothesis: grow by making ICP jealous — only if the work is actually excellent.
  • Example campaign “Renaissance” for communications division; when it works internally it becomes training, onboarding, SOPs, client POV.
  • He still runs marketing as CMO of his own firm to stay sharp.

8. Team operating system

  • No approval theater; public review of work; public praise too.
  • Weekly initiative meetings (SEO weekly since March 2025 — stop and it slides).
  • Lead who shadows + handles follow-through.
  • ~6 FT marketing people + shared SEO/paid on accounts.
  • Sellable story: CAC down, bookings up.
  • Proximity is non-negotiable if you want this to work.

  3. For money “best agency” terms: reverse AI overview citations.

  5. One high-quality campaign when creative deserves jealousy — not daily noise.
