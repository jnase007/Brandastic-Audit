How To Launch New Services/Offerings

~59 min masterclass. You should not need to rewatch. This briefing is what the video contained and what Brandastic should take from it — same depth standard as Video 01.

What this video is

A large agency operator (Directive) explains how he adds new service lines and divisions.

He tried buying agencies to get ecommerce and communications capability. That path was expensive and low quality. He switched back to building / incubating those capabilities inside the company.

Then he walks the actual kit you need to launch: research, methodology decks, website pages, proposals, scopes, metrics, content, training, and when you’re allowed to hire managers.

Underneath everything is a hard filter: only sell what you could win in a real competitive pitch.

What he said, beat by beat

1. Buying agencies was a bad deal

  • Founders he could afford were not better than leaders already inside.
  • Targets had revenue but weak contracts, weak differentiation, weak depth.
  • Paying a revenue multiple didn’t buy enough “other assets.”
  • Work-ethic mismatch with sellers.
  • Just running deal flow cost about $45,000/month.
  • He told sub-$10M agencies: this warning is for you too.

2. Incubation already worked for him

  • He’d grown a P&L to about $3M in under 12 months by incubating.
  • Cheaper, more control, more enjoyable than M&A theater.

3. What he’s building now

  • Platform strategy on a big performance base (he cited ~$30M performance P&L).
  • New divisions: commerce and communications.
  • B2B world simplified into three macros: technology · industrial · services.
  • CS fix: multi-service clients over ~$20K get an account director so SEO/media/creative stop running separate meeting stacks that create churn.

4. The launch kit (the heart of the video)

For a new service/division he builds, roughly in order:

  1. Market research if you’re weak in the category (interview real CMOs/heads of marketing; same questions; record). Don’t invent the methodology before research finishes. Commerce example: Snap-on / Grainger / Schneider-class interviews; tools/problems like Feedonomics.
  2. POV + methodology deck — they even split methodology (Discoverability OS) for ecommerce vs everyone else so sales doesn’t pitch the wrong story.
  3. Commercial kit: overall deck, channel decks, SOWs, proposal pages.
  4. North Star Metric tracker — if you take money, you must show whether it works.
  5. Website: service pages, methodology pages, sub-brand guidelines.
  6. Content pack: listicle + guide(s) + ~5 thought-leadership pieces (same family as Video 01’s 8-piece system).
  7. Tech + training + onboarding.
  8. People triggers: not a middle manager on day one. Example: after ~5 strategists/accounts. Too many resources too early shortens the honest success timeline. Script when they ask for help: “Are you willing to hit the goal 3 months faster? That’s the trade.”
  9. He claimed a new department can roll in about 30 days when this kit exists — CEO gives what/why macros; leaders fill how so they own it.

5. What you may offer

  • RFP rule: only offer it if you could win the best account’s RFP in that category.
  • Never cross-sell-only services — you’ll run out of attach accounts and never get real gross profit.
  • No-brainer: SEO shop should add paid media (he did around $1.5–3M).
  • Refuse scatter (“client wants email and we’re not experts”).
  • MVD (minimum viable department): some things stay small P&L but are required (revops to connect CRM/spend). Video as creative inside paid, not a fake video agency.

6. Leadership / retention side notes

  • Managers offset churn with upsell/cross-sell/referral/return POC; NRR bonuses at scale.
  • But service attach has diminishing returns — hence new divisions still matter, carefully.
  • SOPs: “moments that matter” in first 90 days; raise the floor without lowering the ceiling.

What Brandastic should do with this

  1. Export every service we sell → columns: win top RFP? / only cross-sell? / keep · MVD · kill.
  2. Fix no-brainers (SEO ↔ paid honesty).
  3. Pick zero or one incubation this half — only after vertical/ICP lock from Video 01.
  4. If one: run the full launch kit with a named owner.
  5. Justin = what/why. Owner = how.
  6. Default: do not buy an agency for a capability.
