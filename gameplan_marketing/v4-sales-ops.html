Video 04 / Sales + measurement
VIDEO 04

Sales process + measurement — full detail

Ads without sales discipline waste capital. This is the codependency stack with tools and numbers.

1. Tools he named

  • HubSpot — marketing
  • Salesforce — sales
  • Gong — manage AEs / see activity truth fast (“managing AEs without it is pouring salt in your eyeballs”)

2. Why sales process is mandatory

  • He is blunt: you must be strong in sales process.
  • Spreadsheet + no CRM / no stage discipline → capital underperforms once it is not only the founder selling.
  • Even founder-only is better with a system.
  • He showed live hygiene: intros, strategy calls, proposals, SOWs, legal — activity visible, next steps scheduled, stale days visible.

3. Financial model (proposal held → close)

  • Win rates from proposal held (pitch given), not only contract signed.
  • Band:
    • Under ~20% → positioning/pricing problem
    • Over ~40% → often underpricing
    • Directive aims roughly 30–40%
  • Put win rates into the model before media spend plans.

4. Ad measurement

  • Set up CAPI on LinkedIn and Meta.
  • Optimize to influence revenue, not last-click / first-touch vanity.
  • Best Meta CTR maybe ~4–6% → ~94% of exposed people never click → last-click will make you kill good spend.
  • Report revenue ads influenced, not only directly drove.

5. Brandastic tickets

  1. CRM stages real and reported weekly.
  2. Proposal-held win-rate dashboard (20–40% band).
  3. Call recording/review habit (Gong-class).
  4. CAPI on Meta + LinkedIn before scaling.
  5. Monthly influence pipeline + revenue to Justin — not only CPL.
