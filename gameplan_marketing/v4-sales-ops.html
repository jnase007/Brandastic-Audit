Sales process + measurement — full detail

Tools

  • HubSpot marketing
  • Salesforce sales
  • Gong AE management (“without it is pouring salt in your eyeballs”)

Process

  • Stage discipline required.
  • Spreadsheet + no CRM hygiene → capital underperforms once not only founder selling.
  • Live hygiene: intros, strats, proposals, SOWs, legal visible; next steps scheduled.

Financial model

  • From proposal held → close (pitch given).
  • Under ~20% → positioning/pricing problem.
  • Over ~40% → often underpricing.
  • Directive aims ~30–40%.

Ad measurement

  • CAPI on LinkedIn + Meta.
  • KPI: influence revenue, not last-click.
  • Meta CTR ceiling ~4–6% → ~94% never click → last-click lies.

Brandastic tickets

  1. CRM stages weekly.
  2. Proposal-held win-rate dashboard.
  3. Call review habit.
  4. CAPI before scale.
  5. Monthly influence pipeline/revenue to Justin.
