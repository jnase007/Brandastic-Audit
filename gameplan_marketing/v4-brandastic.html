Brandastic application — advertise the agency

Execute in order.

  1. ICP one-pager: VP/Dir Marketing default; exclusions (no practitioner-threat targeting).
  2. TAM build + verify (week-scale).
  3. Primer (or equivalent): upload/match; A/B list vs native.
  4. CRM stages + proposal-held win rate (20–40%) + call visibility.
  5. One offer path: Meta monthly webinar with delivery leads OR show-up gift incentive.
  6. Do not lead with free-audit retarget.
  7. Creative: change-agent buyer; division/outcome language.
  8. CAPI on; monthly influence pipeline + revenue to Justin.
  9. New-biz ad cap separate from client media (Video 01).
  10. Only spend on TAM slices with real CAC room.
Win = discipline (list, offer, sales, influence KPI) — not copying $270K/mo LinkedIn.
