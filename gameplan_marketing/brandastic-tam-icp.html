SoCal breakdown

  • Charter schools: ~300–400 (LA metro = 154, Cause IQ)
  • Private K-12: ~2,500–3,500 (LA County alone ~1,600+)
  • Colleges: 100+ (NCES/IPEDS)
  • Online programs (CA-based): ~50–80

Why education is easiest

  • CalOPS at $21K/month is real proof
  • Every school needs enrollment marketing
  • Clear pain: families have to find the school online
  • National reach — not limited to SoCal
  • Higher ed digital spend: $2.77B in 2025 (Simpson Scarborough)

The honest problem

  • Abutment manufacturers = only ~35 companies in the whole US
  • Big four (Nobel Biocare, Dentsply Sirona, Straumann, Zimmer Biomet) have in-house teams
  • Our niche (Preat, Endes USA type companies) = maybe 10–15 potential clients total
  • Surgical marketing is complex — compliance, regulation, long sales cycles
  • We have no medical device clients currently

Where scale exists (if we expand)

  • Dental labs (4,200–9,300) — much bigger pool
  • Supply distributors (473+) — need SEO to reach dentists
  • Current clients (Salvin $4.9K, Paragon $3.5K, Young Surgical $4.9K, Dental Smart $7.8K) prove the offering
  • But this would be a different sell than abutment manufacturers

Our lane

  • Tier 2 and Tier 3 suppliers (10–200 employees, owner-led)
  • AS9100 / ITAR certified shops
  • Current clients: Morehouse ($6.3K), Wearparts ($3.9K), LabTech ($6.1K)
  • SoCal advantage — we are local, can visit shops

The challenge

  • Relationship-driven — buyers know their suppliers
  • Many owners don’t believe in marketing
  • Budgets are small: $20K–$80K/year
  • Long sales cycle — certs matter more than websites
  • No public data on exact small supplier count
