Education · the full picture

TAM · targets · channels · recommendation

QuestionAnswer
How big?44,000+ schools in the US. Charter (~7,500), private K-12 (~29,730), colleges (~5,999), online/virtual (~700+). Total education marketing spend: $4.7B+/year. Higher ed digital alone: $2.77B. SoCal: ~3,000–4,000 schools. Sources: NCES, IPEDS, EAB, Simpson Scarborough.
What we'd focus onOnline and hybrid charter schools that depend on search for enrollment. Private K-12 with real tuition revenue ($50K–$500K marketing budgets). Small-to-mid colleges that can't afford a big agency. Start with CalOPS peers.
If we got 10% of the marketNationwide: ~700–1,400 addressable × $7K avg × 12 = $5.9M–$11.8M/year.
SoCal only: ~200–400 × $7K × 12 = $504K–$1.3M/year.
Even 1% nationwide = $3.4M+.
Apollo targetingTitles: Director of Enrollment, VP Marketing, Director of Marketing, Head of School, Superintendent, Dean of Admissions.
Filters: Industry = Education. Sub-type = charter, private, online. Size = 50–2,000 employees.
Where to find themLinkedIn outbound · Direct email with CalOPS proof · SEO for "enrollment marketing" · Education conferences (NACAC, charter summits) · CalOPS referral to peer schools.
Proof we haveCalOPS at $21,000/month. Live case.
Recommendation: Best growth bet. Biggest market, best proof, highest revenue, national reach.

QuestionAnswer
How big?Abutment/implant manufacturers: only ~35 in the US. Dental labs: 4,216–9,341. Supply distributors: 473+. Sources: iData Research, IBISWorld, KokoQuest.
What we'd focus onToday: Preat, Endes USA type companies. To scale: expand into dental labs and distributors. NOT general dentist offices.
If we got 10% of the marketAbutments only: 1–2 clients = $48K–$168K (too small). Expanded to labs + distributors: $4.6M–$8.2M.
Apollo targetingTitles: Owner, President, GM, VP Sales, Marketing Manager.
Filters: Industry = Medical Devices, Dental. Size = 10–200 employees. NAICS = 339114, 339116.
Where to find themTrade shows (Chicago Dental, ADA) · LinkedIn · Direct email with Salvin/Young Surgical proof · Industry directories.
Proof we haveSalvin ($4.9K), Paragon ($3.5K), Young Surgical ($4.9K), Dental Smart ($7.8K).
Recommendation: Keep current clients. Do NOT build growth around abutment mfrs alone — only 35 exist. Explore labs and distributors for scale.
