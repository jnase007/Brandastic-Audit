Brandastic TAM & ICP — strategy deep enough to build from

Directive didn’t invent ads first. They spent about a year curating ~20,000 → ~10,000 ideal accounts around a sharp ICP. This page is our version: locked verticals, titles, sizes, list sources, campaign motion, and a 90-day build plan.

LOCKED by Justin (2026-10-09)
Primary: Dental / surgical / medical B2B (labs, supply, specialty med product) — US national.
Secondary: Education — CalOps = California public schools — separate TAM & campaigns, never mixed into dental lists.
Corrections: CalOps ≠ industrial; Morehouse ≈ food; Adopt a Highway ≠ nonprofit.

1. Why this is the strategy (not a slogan)

  • Your book already proves dental/med/surgical B2B buys multi-service SEO+PPC retainers (Prudental, Salvin, Young Surgical, DESS, Labtech).
  • CalOps proves education can be your largest account — but the buyer, procurement, and message are different. Treat it as lane 2.
  • Directive-style paid (Primer lists, $105 gifts, LinkedIn, webinars) only works when the list is ICP-true. A mixed OC “any business” list will burn cash.
  • Food/retail/RE clients stay in the book for delivery; they are not the primary paid new-biz engine.

4. How we build the primary TAM (step-by-step)

  1. Seed lookalikes — for each of Prudental, Salvin, Young Surgical, DESS, Labtech, Wearparts: 40–80 peer companies (competitors, similar size suppliers, association members).
  2. Directories / associations — NADL and state lab associations; dental/surgical supply dealer directories; trade-show exhibitor lists.
  3. Data tools — Apollo / Clay / ZoomInfo / LinkedIn Sales Nav with industry + headcount filters; BuiltWith if tech signal matters.
  4. AI pass — enrich domain, size, industry fit; drop obvious mismatches.
  5. Human verify — website + LinkedIn: do they sell B2B to clinics/dealers? Marketing budget plausible?
  6. Justin 100 random scrolls — ~90/100 must be accounts we’d actually want before spend (Directive method).
  7. Load Primer — list vs native A/B on LinkedIn/Meta.
  8. Fields per account: company, domain, industry tag, employees, revenue band, buyer titles + LI URLs, why-fit one-liner, source, fit score 1–5, exclude reason.

5. Campaign strategy once the list exists

Primary dental/med — full funnel

  1. Relationship first (V01): reopen past dental/med prospects; job-change alerts; white-space on current dental/med clients; light Goody for top accounts.
  2. Content (V01+V03): one full 8-piece node for “dental lab / surgical supply SEO+PPC” before other verticals.
  3. LinkedIn Message Ads (V04): hero titles; problem = dealer demand / quote flow / product visibility / competitor share — not generic leads. Face = delivery lead (Lauren pattern). Offer stack AirPods → Kindle → $105 Amazon after call.
  4. Meta webinars: vertical topics; AEs pitch signups; team presents; AE owns follow-up.
  5. Measure: held meetings + influenced pipeline including gift COGS; CAPI on; not vanity CPL.
  6. Sales OS: proposal-held win rate visible before scaling spend.

Secondary education — protected lane

  1. Map CalOps buyer titles & deal shape with account team.
  2. Build CA LEA list (150–400).
  3. Relationship + SEO/comms delivery first.
  4. Paid only if ACV clears; separate creative and lists forever.

6. What we explicitly will not do

  • OC “any business” paid lists
  • One TAM mixing CalOps + dental + Friar Tux + food
  • Ads to clinicians as the agency buyer
  • Free-audit hero offers
  • Launching five new services before this ICP is staffed with a list
  • Spending gift-card budget before 100-scroll QA passes
