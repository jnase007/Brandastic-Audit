Internal processes

What “internal processes” means

These are not client billable “products.” They are how Brandastic runs so the revenue work stays clean and doesn’t depend on Justin remembering everything.

The three internal pieces from Video 1

  1. Weekly team rhythm — short written update every Thursday: what I finished, why it mattered, what’s next, where I’m stuck.
  2. When we lose a client or miss a deal — we write down the real reason (not a sugarcoated story) and fix one thing.
  3. What else each client might buy — a simple map per big client so we expand after we’re already winning for them.

Why this sits separate from revenue: if we mix “send gifts” with “how we do Thursday updates,” the team gets confused. Money work and operating work need different pages.
