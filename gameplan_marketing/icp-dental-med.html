The real problem

  1. Dealers/clinics search capability + trust; sites say “quality since 1987.”
  2. Work still comes from reps. Site does not win RFQs.
  3. Owners jump to ads before the site converts.
  4. Content is generic healthcare, not supplier demand gen.

What they hack together now

  • Outdated brochure site + PDF catalog
  • Directory listings they forget to update
  • Trade show booth, weak follow-up
  • Rep network only — no digital demand engine

6. How we write to them

How to talk

Point: Their product is already good. The site makes it hard for a dealer or clinic to see that fast.

Ask: Want a plain note on what shows up in 30 seconds on your site?

Tone: Respect the lab. No “leads.” No hype. No “you look outdated.”

Skip these lines

  • “Double your leads in 90 days”
  • Talking like they’re a dentist’s office
  • “Synergy,” “growth engine,” “game-changer”
  • Long pitches before you’ve looked at their site
Email 1 · first note
Hi {{first_name}},

I run Brandastic in Costa Mesa. We work with a few dental and medical suppliers on their websites and search.

I looked at {{company}}. The product story is there — it’s just buried. A dealer or clinic has to dig to see what you make and how to start.

If you want, I’ll send 5 plain notes on what shows up in the first half-minute on the site. No call needed.

Justin

7. 90-day team motion

Days 1–14

Sheet + 500–2K raw lookalikes from proof clients + NADL/directories. Apollo filters locked.

Days 15–30

AI enrich + human verify → 300+ scored. Justin 100-scroll QA before spend.

Days 30–60

Reopen + white-space on current dental/med book. Scorecard live. First 8-piece outline.

Days 60–90

Primer + LinkedIn tests only if QA passes. Webinar or gift offer. Kill if meetings don’t convert.
