The real problem

  1. Dealers/clinics search capability + trust; sites say “quality since 1987.”
  2. Work still comes from reps. Site does not win RFQs.
  3. Owners jump to ads before the site converts.
  4. Content is generic healthcare, not supplier demand gen.

What they hack together now

  • Outdated brochure site + PDF catalog
  • Directory listings they forget to update
  • Trade show booth, weak follow-up
  • Rep network only — no digital demand engine

6. Copy the team can use

Words that work

One sentence: You already make the product. A dealer should see that and know how to start a quote without hunting a PDF.

Headline: Your lab already does the work. Buyers should not have to hunt to see it.

CTA: Send your URL. We’ll tell you what a dealer can find — and what they can’t.

Do not say

  • Patient-clinic language as if they are a dentist office
  • “Double your leads in 90 days” with no proof
  • Aero/prime language on a dental lab
  • “You look like 2009”
Owner / VP email · template
{{first_name}},

I was reviewing {{industry}} suppliers in {{geo}}.

The work at {{company}} is clear. What is harder for a new dealer or clinic is seeing what you make, who it is for, and how to start without hunting a PDF.

We help a short list of dental and medical suppliers with the site, search, and follow-up — not noise.

If useful, I can send a short read on what a buyer can find today. No call required.
