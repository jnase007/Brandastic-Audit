Section A · Geo choice

Local market vs national market

Use this to decide whether to hunt local first, go national, or run a hybrid.

Local market

California / SoCal focus (optional first wedge)

$Dense supplier/lab clusters in CA
List~150–400 CA accounts
ProsCloser travel, denser networking
ConsSmaller pond than national
  • Good if you want door-knock / event density near OC
  • Still enough labs/suppliers for a serious local list
  • Risk: miss national dealer-network buyers your proof clients already resemble

National market

United States (recommended default for this ICP)

$5–7B+Dental lab market context
8–12K+US commercial labs
List500–1,500 verified v1
ProsMatches proof clients + Directive-style scale
  • Best fit for LinkedIn + content + list machine
  • Proof clients already look national/supplier, not OC-only clinics
  • Optional boost states: CA, FL, TX, NY, PA, IL, OH, etc.
Recommendation: NATIONAL primary for Dental/Med/Surgical. Optional CA density boost inside the national list — not CA-only as the whole strategy.
