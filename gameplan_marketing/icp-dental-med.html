ICP 1 · Primary · locked

National dental, surgical & medical suppliers.

This is Brandastic’s primary ICP. Labs and suppliers that sell to clinics and dealers — not patient clinics as the agency buyer. Same five-section depth as the aero GTM, pointed at the book you already win.

I help marketing leaders and owners at US dental and medical suppliers get found by the clinics and dealers that should already be buying from them.

1. Researcher

The industry is large. Our market is labs and specialty suppliers that can pay retainers — not “all of healthcare.”

US dental labs
8–12K+

Commercial labs, order of magnitude. Pond is real without boiling ocean.

Lab market $
$5–7B+

US dental lab market planning range (re-verify for external decks).

Our v1 TAM
500–1,500

Verified accounts from a 2–5K start list.

Who can pay
$4.5–15K

Monthly SEO+PPC path. Sweet spot ~$5–75M cos / 20–300 people.

Proof already in the book

  • Prudental Labs
  • Salvin
  • Young Surgical
  • DESS USA
  • Labtech
  • Wearparts (industrial cousin)

The real problem

  1. Dealers and clinics search for capability + trust. Sites say “quality since 1987.”
  2. Work still comes from reps and relationships. The site does not win RFQs.
  3. Owners think marketing means ads before the site can convert.
  4. Content is generic healthcare, not supplier demand gen.

Titles

Primary: VP Marketing · Director of Marketing · Head of Marketing · Owner/Founder/President · CMO

Secondary: Demand gen / growth / performance marketing · eCom · sales+marketing hybrid

Not hero: dentists/clinicians, clinic office managers, in-house SEO specialists

Smallest test

  1. Seed 100 lookalikes of the five proof clients
  2. Justin 100-scroll QA on a 300-row list
  3. 10 reopen/white-space conversations inside current dental/med book
  4. If 3+ will expand or refer, keep list build

Offer

  • Open multi-channel retainers $4.5–15K (SEO+PPC+web)
  • Paid ads/gifts only after site converts and list QA passes
  • Offers stack: AirPods → Kindle → $105 Amazon after show-up (V04)

3. Copywriter

Respect the lab. Talk about dealers and clinics. Never “you look like 2009.”

Words that work

One sentence: You already make the product. A dealer should see that and know how to start a quote without hunting a PDF.

Headline: Your lab already does the work. Buyers should not have to hunt to see it.

CTA: Send your URL. We’ll tell you what a dealer can find — and what they can’t.

Do not say

  • Patient-clinic language as if they are a dentist office
  • “Double your leads in 90 days” with no proof
  • Prime/aero language on a dental lab
Owner / VP email
{{first_name}},

I was reviewing {{industry}} suppliers in {{geo}}.

The work at {{company}} is clear. What is harder for a new dealer or clinic is seeing what you make, who it is for, and how to start without hunting a PDF.

We help a short list of dental and medical suppliers with the site, search, and follow-up — not noise.

If useful, I can send a short read on what a buyer can find today. No call required.
