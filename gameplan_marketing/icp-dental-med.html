Dental / implant market size
How big is this market — honestly?

Brandastic's actual niche is dental implant and abutment manufacturers (like Preat and Endes USA), not general dentist offices. This is a small but specialized market.

The honest problem

  • Dental abutment manufacturers = only ~30–50 companies in the whole US
  • The big four (Nobel Biocare, Dentsply Sirona, Straumann, Zimmer Biomet) have in-house marketing teams
  • Smaller players like Preat and Endes USA are the real targets — but there are only a handful
  • Surgical and medical devices have complex compliance requirements that limit marketing approaches
  • We do not have medical device clients currently — only dental abutment adjacent

Where the opportunity actually is

  • Dental labs (4,200–9,300 in US) — much larger addressable pool than manufacturers
  • Dental supply distributors (473+) — need SEO and digital marketing to reach dentists
  • Specialty surgical supply companies — similar motion to abutment manufacturers
  • Current clients (Salvin, Paragon, Young Surgical, Dental Smart) prove the offering works
  • Proof from abutment work transfers to labs and distributors
