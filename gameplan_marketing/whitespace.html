Brandastic GTM / What else to sell clients
How we work inside (feeds revenue later)

What else each client might buy

For our top clients, keep a living one-page map of what they buy now and what they might buy after we are already winning for them.

Why this is an internal process

It is how account managers work accounts (a sheet and a monthly review). The money shows up later as expansion. Rule: hit their goals first, then ask for more work.

Sheet columns

  • Client name
  • What they buy now
  • What they wanted but cut during sales
  • What we might offer in 90 days
  • Proof we need first (example: two months of hitting lead goal)
  • Blocker
  • Next conversation date

Stacy + Maxwell fill top 15. Justin reviews monthly for 45 minutes.

Step-by-step

Create the sheet this week

Name it White Space Top 15.

Fill from what AMs already know

No perfect research project. Honest first pass.

Put monthly review on Justin’s calendar

Same time each month.

This is a working handbook. Each page should be clear enough that someone can do the job without a meeting.
