What else each client might buy

For our top clients, keep a simple one-page map of what they buy now, what they wanted but didn’t buy yet, and what we might offer after we’re already winning.

Why this is “internal”

It’s an account manager habit — a spreadsheet/process. The money shows up later as expansion. We keep it under internal so AMs know it’s part of how we run accounts.

Columns on the map

  • What they buy now
  • What they wanted in the sales process but cut
  • What we might offer in the next 90 days
  • What proof we need first (example: hit their lead goal two months in a row)
  • What’s blocking it
  • When we talk next

Hard rule: hit their goals before asking for more money.

Owners: Stacy and Maxwell for top 15. Justin reviews monthly.

First things to do

  1. Make the sheet for top 15 clients.
  2. Fill it this week from what AMs already know.
  3. Put a 45-minute monthly review on Justin’s calendar.
