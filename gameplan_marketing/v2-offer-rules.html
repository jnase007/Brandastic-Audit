Video 02 / Offer rules
VIDEO 02

Personas, ICP boundaries, RFP rules — full detail

1. Personas they sell to

  1. VP of Marketing
  2. Director of Marketing
  • They do not easily reach CRO / VP of Sales — weak relationships, hard new POCs.
  • Therefore: do not offer services outside marketing-leader KPIs as primary new-biz motions.
  • Example failure: sales-ops as a lead motion into sales leaders when your relationships are marketing buyers.
  • Expand services inside archetype KPIs — that is how the engine stays coherent while expanding.

2. RFP rule (non-negotiable)

  • Only offer a service if you could win the best account’s RFP in that category.
  • If not → do not offer it.
  • Historical miss: offered video; got destroyed whenever it was a real video pitch.
  • Never offer cross-sell-only services — attach inventory runs out faster than you think; no lasting gross profit; agency becomes broad and unfocused.

3. No-brainer expansion path

  • Started as SEO shop.
  • Launched paid media around $1.5M–$3M revenue.
  • Refused ad-hoc “client wants email and we’re not experts.”
  • Refuse the overloaded “does everything” buyer who wants a pile of random tactics.

4. MVD — Minimum Viable Department

  • Some capabilities never become huge P&Ls but are required.
  • Example: RevOps — may stall around smaller P&L (~$3M thinking) but required to connect CRM/Salesforce to run serious spend.
  • Video: keep as creative mix inside paid (MVD), not fake standalone video agency.
  • Fund the minimum. Don’t pretend it’s a $10M division overnight.

5. NRR / comps (ties to offer expansion)

  • Path past ~$20M in their peer set: net revenue retention.
  • Managers/leaders/strategists: bonus for offsetting churn via upsell, cross-sell, referral, return POC.
  • 100% NRR month → bonus ÷ 12, accrues monthly.
  • Warning: service addressable market has diminishing returns — new divisions still needed carefully (this whole video).

6. Brandastic tickets

  1. Write ICP with primary titles + exclusions.
  2. Service audit: Win best RFP? Cross-sell only? Keep/MVD/Kill.
  3. List services outside buyer KPIs and cut or MVD them.
  4. Document no-brainer pairs (SEO+paid) and forbidden ad-hoc work.
