What services to offer

Their hard filters — this is how you expand without becoming unfocused and killing margin.

Rules they stated

  1. RFP rule: Only offer a service if you could win the best account RFP in that category. If not, don’t offer it.
  2. No cross-sell-only services: If you can only attach it to existing clients and couldn’t win new business on it alone, it won’t have real gross profit — you’ll run out of attachable accounts.
  3. No-brainer pair: If you offer SEO, offer paid media. They started SEO, launched paid around $1.5–3M revenue.
  4. Refuse scatter: Don’t say yes to random “we need email too” from weak buyers when you’re not expert — that was their small-agency trap.
  5. MVD (minimum viable department): Some capabilities stay small P&L but are required (example: revops to connect CRM/Salesforce so you can run serious spend). Fund the minimum, don’t pretend it’s a $10M division overnight.
  6. Video example: Still offer video as creative mix inside paid — not as a fake standalone video agency they couldn’t win RFPs for.
Plain English: Don’t add a service just because one client asked. Add it when you can beat real competitors for that work — or when it’s a minimum piece of infrastructure you need to deliver core work.
