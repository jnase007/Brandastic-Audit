Ads as support — not the whole plan

We still run ads. We’re good at ads for clients, and ads can help Brandastic get meetings too. But the lesson from the friend agency is: if growth only comes from buying more ads, you can end up on a treadmill.

What this means for us

  • Keep doing excellent paid media for clients (Sam’s lane).
  • For Brandastic’s own new-business ads, set a monthly cap.
  • Use ads to support people who already touched us (came to an event, read a hub page, got a proposal before).
  • Every month, compare: how many meetings came from ads vs events/referrals/triggers/website.

First things to do

  1. Separate reporting: “Client ad accounts” vs “Brandastic new-business ads.”
  2. Justin sets the monthly new-business ad budget number.
  3. Sam builds audiences from event RSVPs, website readers, past proposals.
  4. Weekly: kill weak ads; keep hooks short and clear.

You’re done when

Budgets are split, cap is set, and we’ve done one monthly review of where meetings actually came from.
