Information architecture + TAM spend map — full detail

IA rules

  1. SEO/content IA starts with services and subservices.
  2. Each page type has a defined use case.
  3. Vertical/fit pages: design to rank if possible; primary job is cognitive fit — “oh shit, these guys do exactly what I need.”
  4. Not endless thin industry pages as the spine.
  5. B2B macros covering entire TAM:
    1. Technology
    2. Industrial
    3. Services
  6. Industries can exist hidden deeper — don’t run industry spam as primary ad/content spine.
  7. IA and GTM must be one narrative.

Spend map example

  • $50K total example threaded by: division × TAM bucket × funnel stage × channel × audience × dollars (e.g. $40K + $10K).
  • Do not put advertising/content capital into Services TAM (CAC headroom too low).
  • Services TAM handled with email via Instantly VIP.
  • Put capital into Industrial + Technology.
  • Don’t over-invest content/ads into Performance if market already knows you for performance — push new divisions (Commerce, Communications).
  • Parent brand colors (purple/black) on home/global; each division gets own color + sub-brand on its pages.

Brandastic tickets

  1. Redraw site IA from services/subservices.
  2. Pick macros/verticals and which get paid/content capital vs email-only.
  3. Division or service-line color/sub-brand rules if we run multiple offers publicly.
  4. Align ad + content calendar to the same map (one narrative).
