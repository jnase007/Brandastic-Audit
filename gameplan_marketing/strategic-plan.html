Our plan · not their company copy-paste

Brandastic strategic plan from Videos 1–4

They are ~$27–30M and B2B-deep. We are ~$2.3M and still scattered on who we sell to. This plan is what we should do with their system — in the right order — so it fits Brandastic.

The point of the masterclass for us: not to become Directive in 90 days. It is to stop random marketing, lock who we serve, build a bookings machine we can run with a small team (and AI), and only then spend money on ads and new offers.

Who we are (planning assumptions)

  • ~$2.3M agency, not $27M
  • Services are wide (SEM, social, web, creative, photo/video, etc.) but ICP/verticals are not locked
  • We are not cleanly “B2B only” in how the market sees us yet
  • Team is lean; we cannot copy $270K/mo LinkedIn or $65K content sprints casually
  • We can copy their structure: personas, 8-piece content, launch discipline, TAM+offer, sales hygiene
  • Tinny/AI can draft a lot of the content machine; humans publish and own relationships

The one strategic sequence (do not shuffle)

  1. Lock who we sell to (from V02 personas + V01 verticals gap + V04 targeting)
  2. Clean what we sell (V02 RFP rule — kill scatter offers)
  3. Relationship bookings engine (V01 — highest near-term revenue at our size)
  4. Content/SEO machine on locked services × verticals (V01 8-piece + V03 recipes)
  5. Sales process hard enough to take paid (V04)
  6. Advertise the agency only after 1–5 (V04) — small, disciplined
  7. Launch at most one new offer only after the above is breathing (V02 kit)

If we jump to ads or new services first, we waste money. That is the whole recommendation.

Phase 0 — Decisions only Justin can make (TAM/ICP build page) (1 week)

Goal: end scatter.

  1. Primary orientation: B2B / operator brands (default yes — matches their system and our best retainers).
  2. Pick 3 verticals max for the next 12 months (examples to choose from, not all):
    • Ecommerce / product brands
    • Home services / multi-location local
    • Health / dental
    • Education
    • B2B professional / industrial
  3. Buyer titles we sell to: default from the training — VP/Director of Marketing or owner/founder who acts as marketing lead. Write exclusions (who we will not build offers for).
  4. Service keep/kill list using RFP rule: only keep what we could win a competitive pitch for; MVD the rest; kill cross-sell-only junk.

Done when: one page signed off — Orientation · 3 verticals · Buyer titles · Keep/MVD/Kill services.

Phase 1 — Bookings from relationships (next 30–60 days) · Video 01 money

Why first: At $2.3M, their ~$7M referral/return-POC motion is the highest-ROI idea — scaled down. Faster than SEO.

  1. Return POC / reopen list — past proposals, past clients, engaged contacts. Weekly outreach (human, not spam).
  2. Job-change + trigger alerts — simple stack (even manual LinkedIn + CRM notes at first).
  3. Goody / gifting — small monthly budget (~$750–$1.5K), not unlimited; for clients + high-propensity returns.
  4. Client nights — Costa Mesa scale, not $8.5K Directive nights. Start quarterly or every other month; 50/50 client/prospect; experiential if possible.
  5. White-space on top 15 clients — expand map before new logos only.
  6. Thursday rhythm — short public updates: pipeline, proposals, bookings, losses. Names on the board.

Success metric (12 months, serious execution): roughly +$250K–$500K from referral/return/expand motions combined with the rest of the plan; overall serious case still about +15–25% if we execute (see hub revenue block).

Phase 2 — Content machine on what we kept (days 30–120) · Videos 01 + 03

Why second: compounds; slow in year one; worthless if verticals still fuzzy.

  1. IA: services/subservices first on brandastic.com.
  2. For each priority service × locked vertical: their 8-pack (ultimate guide, listicle, hiring guide, 5 thought leadership).
  3. Listicle craft from V03: sidebar+body anchors, commercial intent block, TL;DR, AI draft + human edit.
  4. Wave 1 only: e.g. SEM, Social, Shopify/Web, Photo/Video, Branding — finish one node before boiling the ocean.
  5. Commercial-query market share: own lists + directory/Clutch-style bets only when AI overview cites them and ROI is obvious.
  6. Weekly SEO/content meeting (or it slides).
  7. Production: Tinny/AI drafts; human publishes; no $400–500/piece writers required unless we want them.

Success metric: one full service node live with all 8; rankings movement on a short commercial keyword list within 90–180 days; year-1 revenue from SEO modest, year-2 more.

Phase 3 — Sales OS hard enough for paid (parallel with Phase 1–2) · Video 04

Why before big ads: they said capital dies without process.

  1. CRM stages real (proposal held visible).
  2. Proposal-held win rate tracked (aim band ~20–40%; diagnose under/over).
  3. Call visibility / review habit (Gong-class discipline; tool can differ).
  4. Intro calls expand to outcome, not only the service they asked for.

Done when: Justin can see pipeline hygiene in minutes, not a scavenger hunt.

Phase 4 — Advertise the agency (only after Phase 0–3) · Video 04

Small, disciplined, Brandastic-sized.

  1. Build verified account TAM for locked verticals (not a scraped email dump).
  2. Load with Primer (or equivalent); test list vs native.
  3. One offer path only:
    • Meta monthly webinar with delivery leads on camera, OR
    • Show-up incentive (gift only if they attend)
  4. Message VP/owner change agents — never craft-threat ads to practitioners.
  5. Advertise division/outcome, not “we do everything.”
  6. CAPI on; report influence pipeline/revenue monthly.
  7. Do not lead with free-audit retarget.
  8. Cap new-biz ads; keep separate from client media. Scale only after TAM+offer+sales prove out.

Budget posture: test monthly (think low thousands to start, not $50K), kill or iterate on held meetings + influenced pipeline — not vanity CPL.

Phase 5 — Launch at most one new offer (optional, later) · Video 02

Only if Phases 0–2 are real.

  1. Must pass RFP rule and fit locked buyers/verticals.
  2. Run full 30-day kit: research → methodology deck → pages → SOW → NSM → content pack → hire trigger.
  3. No manager day one; protect from expectation bloat.
  4. Default still: build, don’t buy a small agency for a capability.

What we should not do

  • Copy $270K/mo LinkedIn or $8.5K events on day one
  • Launch five new services because a client asked
  • Spray 8-piece content across every service with no vertical
  • Buy an agency to “add a capability” cheaply
  • Run free-audit retarget as the main offer
  • Advertise to threatened practitioners
  • Skip sales hygiene and “just turn ads on”
  • Treat the playbooks as reading homework instead of tickets with owners

Owners (suggested)

  • Justin: Phase 0 decisions; Thursday bookings review; proximity on strategy
  • Growth lead / CJ: content 8-packs, site IA, weekly SEO meeting
  • AMs: white-space, gifts, reopen touches, NRR behaviors
  • Sales: stages, proposal hygiene, intro outcome expansion
  • Tinny/AI: draft content packs, TAM sheets, page structures, checklists
Bottom line: Videos 1–4 become one Brandastic plan — lock who we are → book from relationships → content on that lane → sales OS → small paid → maybe one launch. That is how a $2.3M shop uses a $30M playbook without going broke or getting more scattered.
