How this roadmap works

One masterclass video = one labeled section in the left nav. We process videos one at a time. This is only Video 01 filled so far.

Rules

  1. Label by training topic — e.g. Video 01 is “Bookings / GTM engine,” not a random pile of tips.
  2. Only use what they said in that video for that section.
  3. Turn it into Brandastic runbooks — who, steps, done-when.
  4. Split revenue drivers vs internal processes inside each video when needed.
  5. Next video gets a new section (Video 02, 03, 04) — we do not dump everything into Video 01.

For the team

Open the video section you’re executing. Follow that runbook. Thursday updates report progress by video/play.

For AI agents

Scope work to one video ID (V1, V2…). Do not mix instructions across videos. Prefer pages under that video’s nav group.
