How this handbook works

Every important page is a runbook: what it is, why we care, who does what, exact steps, and what “done” looks like. Not a brainstorm list.

When you open a page, look for these blocks

  • What this is — plain English definition
  • Why we are doing it — the business reason
  • Who does what — names
  • Step-by-step — numbered work with owners
  • Copy you can reuse — emails, Slack messages
  • Done looks like this — so nobody fakes progress

Revenue vs internal

Revenue drivers should create meetings, proposals, expansion, or inbound leads.

Internal processes do not invoice a client by themselves. They stop chaos so the revenue work keeps moving.

This is a working handbook. Each page should be clear enough that someone can do the job without a meeting.
