6) Questions to ask in the room

Scoreboard

  • What is our real recurring client count today?
  • What is net new clients 2026 (won minus lost)?
  • What revenue sits in the top 3 clients?
  • What cash floor do we need every month?

Wins and losses

  • Which logos did we win in 2026, and how did they find us?
  • Which logos did we lose, and why in one sentence?
  • What would have prevented each loss?
  • Which referrals can we repeat on purpose?

ICP and offers

  • If we only serve two ICPs in 2027, which two?
  • What client should we not take next year?
  • What is our minimum monthly retainer?

Marketing

  • Where did 80% of pipeline come from?
  • What will we stop doing in marketing?
  • What one campaign will we run for 90 days with an owner and a number?

Team

  • Where are we over capacity (hours / quality / response)?
  • What is the next hire or contractor — success in 90 days?
  • Who owns new business vs delivery?

Close

  • What 3 numbers do we review every month?
  • What 3 projects for Q1 only — owner and date?
  • When is the 90-day check-in?
