Quick math (for the room)

Today: ~$120K/mo recurring ÷ ~22 companies ≈ ~$5,500/mo average per client (if you count packages that way). With ~17 “core” recurring marketing clients it looks closer to ~$7K only if the book is that concentrated — use the real list, don’t force one average.

To grow revenue from ~$2.12M toward $2.5M you need about +$380K/year (~+$32K/mo). At today’s ~$5.5K average that is roughly +6 net clients (or fewer if average ticket rises).

17 → 46 clients at $5.5K would be ~$3.0M/year — that is a big jump (more like the longer path to $3M+, not a small step from $2.12M to $2.5M).

$7.5K/mo average is a great hope. Possible with mix shift (more CalOps-size work, less tiny retainers) — not guaranteed. Safer plan: grow count a little and average a little, not only one lever.
