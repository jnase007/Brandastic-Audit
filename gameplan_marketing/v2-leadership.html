Leadership while launching

  • Protect people from expectations: giving resources too early shortens the success timeline you can honestly give them.
  • Resource ask trade: “I hear you — are you willing to hit the goal 3 months faster?” Make the exchange visible.
  • Ownership: leaders must help shape the plan or they won’t uphold it for a year.
  • SOPs / moments that matter: in first 90 days of engagement, define moments that matter; team builds SOPs. Raise the floor without lowering the ceiling (don’t overscope so greatness dies).
  • Churn math at scale: they bonus managers on replacing lost revenue via upsell/cross-sell/referral/return POC; 100% NRR month bonus. But service addressable market has diminishing returns — which is why new divisions/services still matter carefully.
  • CS structure: multi-service clients over ~$20K → account director instead of three separate strategist meeting stacks (churn/ inefficiency fix).
