Video 02 / Leadership
VIDEO 02

Leadership, NRR, comps — full detail

How they run people and retention so new service launches don’t die to churn.

1. Protect people from false expectations

  • Giving headcount/tools too early shortens the honest runway you can give someone to succeed.
  • Best way to love someone: protect them from expectations.
  • When they ask for another resource: “I hear you. I don’t disagree. Are you willing to hit the goal 3 months faster? That’s the real exchange.”
  • If they say no → wait. Make the trade visible. Leaders are not mind readers about your economics.

2. Emotional ownership of the plan

  • CEO gives macro what + why (maybe ~80% of a high-level checklist).
  • Does not fill every sub-bullet.
  • Leaders must shape the how or they won’t have the spine to run it for 12 months.
  • If they don’t feel it’s theirs, rollout fails — too complicated to force top-down.

3. Moments that matter / SOPs

  • As COO work: first 90 days of client engagement — define moments that matter.
  • Team builds SOPs for those moments; leader forces codification, does not write every SOP personally.
  • Belief: if every engagement gets through pilot cleanly, churn becomes nearly impossible → path toward very high retention.
  • Raise the floor without lowering the ceiling — overscoping SOPs kills greatness.

4. NRR engine (path past ~$20M)

  • Hardest honest agency problem: sustaining success.
  • Peer pattern (Power, Tenility, PMG style shops): scale past ~$20M through net revenue retention.
  • Every middle manager / leader / strategist: base comp + bonus for offsetting churn via:
    • upsell
    • cross-sell
    • referral
    • return point of contact
  • 100% NRR in a month → bonus, divided by 12, accrues monthly.
  • Cannot outrun churn with new biz alone at scale.
  • Warning: service addressable market (SAM) has massive diminishing returns — cannot infinitely attach 5 services into the same 100 accounts.
  • TAM almost never truly saturated; SAM is the real constraint → careful new divisions still needed.

5. Account structure reminder

  • Multi-service >~$20K → Account Director (pay ~$151–175K, OTE <$200K).
  • Big pitches: AD paired with AE.

6. Brandastic tickets

  1. Write the resource-ask trade script into manager culture.
  2. For any launch: Justin = what/why; owner = how (must co-build).
  3. Define first-90-day “moments that matter” for clients; AMs document SOPs as they go.
  4. Pilot NRR behaviors for AMs (upsell/referral/return POC) before fancy bonus math.
  5. Do not launch 5 new services at once — SAM constraint is real at our size too.
