  • Principal as default hero
  • Intern / substitute

6. Copy the team can use

Words that work

One sentence: Parents and communities should find the district’s story without hunting.

CTA: If useful, we can show what families see on your site today — short, no pitch deck.

Do not say

  • B2B pipeline / RFQ / dealer language
  • Aero or dental supplier language
  • Hype enrollment promises without proof
Comms / PIO email · template
{{first_name}},

I work with California education teams on how families find the district online.

At {{district}}, the work in the community is clear. What is often harder is making that story easy to find and trust on the site.
