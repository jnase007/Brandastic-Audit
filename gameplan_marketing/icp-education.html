ICP 2 · Secondary

Online public schools (CalOPS niche)

The simple idea

We already win with CalOPS — California Online Public Schools. They need families to find them online and inquire. We run SEO, paid, social, and site work against that job.

The niche is other schools with the same job: full-time or heavy-online public/charter schools that compete for enrollment on the internet. Same services. Same motion. Live proof.

It is not: universities, and it is not cold-emailing every traditional school district in California. Districts are a different sale (procurement, boards, slow). CalOPS is an enrollment brand.

Why this can scale: most states have online public options — state virtual schools, online charters, multi-state networks. Year 1 = California peers. Later = cherry-pick states. Prefer operators that can support a real retainer (aim ~$5M+ scale when we can score it).

7. 90-day plan

Days 1–14

Confirm CalOPS white-space/referrals. Build CA peer list (online/virtual only). No district dump.

Days 15–45

Clean to 40–120. Send 15–40 human emails using the site-review offer. Track replies.

Days 45–90

2–4 real conversations. One case narrative for enrollment marketing. Decide if niche deserves more budget.

Kill rule

If only tire-kickers reply and nobody will pay a retainer, keep CalOPS as delivery and pause outbound.

Wrong path: blast CA district PIOs. Vague “notes about your site.” University hunting.
Right path: CalOPS-shaped online schools. Clear site-review offer. Retainer conversation only after value is obvious.

SoCal education market at a glance

Brandastic sweet spot

  • Online and hybrid charter schools that depend on search for enrollment
  • Private K-12 with tuition revenue and real marketing budgets ($50K–$500K/yr)
  • Small to mid-size colleges that cannot afford a big agency but need enrollment marketing

CalOPS is our flagship proof. Every similar school is a warm lead.

Where NOT to spend time

  • Large universities with $2M+ internal marketing teams
  • Public school districts with no marketing budget
  • Schools with <$2K/month total marketing capacity
  • Out-of-state schools with no SoCal connection
Addressable for Brandastic (education): Roughly 200–500 schools in SoCal + nationally that are online-heavy, have real enrollment marketing budgets, and would pay $3K–$21K/month for search + paid + site work. CalOPS at $21K/month is the proof that this market pays.
