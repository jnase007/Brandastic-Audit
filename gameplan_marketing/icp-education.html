Online & charter public schools (CalOPS lane)

Not school districts as a cold list. Proof client is CalOPS — California Online Public Schools. The hunt is peer schools that need enrollment marketing (SEO, paid, social, site) — same motion we already run — not PIO cold email to every CA district.

Correction: Mass “education district” outreach does not fit Brandastic. Districts are slow procurement, wrong titles, and not how CalOPS grew with us.
Right lane: online public schools, virtual/hybrid charters, independent-study programs, enrollment-driven schools that look like CalOPS.
We help online and charter public schools get found by families who need them — clear site, search, and ads that drive real enrollment interest.

4. Titles for list & ads

Primary (hero)

  • Head of School / Superintendent (charter/online)
  • Executive Director / CEO (charter network)
  • Director of Enrollment / Student Recruitment
  • Director / VP of Marketing
  • Director of Growth / Outreach

Secondary

  • Director of Communications (if they own enrollment ads)
  • Operations lead who owns growth
  • Board chair only if operator-led and small

Do not target as hero

  • Random district PIOs as a cold list
  • Classroom teachers / principals only
  • County office contacts with no enrollment budget

6. How we write to them

How to talk

Point: Parents searching for online/flexible school options should find you fast and understand if it’s a fit.

Tone: Respect educators. Enrollment is real — don’t sound like a lead farm.

Skip

  • District board / RFP jargon as the opener
  • “We help all California schools”
  • Treating them like a dental supplier or machine shop
Email 1 · peer school
Hi {{first_name}},

I’m Justin with Brandastic in Costa Mesa. We handle SEO, ads, and the site for California Online Public Schools (CalOPS).

I looked at {{school}}. Parents who need a flexible/online option should be able to tell in under a minute if you’re right for them — and how to start.

If you want, I’ll send three plain notes on what shows up today. No call required.

Justin

7. 90-day team motion

Days 1–14

Build lookalike list of CA online/charter/virtual schools (not district dump). Confirm CalOPS white-space.

Days 15–45

20–40 peer schools researched. 8–10 human notes using CalOPS as proof (only if approved to name).

Days 45–90

2–3 serious conversations. Case narrative for enrollment marketing.

Kill rule

If peers won’t buy retainers and only districts reply with RFP theater — keep CalOPS as delivery, don’t force a lane.

Wrong: Apollo blast every CA unified school district PIO.
Right: CalOPS-shaped schools that compete for enrollment and already buy digital.
