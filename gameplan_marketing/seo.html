Brandastic GTM / Website content (SEO)
Revenue driver

Website content that brings leads

We improve brandastic.com so buyers can find us and understand us — then ask for a call. This is not random blogging. It’s a few strong “home base” pages with supporting articles underneath.

What this is (plain English)

Imagine four clear doors on our website:

  1. Paid ads / media help — for companies wasting money on ads
  2. SEO help — for companies that need search growth done properly
  3. Marketing leadership help — for companies that need a senior marketing brain without hiring a full-time CMO
  4. Websites that convert — for companies whose site doesn’t turn visitors into leads

Each door (hub page) gets supporting articles that answer real questions and link back to that door. Every page asks them to talk to us.

Why it matters

The friend agency built lots of content around clear themes and linked it together. That’s how search and sales enablement work long-term. Ads stop when you stop paying. Good pages keep working.

How big we go (we’re $2M, not $30M)

  • About 20–40 pieces per quarter, not hundreds
  • Pay writers or staff side-work about $150–$400 per piece
  • One editor checks quality before publish
  • Owner of structure: CJ. Justin does short point-of-view pieces. Chloe helps share them.

First things to do

  1. List what service pages and blogs we already have.
  2. Write a simple outline for Door 1 (Paid) and Door 2 (SEO).
  3. Publish Door 1 as a strong page with a clear “talk to us” button.
  4. Write 6 helpful articles that link to Door 1.
  5. Track form fills/calls from those pages (not just traffic).

You’re done when (first milestone)

Door 1 is live, six supporting articles are live, links point to the door, and we can see if anyone converts from it.
