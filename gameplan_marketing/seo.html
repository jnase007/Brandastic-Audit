Brandastic GTM / 4. Website that brings leads
Revenue driver

Website content that brings leads

We are going to make brandastic.com clearer for buyers. Not random blogs. A few strong “doors” (hub pages) with helpful articles under each door, each one asking people to talk to us.

What this is

Four doors on the site:

  1. Paid ads help — for companies wasting ad money
  2. SEO help — for companies that need search growth done right
  3. Marketing leadership help — senior marketing brain without a full-time CMO hire
  4. Websites that convert — site is pretty but does not produce leads

Each door gets a main page plus supporting articles that link back to it.

Structure owner: CJ Justin: short POV pieces Chloe: share/posts Writer: drafts

Volume for our size: 20–40 pieces a quarter (not hundreds). Pay about $150–$400 per piece.

Step-by-step for the first door

Inventory what we already have

List current service pages and blogs. Mark what is outdated or thin.

Outline Door 1: Paid ads help

Write the page sections in plain English: who it’s for, problems we solve, how we work, proof, FAQ, big “talk to us” button.

Publish Door 1

Ship the page on brandastic.com. Make sure mobile is clean and the form/call CTA works.

Write 6 supporting articles

Examples: Google vs Meta for leads; when to kill an ad; creative hooks that work; agency vs in-house. Each article links to Door 1 and ends with a CTA.

Measure the right thing

Track form fills and calls from these pages — not only traffic. Name the tracking something simple like “website paid door.”

Done looks like this (first milestone)

Door 1 live, six articles live, links point to the door, CTA tracking works, team can see if anyone converted.

This is a working handbook. Each page should be clear enough that someone can do the job without a meeting.
