Revenue driver

Stay close when people move

A lot of our future revenue is already in our phone: past proposals, old contacts, clients who change companies. This runbook is how we notice and show up.

What this is

A weekly habit (30 minutes) plus fast outreach when something happens — especially job changes. Often pair with a Goody gift.

Why we are doing it

The friend agency booked serious revenue from people they already knew and from “congrats on the new role” moments. That is cheaper and warmer than cold ads.

Owner: Melissa Help: Justin, AMs

Step-by-step

Build the watch list

Export ~100 people: current client contacts + past proposals from the last 18 months. Put them in a sheet with LinkedIn URL and last touch date.

Friday scan

Every Friday, Melissa spends 30 minutes checking for job changes / company news. LinkedIn is fine until we automate.

Send within 48 hours

Short human note. No long pitch.

Hey [Name] — saw the new role at [Company]. Congrats. That’s a big seat. If you ever want a second set of eyes on what marketing is doing there, I’m around. Proud of you either way.

Ask for one intro after a clear win

When we crush a goal for a client, AM or Justin asks for one named intro and gives two forwardable sentences.

Would you intro me to one peer who complains about [problem we just solved]? Forwardable blurb: "Justin’s team at Brandastic just helped us [result]. If useful, he’s good to talk to about [topic]."

Done looks like this

Watch list of 100 exists, Friday habit is on the calendar, two templates saved, at least three real outreach sends logged.

This is a working handbook. Each page should be clear enough that someone can do the job without a meeting.
