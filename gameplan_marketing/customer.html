Revenue driver

Stay in touch and reopen old doors

Many good opportunities are people we already know — old proposals, old contacts, clients who change companies. This page is about noticing those moments and reaching out like a person.

What this is

A simple weekly habit: look for “triggers” (job changes, funding news, launches). Then send a short human note, and often a Goody gift. No long sales email.

Why it matters

The friend agency got a lot of bookings from referrals and people they already knew. Job-change messages (congrats on the new role) often opened “can you look at what my new company is doing?” conversations.

Who owns it

Melissa leads. Justin helps on important names. Account managers flag client contacts.

First things to do

  1. Export a list of about 100 important contacts (clients + past proposals).
  2. Every Friday, spend 30 minutes checking for job changes (LinkedIn is fine to start).
  3. Save two short note templates: (1) new job congrats, (2) “saw your news” value note.
  4. When something happens: note within 48 hours + gift if it fits.
  5. After we clearly win for a client, ask for one introduction by name. Give them two sentences they can forward.

You’re done when

Watch list exists, templates exist, and at least three real trigger outreach sends are logged.
