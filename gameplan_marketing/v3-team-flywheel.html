Marketing team flywheel

  • He still runs marketing as CMO of his own agency — proximity is the superpower.
  • No approval theater; public review of work; public accountability and public praise.
  • Weekly initiative meetings (SEO weekly since it got good March 2025 — stop the meeting and it slides).
  • Develop a lead who shadows so admin isn’t all on the CEO.
  • ~6 FT marketing people + people split on client SEO/paid; goal story for a sale: CAC down, bookings up.
  • If you won’t be in the work, don’t expect the system to win.
