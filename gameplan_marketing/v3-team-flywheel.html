Marketing team OS — pay, meetings, proximity

1. Treat yourself as a client

  • Rough shape: ~6 full-time marketing people spanning content, paid, customer marketing, creative (static + motion), revops/Salesforce hygiene.
  • Some also work on client accounts; the core group runs Directive’s own GTM.
  • He is still the CMO of his own marketing team.

2. Content pay model

  • $500 per article to employees who moonlight after hours (not during client work hours).
  • $350–$500 freelancers for commodity topics.
  • Internal writers for methodology / brand POV uniqueness.
  • Initial boat content sprint ~$65K–$68K.
  • He books himself as a $20K account on the SEO consultant’s revenue-under-management (two $10K / ~20-hour units) so it counts toward their bonus.

3. Cadence and culture

  • No approval theater — ship; review live.
  • Public walkthrough of ads/work in meetings.
  • People hate it at first, then like public accountability and public praise.
  • Weekly SEO meeting since it got good March 2025 — if he stops the meeting, quality slides.
  • Weekly: him + paid buyer + creative strategist + lead.
  • Lead shadows him for future marketing leadership; handles admin/follow-through so he isn’t buried.
  • Everything is a weekly initiative for anything you want to get better at.

4. Proximity / strategy soul

  • Still does about 3 strategy sessions a day personally auditing account strategy.
  • COO, MD, VP in the room — retraining the company on how to do strategy as it scales.
  • “The bigger you get, the easier it is to lose your soul.”
  • Proximity is the CEO/owner superpower. Hire-execs-and-disappear is not real in agencies.
  • If you won’t be involved, don’t expect this system to work.

5. Brandastic tickets

  1. Put weekly SEO/content meeting on the calendar with a named owner (Justin or accountable lead).
  2. Define content pay model (AI+edit and/or per-piece) and when internal vs freelance.
  3. Public review culture for growth work (screenshots, hooks, what shipped) — no long approval chain.
  4. If using contractors for SEO/content, decide how Brandastic “accounts” count toward their goals.
  5. Justin keeps a fixed weekly strategy touch so quality doesn’t slide.
