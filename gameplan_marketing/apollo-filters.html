Exact Apollo filters

Apollo filters for every ICP

Copy these into Apollo as starting searches. Industry tags are noisy — always hand-clean. Export → score → Justin 100-scroll QA before spend.

How to use
1. Run Company search first with filters below.
2. Then open People on that company set with title filters.
3. Exclude junk with negative keywords.
4. Save as a list named ICP1_DentalMed_v1 / ICP2_EducationCA_v1 / ICP3_AeroSoCal_v1.

ICP 1 · PRIMARY Dental, Medical, Surgical B2B

ACompany filters (run first)

# Apollo → Search → Companies Location: · Country: United States · (optional boost) States: CA, FL, TX, NY, PA, IL, OH, MI, NJ, GA, NC, WA, AZ — do NOT limit to Orange County only # of Employees: · Min 20 · Max 300 · Also run a second pass: Min 11 · Max 200 (catch smaller labs) Revenue (if available in your Apollo plan): · Min $3,000,000 · Max $75,000,000 · Prefer $5M–$50M when scoring Industry / Keywords (use BOTH industry picks + keyword OR): Industries (pick what Apollo shows closest to): · Medical Device · Medical Equipment Manufacturing · Biotechnology (use sparingly — noisy) · Hospital & Health Care (only if company is a SUPPLIER, not a hospital) · Manufacturing (then keyword-narrow hard) Company keywords INCLUDE (OR): dental laboratory, dental lab, dental supply, dental supplies, dental equipment, dental materials, oral surgery, surgical supply, surgical instrument, medical device, med device, orthopedic, dental CAD, dental implant manufacturer, dental prosthetics, sterile processing supply, dental dealer, dental wholesale Company keywords EXCLUDE (NOT): dentist, dental office, dental clinic, orthodontist, DSO, hospital system, urgent care, patient care, veterinary only, software only, SaaS only, staffing, insurance, real estate Technologies (optional signal): · leave blank first pass; later filter for Shopify/BigCommerce only if ecom suppliers # Lookalike seed (manual): Paste domains of: prudental / salvin / young surgical / dess / labtech / wearparts peers Use Apollo "Similar companies" where available

BPeople filters (on that company list)

# Apollo → People · locked to ICP1 company list Person Location: United States (or match company HQ) Seniority: · Owner, Founder, C-Suite, VP, Director, Head · (optional second pass) Manager — demand gen only Job Titles INCLUDE (OR): "VP Marketing", "Vice President Marketing", "Vice President of Marketing", "Director of Marketing", "Marketing Director", "Director Digital Marketing", "Head of Marketing", "Chief Marketing Officer", "CMO", "Owner", "Founder", "Co-Founder", "President", "CEO", "Chief Executive", "Principal" (only with Owner/Founder context — verify), "Demand Generation", "Demand Gen", "Growth Marketing", "Performance Marketing", "Digital Marketing Manager" Job Titles EXCLUDE (NOT): Dentist, DDS, DMD, Hygienist, "Office Manager", "SEO Specialist", "SEO Manager", "Content Writer", Intern, Student, Recruiter, "Account Executive" (unless company is tiny) # Persona priority when multiple people at one company: 1) VP/Director Marketing or CMO 2) Owner/Founder/President/CEO if no marketing seat 3) Demand gen / growth manager

CClean rules (after export)

  • Drop pure clinics/DSOs/hospitals (wrong side of the counter).
  • Drop software-only dental SaaS unless they manufacture/supply physical product.
  • Fit score 1–5: 5 = looks like proof clients (lab/supply, right size, marketing budget plausible).
  • Justin 100 random scrolls before any paid.
