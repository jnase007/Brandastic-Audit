Internal process

Weekly team rhythm

Every Thursday, people who own growth work write a short public update. This replaces mystery status and long meetings.

What you write (keep it short)

  1. Done — what I finished (with links if useful)
  2. Why it mattered — one sentence
  3. Next — up to 3 bullets for next week
  4. Stuck — only if something is blocking you

Channel: #growth-updates

Who posts: Sam, Chloe, Melissa, whoever owns events, whoever owns content.

How work ships

  • Normal posts/ads/pages do not need a long approval chain.
  • If it meets our quality bar, publish.
  • Justin/Sam give feedback after it’s live (“this hook is weak — try this”).
  • The point is speed + craft, not bureaucracy.

You’re done when

The channel exists, the template is pinned, and we get two Thursdays in a row where everyone required actually posts.
