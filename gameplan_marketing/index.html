How we grow without living only on ads

A friend agency that makes around $30M a year showed us how they get meetings and keep clients. This site turns that into our plan at about $2M — written so Sandy, Melissa, Sam, Aimme, AMs, or an AI helper can actually do the work.

If you only remember one thing: we will build relationships on purpose (nights out, gifts, staying close when people change jobs), make the website answer buyer questions, and use ads as backup — not as the whole engine.
