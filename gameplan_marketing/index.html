Brandastic growth plan

This is our plan to get more good meetings and more revenue — without only buying ads. A friend agency that does about $30M a year showed how they grow. We turned that into a plan for us at about $2M.

In plain English

There are two kinds of work in this hub:

  1. Revenue drivers — things that help us get clients, keep clients, and sell more to clients.
  2. Internal processes — how our team works so those things actually happen (updates, reviews, account maps).

Click a page on the left. Each page says what it is, why it matters, who owns it, and the first steps.

Money side

Revenue drivers

Events with clients, gifts, staying close when people change jobs, website content that brings leads, and ads only as support.

 Team side

Internal processes

Weekly check-ins, learning from lost clients, and a simple map of what else each client might buy later.
