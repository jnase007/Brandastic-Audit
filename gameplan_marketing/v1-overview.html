Marketing Strategies That Drive Bookings For Agencies

What this training was about

How a ~$30M agency builds bookings without depending only on ads: private experiential events, customer marketing/gifting, a strict content system (8 pieces per service/sub-service/category), team operating rhythm, and paid as a bridge.
