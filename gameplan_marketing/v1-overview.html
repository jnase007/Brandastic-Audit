Marketing Strategies That Drive Bookings For Agencies

Full documentation from the masterclass — not skim notes. Their words → Brandastic actions. Source notes also saved as source-notes-v1-bookings.md.

Core problem they solved: Grew on heavy ads (up to ~$270K/mo). Retention looked fine (~94–95% MRR) until scale. At one point needed ~$1.2M/mo bookings just to break even after churn. Ads became “crack cocaine growth.” Jan 2025: cut ad spend ~$170K/mo, did not cut marketing — reinvested into team + non-ad motions.

The 3 bets (GTM bridge off ads)

  1. Events — private experiential nights (~$8,500/mo), not conference sponsorships
  2. Customer marketing — reopen past contacts, job-change gifts, referrals, SaaS-style customer marketer hire
  3. Content/SEO machine — 8 pieces per service / sub-service / category

Paid ads stay as a bridge/backup, not the only engine.

1) Events — numbers & rules

  • Cadence: 1 private event / month
  • Budget target: ~$8,500 all-in (travel + entertainment + 2–3 staff)
  • Format that works: experiential (pasta making, wine tasting, activity + dinner) > fancy private-room dinners
  • Headcount: up to ~25 on experiential; fancy dinners price out above ~10 seats
  • Mix: ~50% prospects (VP+ from ICP) / ~50% customers
  • Plus-one unlock: date-night commitment kills no-shows
  • Pre-call: short intro call before dinner so the night is relationship, not pitch theater
  • Outcomes they see: renewals, cross-sell, referrals — not a flood of brand-new cold logos yet
  • Owner: one internal person runs end-to-end

2) Customer marketing — numbers & rules

  • Hire SaaS customer-marketing talent (they’re built for this)
  • Past POCs + referrals: ~$7M bookings last year from that motion (their claim)
  • Hard 30-day push example: 4 meetings booked
  • Triggers: job changes, capital raises, buying signals → move fast
  • Job-change champagne/gift: congratulate → often opens “audit my new company”
  • Gifting: no approval friction for client-love gifts; keep under ~$100; personal > expensive
  • Strategist flags need → ops sends (their “Leia” role)

3) Content / SEO — the 8-piece system

  • For every service, sub-service, and category: publish 8 pieces
  • Do that across the catalog until you dominate the keywords
  • This is a machine, not random blogs
