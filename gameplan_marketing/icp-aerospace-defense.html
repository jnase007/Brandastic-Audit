ICP 3 — Sell the suppliers. Do not chase SpaceX.

Owner-led SoCal manufacturers (20–200 people) that already make parts and look weak online. Costa Mesa sits next to the cluster. This is a wedge, not a full Brandastic pivot — and not Anduril’s agency.

Tertiary ICP Geo: SoCal Owners first No AVL promises No PPC on cold

Tinny’s take

  • Yes — integrate as ICP 3. The /newmarket brief is already deeper than most agency ICPs. It belongs in the Strategies hub beside dental/med and education.
  • Fits the book: owner-led manufacturers (machining, specialty supply) is the same “shop looks dated / buyer can’t RFQ” problem as dental labs — different industry language, same product (site + get found + show follow-up).
  • Do not let it steal ICP 1 oxygen: dental/med is still the cleaner national list+LinkedIn machine with logos already. Aero is SoCal hunt + shows (Pasadena Oct 14–15, LAX Oct 21–22).
  • Agree with the guardrails: no prime chasing, no AVL promises, no ads first, $5–8k open (not $10k ads), Echo-Factory is the real specialist competitor, kill if 20 show talks + 10 calls and nobody pays for the site.
  • Titles: owner/CEO/president first (Apollo: marketing seats are thin). BD second. Don’t wait for a CMO.
