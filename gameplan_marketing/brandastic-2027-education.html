08 · LinkedIn posts
Education-specific · rotate weekly
Enrollment pain
Online schools don’t need “brand awareness.” They need families to find them and reach out and sign up. We help schools show up in search, run the right ads, and build a website families trust. Aimed at families starting the enrollment process — not noise.
10-second test
Enrollment marketing fails in the first 10 seconds. Can a parent tell: • who the school is for • what happens next • why trust you If a parent can't answer those in 10 seconds, no amount of ad spend won’t save it.
Order matters
Fix the way for families to sign up before buying more ads. Most schools pour money into awareness while their site loses families at the front door.
Proof
We run enrollment marketing for the kind of work we do for CalOPS online schools. Same motion, different school. If families have to find you online, that’s the job.

Step-by-step outreach — set this up in Reply.io
How we reach out to enrollment leaders

This is the exact sequence the team follows. Every message goes through Reply.io. All contacts come from Apollo. Here is the order and timing.

StepWhenChannelWhat you send
1 Day 1 LinkedIn Send a connection request. Message: "I work with a few online and charter schools on their enrollment marketing. Always curious to connect with others in the space."
2 After they accept LinkedIn Thank them and share what we do. Message: "Thanks for connecting! We built the enrollment marketing for CalOPS, Think Together, and Heart School — showing up in Google search, running the right ads, and having a website families trust, that helped CalOPS increase enrollment by 75% and generate over 90,000 leads in one year. If that is useful to you: 15-minute walkthrough on how we do it, then you decide if it fits your school."
3 Day 4–7 LinkedIn Follow up if they did not reply. Message: "Quick follow-up — if enrollment is something you are working on right now, happy to walk through what CalOPS does differently. 15 minutes."
4 Day 3–5 Email Send a cold email (runs at the same time as LinkedIn). Subject: "Enrollment marketing at [School Name]." Body: introduce yourself, mention CalOPS results, offer a 15-minute walkthrough.
5 Day 8–10 Email Bump email if no reply. Subject: "Re: Enrollment marketing at [School Name]." Keep it short: "Floating this once more in case it got buried. Happy to keep it to 15 minutes, or tell me a better month."
6 Day 12–14 Value Give them something useful for free. Subject: "One thing I noticed on [School Name]'s site." Body: "I was curious about how your school handles enrollment online, so I spent a few minutes on your site. A couple of things stood out. If you are open to it, I would be happy to share what I saw and see if there is anything we can help with."

What the team needs to do

  • Step 1: Pull the target list from Apollo (already done on the data pull slide)
  • Step 2: Load the screened contacts into Reply.io
  • Step 3: Set up this sequence in Reply.io with the timing above
  • Step 4: Reply.io sends the messages automatically on schedule
  • Step 5: When someone replies, a real person takes over the conversation

How to know it is working

  • Connection accept rate: aim for 20%+
  • Reply rate: aim for 5–10%
  • Walkthroughs booked: aim for 3–5 per month
  • If reply rates are low, change the message copy
  • If accept rates are low, change the target list
