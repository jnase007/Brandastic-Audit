08 · LinkedIn posts
Education-specific · rotate weekly
Enrollment pain
Online schools don’t need “brand awareness.” They need families to find them and reach out and sign up. We help schools show up in search, run the right ads, and build a website families trust. Aimed at families starting the enrollment process — not noise.
10-second test
Enrollment marketing fails in the first 10 seconds. Can a parent tell: • who the school is for • what happens next • why trust you If a parent can't answer those in 10 seconds, no amount of ad spend won’t save it.
Order matters
Fix the way for families to sign up before buying more ads. Most schools pour money into awareness while their site loses families at the front door.
Proof
We run enrollment marketing for the kind of work we do for CalOPS online schools. Same motion, different school. If families have to find you online, that’s the job.
