Internal process

When we lose a client or miss a deal

We tell ourselves the truth. Sugarcoating lost work is how agencies repeat mistakes.

Lost client

  1. Create Slack channel #client-offboarding.
  2. Within 5 days of notice, post a clear write-up of what happened.
  3. Use a fixed AI prompt on the notes/emails/calls to list root causes (so it’s not only feelings).
  4. People can add context. They can’t delete the hard findings.
  5. Assign one fix with an owner and a date.

After a sales call (Justin or Melissa on the call)

Stay 5–10 minutes after and ask:

  • Did we rush?
  • Did we get to pricing cleanly?
  • Were the right decision-makers there?
  • What one slide or line should change?

Write down one improvement the same day.
