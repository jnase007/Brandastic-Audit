How we work inside

When we lose a client or miss a deal

We write down the real reason. Soft stories feel nice and keep us stuck.

Lost client — exact process

  1. Use Slack channel #client-offboarding.
  2. Within 5 days of notice, AM posts what happened.
  3. Run a fixed AI prompt on emails/call notes to list root causes.
  4. Post the prompt and the output. People may add context. Nobody deletes the hard parts.
  5. Pick one fix. Give it an owner and a date.

After a sales call Justin or Melissa joined

Stay 5–10 minutes and answer:

  • Did we rush or ramble?
  • Did pricing feel clean?
  • Were the decision-makers in the room?
  • What one line or slide changes before next time?

Write the one change down the same day.

This is a working handbook. Each page should be clear enough that someone can do the job without a meeting.
