BRANDASTIC 202707
07 · The close rate problem

78 leads went nowhere

93 leads. 15 contracts. 78 lost. We do not systematically know why — and that is the single biggest strategy gap.

What to audit this month

  • Categorize every lost lead: wrong ICP, budget, timing, ghosted, lost to competitor, slow follow-up
  • Tag which channel each came from
  • How many got a proposal vs just a call?
  • How many had same-day follow-up?

What we expect to find

  • 30–40% were wrong ICP (reason to lock markets)
  • 15–20% ghosted after slow follow-up (process fix)
  • 10–15% lost on price (package architecture fix)
  • The rest: timing, budget, competitor — real losses

What 22% close rate means

  • Same 93 leads → 20 contracts instead of 15
  • 5 more at $6K/mo = $360K/year
  • That nearly closes the $380K gap alone
  • No extra ad spend. No extra leads. Just discipline.

Rule: every lost deal gets a one-sentence reason within 48 hours. No exceptions. If we can’t name why we lose, we can’t fix it.
