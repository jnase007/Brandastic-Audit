03 · Ideal target market

Who we want more of

Primary · locked

Dental / medical / surgical B2B

Suppliers & labs that sell to clinics and dealers — not patient clinics as the main agency buyer. SEO + PPC core.

Secondary

Education (CalOPS peers)

Online / heavy-online public & charter schools. Enrollment inquiries. Live CalOPS-scale proof.

Tertiary · SoCal

Aero / Defense manufacturers

Owner-led shops & suppliers — not primes. Get found + quote path. $5–8k open packages.

Buyer we sell to

  • Owner / founder who acts as marketing lead
  • VP / Director of Marketing
  • Operator who cares about leads and revenue

Who we should not chase

  • Tiny budgets that cannot support a real package
  • “Just post for us” with no strategy
  • RFPs we cannot honestly win
  • Anyone who only wants the cheapest hands

Room decision: lock two or three focus markets for 12 months. Everything else is exception-only.

How demand becomes booked work

Primary engines (fund first)

  • Search: pages + guides for locked services × markets
  • Referrals: formal ask system + partners
  • Return / reopen: past proposals + past clients
  • Whitespace: grow top accounts before only hunting new

Support engines

  • Proof library (case studies by market)
  • Light LinkedIn (human, not spam)
  • Client nights / gifts — small, measured
  • Paid for Brandastic — only after sales OS is tight

Default money mix

70% search + referrals + proof · 20% one paid test · 10% events / relationships

BRANDASTIC 202717
Calendar · Days 31–90

Proof + pipeline

W5–6Full reopen pass on the 50-name list. Search pages live for locked offers × markets. Weekly pipeline 20 min without fail. Whitespace notes on top 5 accounts.
W7–8Second proof wave (before/after or short story). Double what books meetings; drop what doesn’t. Referral system on autopilot after wins.
W9–10Tighten close path. Every lost deal has a one-sentence reason. Avg package and recurring cash on the board. Decide if sales OS is ready for a small paid test.
W11–12Publish one clear proof story. QBR-style lookback: conversations · proposals · won/lost · cash. Name next-quarter rocks. Paid test only if follow-up is tight — otherwise keep compounding search + relationships.
