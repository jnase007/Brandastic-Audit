BRANDASTIC 202703
Ideal target market

Who we want more of

Priority

Dental / medical / surgical

Pattern already live (JB White, Paragon, Dental Smart, Young Surgical). Clear offers. Good monthly packages.

Priority

Education (CalOps peers)

Flagship proof. Schools/charters that need serious digital, not hobby social.

Priority

Industrial / specialty B2B

LabTech, Wearparts, Morehouse-style. Search + paid when the offer is sharp.

Buyer we sell to

  • Owner / founder who acts as marketing lead
  • VP / Director of Marketing
  • Operator who cares about leads and revenue, not vanity posts

Who we should not chase

  • Tiny budgets that cannot support a real monthly package
  • “Just post for us” with no strategy
  • RFPs we cannot honestly win (scatter offers)
  • Anyone who only wants the cheapest pair of hands

Room decision: lock two or three focus markets for 12 months. Everything else is exception-only.

BRANDASTIC 202704
Go-to-market strategy

How demand becomes booked work

1. Be findable
→
2. Be referred
→
3. Reopen warm doors
→
4. Prove it
→
5. Close clean
→
6. Expand

Primary engines (fund first)

  • Search: pages + guides for locked services × markets
  • Referrals: formal ask system + partners
  • Return / reopen: past proposals + past clients
  • Whitespace: grow top accounts before only hunting new

Support engines

  • Proof library (case studies by market)
  • Light LinkedIn presence (human, not spam)
  • Client nights / gifts — small, measured
  • Paid for Brandastic — only after sales OS is tight

Default money mix

70% search + referrals + proof · 20% one paid test · 10% events/relationships

If we jump to ads or new services first, we waste money. That is the whole recommendation.
