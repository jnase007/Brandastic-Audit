How to Build a Sales Process

This page is the Directive/Agency Academy sales process sheet turned into a full Brandastic playbook — stages, actions, outcomes, objections, and who gets involved.

Pipeline stages (exact names from file): Stage 0 Intro Call → Stage 1 Strategy Call → Stage 2 Proposal → Stage 3 ROI + Decision Criteria → Stage 4 SOW → Stage 5 Trade.

Stage 0: Intro Call

Goal:

Secure a meeting w/ a prospect who fits the ICP and has a pain.

Actions:

1. Qualify the customer with BANT

Purpose:

- Qualify the account
- Get info for AE to build pitch

Preperation:

- Research - Questions

Outcome:

Schedule a discovery meeting

Next Steps:

Schedule a discovery meeting

Stage 1: Strategy Call

Goal:

Diagnose the pain, identify the urgency, and determine if we can help them

Actions:

1. ACE                             
2. Agenda                     
3. SPI Diagnose           
4. Prescribe              
5. ICED Qualify           
6. Decison Making Process at Company
7. 3 biggest gaps in your SEO and PPC?

Purpose:

- 3 gaps/opps for their search marketing?
- Looking for an agency?
- Who manages their search marketing?
- Do they have a budget and is seo/ppc a priority?
- Timing for getting started?
- Who is the decision maker and whats the process?

Preperation:

- Discovery call preperation- Quick Guide- Full Guide

Outcome:

Schedule proposal with decision makers

Next Steps:

- Timeline for sales process based on impact event
- Invite link to Society, Access to Pulse, One mo. trial of Institute
- Industry relevent case study PDF

Followup:

- If no show, mark "No Show" closed lost it and send back to sales development
- If bad fit, mark "DOA" closed lost
- If good fit or could make something work, move to next stage in pipeline
- If need to get next meeting booked, followup 5 times (IN ONE WEEK) and then mark "Lost Momentum" and send back to sales development

Stage 2: Proposal

Goal:

Understand the pain of the customer and educate them on our value

Actions:

1. ACE                           
2. Agenda                                          
3. Top three pain points: Go deep, prove with case study, ask what impact they think this will have?                            
4. Ballpark pricing                  
5. Confirm decision making criteria              
6. Establish a 3x3

Purpose:

- Show them our solution to their 3 biggest gaps/opps and get them to communcate back that it's valuable
- Go deeper into their organization so that you are talking to more then one person
- Get them excited about working with Directive

Preperation:

- Craft Proposal                         - Account Plan

Outcome:

Decision ROI + criteria review meeting

Next Steps:

- Send intros for 3x3
- Send proposal + recording
- Add event + invites for ROI meeting

Followup:

- If need to get next meeting booked, followup 5 times (IN ONE WEEK) and then mark "Lost Momentum" and send back to sales development

Stage 3: ROI + Decision Criteria

Goal:

Assist the customer through the decision process

Actions:

1. ACE                         
2. Agenda                    
3. Walk thru ROI forecast                        
4. Create decision matrix: us, them, nothing                          
5. Prioritize                              
6. Introduce new criteria.                          
7. Validate impact with 3rd party proof

Purpose:

- ROI: Show the value of hiring Directive and get them to agree to it's accuracy
- Decision Criteria: Uncover assumptions and gaps based on their perspective of Directive

Preperation:

- Build decision criteria

Outcome:

Schedule SOW

Next Steps:

- Send decision criteria worksheet
- Send ROI worksheet
- Attach latest thought leadership as PDF

Followup:

- If need to get next meeting booked, followup 5 times (IN ONE WEEK) and then mark "Lost Momentum" and send back to sales development

Stage 4: SOW

Goal:

Create a contract outlining price and impact it will have on the prospect's business

Actions:

1. ACE                         
2. Agenda  
3. Walk thru SOW and deliverable                          
4. Confirm fit and value of each deliverable                                                                     
5. Present spreadsheet for tradeoffs                             
6. If you give, always get                
7. Setup a reference call

Purpose:

- Make sure we have solved their need with our deliverable
- Confirm their decision process and uncover any gaps
- Get feedback on price + scope and ask for the signature

Preperation:

- Build SOW                           
- Prepare tradeoff sheet                     
- Prepate Quoting Tool

Outcome:

Send SOW with price; scheduled a "trade" meeting w/ final decision makers

Next Steps:

- Send quote spreadsheet
- Send SOW with price
- Connect with a reference

Followup:

- If need to get next meeting booked, followup 5 times (IN ONE WEEK) and then mark "Lost Momentum" and send back to sales development

Stage 5: Trade

Goal:

Close the deal baby

Actions:

1. Keep it light and cordial      
2. Get their negotiation items on table                      
3. Repeat what you heard                 
4. Ask if thats all?                  
5. Have them prioritize               
6. Confirm decision maker            
7. Give slowly and reluctantly                           
8. Make an offer                 
9. Listen to counter offer and repeat                        
10. If ask for more "That changes the deal"                                 
11. Add expiration

Purpose:

- Finalize any last details
- Confirm that management has approved
- CLOSE THE DEAL BABY

Preperation:

- Review red lines                
- Prepare trade-off document for internal use

Outcome:

Update contract and get signed

Next Steps:

- Send final contract with expiration date

Followup:

- If need to get next meeting booked, followup 5 times (IN ONE WEEK) and then mark "Lost Momentum" and send back to sales development

Objection handling (from their sheet)

TypeWhat they sayWhat it tells you / what to do
Lack of understanding the value"We don't need that"Establish the value by asking questions on the impact on their business. Keep asking.
Raising a concern"Im not sure we have the bandwith right now for you"Show them this is not the case. Literally, show them visibly their concern is not one. In this case, you'd show average hours it takes a POC to manage us.
Wrong perception"That wont work with our CMS" "We don't have content resources and you don't offer it"Provide 3rd part proof of how we have overcome this with other clients who are similar
Lack of priority"LinkedIn is not a priority right now"Establish the impact of that service with an ROI calculation based on their current numbers and show them why it needs to be a priority
Pricing is out of their ballpark"We could never get a retainer like that approved"You need to sell at a higher level in the org. Use organizational selling and the 3x3 to get execs involved.
Secret agendaNew marketing executive wants to higher their old agencyNeed to resell any new marketing execs at current accounts or accounts in sales process. Report on value to date and then show projection for future value + roadmap to get there from ops.

Executive involvement by segment

SDR Schedules Disco and Proposal. They are responsible for notifying operations. | AE Schedules ROI-Negotiation stages and is responsible for getting operations involved appropriately.

Discovery Calls | Team Members | Proposals | Team Members | ROI + Decision Criteria | Team Members | SOWs | Team Members

SMB | - | SMB | - | SMB | - | SMB | -

Mid-Market | - | Mid-Market | Innovation Team | Mid-Market | as needed | Mid-Market | Partners, VP, Directors, or Leads

Enterprise | Partners, VP, Directors, or Leads | Enterprise | Partners, VP, Directors, or Leads | Enterprise | Partners, VP, Directors, or Leads | Enterprise | Partners, VP, Directors, or Leads

Definitions

SMB | Bad site, small org, no digital foundation | 5k single service

Mid-Market | Good brand, digital foundation, budget or spend confirmed | 10k single service

Enterprise | You've heard of them, large digital marketing org, big budget/spend | 15k+ single service
