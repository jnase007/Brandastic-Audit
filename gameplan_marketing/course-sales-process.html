How to Build a Sales Process

This is the actual Directive sales process from the course spreadsheet — not a stub. Stages, goals, actions, prep, and outcomes.

Source file: Sales_Process_-_GM_Course.xlsx (hosted). Lesson: How To Build A Sales Process (~1:11).

Pipeline stages (exact)

StageNameGoal
0Intro CallSecure a meeting with a prospect who fits the ICP and has a pain.
1Strategy CallDiagnose the pain, identify urgency, determine if we can help.
2ProposalUnderstand the pain and educate them on our value.
3ROI + Decision CriteriaAssist the customer through the decision process.
4SOWCreate a contract outlining price and business impact.
5Trade / CloseClose the deal.

Stage 0 — Intro Call

Purpose: Qualify the account · get info for AE to build pitch.

Actions: Qualify with BANT.

Questions to cover:

  • 3 gaps/opportunities in their search marketing?
  • Looking for an agency?
  • Who manages search marketing now?
  • Budget — is SEO/PPC a priority?
  • Timing to start?
  • Who is the decision maker and what’s the process?

Outcome: Schedule a discovery / strategy meeting.

Prep: Research + discovery call prep (Quick Guide / Full Guide — named in file).

Stage 1 — Strategy Call

Purpose: Show solution to their 3 biggest gaps · multi-thread past one person · get them excited.

Run of show (exact from file):

  1. ACE
  2. Agenda
  3. SPI Diagnose
  4. Prescribe
  5. ICED Qualify
  6. Decision-making process at their company
  7. 3 biggest gaps in SEO and PPC?

Outcome: Schedule proposal with decision maker.

Stage 2 — Proposal

Run of show:

  1. ACE
  2. Agenda
  3. Top three pain points — go deep, prove with case study, ask what impact they think this will have
  4. Ballpark pricing
  5. Confirm decision-making criteria
  6. Establish a 3×3 (multi-thread)

Prep: Craft proposal · Account plan

Stage 3 — ROI + Decision Criteria

Purpose: ROI — show value of hiring us and get agreement it’s accurate. Decision criteria — uncover assumptions/gaps in how they see us.

Run of show:

  1. ACE
  2. Agenda
  3. Walk through ROI forecast
  4. Create decision matrix: us · them · nothing
  5. Prioritize
  6. Introduce new criteria
  7. Validate impact with 3rd-party proof

Prep: Build decision criteria

Stage 4 — SOW

Purpose: Solve the need with deliverables · confirm decision process · get feedback on price/scope · ask for signature.

Run of show:

  1. ACE
  2. Agenda
  3. Walk through SOW and deliverables
  4. Confirm fit and value of each deliverable
  5. Present spreadsheet for tradeoffs
  6. If you give, always get
  7. Set up a reference call

Prep: Build SOW · tradeoff sheet · quoting tool

Stage 5 — Trade / Close

Negotiation script (exact):

  1. Keep it light and cordial
  2. Get their negotiation items on the table
  3. Repeat what you heard
  4. Ask: is that all?
  5. Have them prioritize
  6. Confirm decision maker
  7. Give slowly and reluctantly
  8. Make an offer
  9. Listen to counter · repeat
  10. If they ask for more: “That changes the deal”
  11. Add expiration

Prep: Review redlines · internal trade-off doc

Outcome: Management approved · close.
