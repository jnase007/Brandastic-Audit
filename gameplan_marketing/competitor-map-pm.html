Where we stack up

If the battlefield is PM software

  • Buildium — mid share, lower published price → SMB landlord/PM default.
  • AppFolio — higher share + higher price → pro PM ops (RHome already lives in AppFolio-class tooling).
  • Yardi / Entrata — largest/enterprise price band.

If the battlefield is RHome (service) vs other PMs

  • Software map ≠ landlord market share. Local/regional PMs won’t show as Buildium bubbles.
  • RHome wedge: multi-market SFH owner acquisition (landlord email engine) while big software brands sell seats — different job.
  • Positioning line: Not another AppFolio. The owner-conversation machine that fills the doors AppFolio manages.
Rabbit / “Gorilla” — not clearly matched in public software lists. If Rabbit is a local PM brand or “Gorilla” is a specific company, send the exact names and I’ll drop them on this chart with share/price estimates.

How to use in a proposal

  1. Show this chart (software field).
  2. Say: big circles own software; they don’t own landlord attention in your cities.
  3. Point to RHome’s offer: direct owner email in priority markets — no ad spend required for the pilot model.
