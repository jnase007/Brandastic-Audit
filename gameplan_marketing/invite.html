Invite email you can copy

Based on the friend-agency invite (Garrett’s style). Justin sends it. No deck. No agenda PDF. One follow-up max.

Why this email works

  • Feels personal
  • Says what the night is
  • Allows a guest
  • Says the point is relationships
  • Says there is no pitch
  • Gives day and time
  • Soft ending
Hi [Name] — I wanted to personally invite you to a [cooking class / wine tasting] we’re hosting for marketing leaders and owners in Orange County — and their guests. We’ve been putting small groups together with clients and friends in the space. This one is hands-on at [Sur La Table South Coast Plaza / Argaux Costa Mesa] — [short dish or tasting focus]. The intent is simple: build real relationships. We encourage everyone to bring a plus one, and there’s no pitch or marketing agenda — just a night out and time to connect with other leaders. It’s [Day], [Month] [Date] at [6:30 pm]. If you’re free, I’d love to have you there and can send the details. Best, Justin

After they say yes, Aimme sends parking/address. After the event, Justin sends a short personal note about something they talked about — not a service menu.

This is a working handbook. Each page should be clear enough that someone can do the job without a meeting.
