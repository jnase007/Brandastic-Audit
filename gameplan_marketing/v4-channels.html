Best LinkedIn ad + channel playbook

Deep notes from How to Advertise Your Agency (Garrett / Directive). Numbers, exact copy, ranked incentives, and Brandastic takeaways — not a skim.

1) The best LinkedIn ad (exact pattern)

He called this his best ad ever written — worked ~7 years and still works. Format: LinkedIn Conversation Ad (convo ad) with no subject line so LinkedIn pulls the first name into the open (native first-name merge is limited; blank subject is the hack).

Exact copy structure (commerce example)

Hey, Aram — 29 minutes, $105 Amazon gift card, and you'll find out if your agency's product feed is quietly killing your margins. I lead Directive Commerce, the only agency obsessed with B2B commerce advertising. Bad feed and poor ads don't just lose clicks — they lose margin. We'll show you exactly how to fix it. Take a call with our experts and I'll send you a gift card right after.

Line-by-line SOP

PieceRuleWhy
OpenFirst name via blank subject on convo adFeels 1:1; can't do clean merge natively the same way
Time charm29 minutes not 30Charm timing — more specific = more believable
Money charm$105 not $100Charm pricing — same stack, sharper
ProblemSpecific ICP pain (here: product feed killing margins)Not “grow your business”
Who you areOne-line category ownership“Only agency obsessed with X”
Proof of stakesLose margin, not just clicksRaises urgency for decision-makers
CTATake a call (active) — not “if interested”Active language only
Gift ruleGift right after the callPays for show-up / held intro, not tire-kickers
Length≤3 short lines of body blocks“If you write more than three lines, people scroll”

Claim from session: ~$30M attributed to this style of ad over time. Treat as his number — use the structure, not the brag as a Brandastic guarantee.

Exact LinkedIn Message Ad (live example)

This is the in-message / Conversation Ad pattern SaaS and B2B agencies copy. Advertiser: Directive. Format: Message Ad.

Exact copy (with merge tags as shown)

Hey %FIRSTNAME%, 29 mins, a $105 Amazon gift card, and you'll find out if %COMPANYNAME%'s product feed is quietly killing your margins. I lead Directive Commerce, the only agency obsessed with B2B ecommerce advertising. A bad feed and poor ads don't just lose clicks, it loses margin. We'll show you exactly how to fix it. Brandastic rewrite template (swap ICP)

Hey %FIRSTNAME%, 29 mins, a $105 Amazon gift card, and you'll find out if %COMPANYNAME%'s [ICP problem] is quietly costing you [money/leads/margin]. I lead Brandastic [category], the [positioning line]. [Stake line]. We'll show you exactly how to fix it. Take a call with one of our experts, and I'll send you the gift card right after. Interested? [Yes! Book Call] [Not sure? Tell me more]

Incentive ladder still: AirPods → Kindle → $105 Amazon. Same Message Ad chassis.

2) Ranked free-product incentives (do not skip)

  1. AirPods — #1
  2. Kindle — #2
  3. Amazon gift card at $105 — #3 (never round to $100 in the ad)

These are show-up / held-meeting incentives, not “download a PDF.” Even after gift cost, cost per held intro fell vs non-incentivized paths in his telling.

Anti-pattern: non-incentive retarget like “unlock your audit” — he said it never worked for him despite good creative.

3) LinkedIn Conversation Ad ops (tools + numbers)

Ops ruleDetail
Format mixContent clips (so you’re not pure DR) + sponsored/thought-leadership + convo ads (“old faithful”)
ThrottleHumans can only get ~2 convo ads / 15 days — auction is throttled
BidsHe sets bids at a minimum ~$100 even if LinkedIn recommends $3 — to win sends in the auction
AudiencesAlways multiple campaigns to different audiences; don’t run one lazy ad set forever
Content engineFull-time video person + Austin studio clips so LinkedIn isn’t only gift-card DR
Ad librariesStudy Meta + LinkedIn ad libraries for inspiration (he walked “Everything Else Evolved” style campaigns)

4) What he said the LinkedIn engine did (credentialing)

  • Peak spend: about $270K/month on LinkedIn for Directive
  • 10,000 target accounts → held sales calls with ~6,800 in 18 months
  • Grew ~$6M → $22M in 18 months on these types of ads
  • Then it got commoditized / less efficient — still the pattern to understand
  • True story he told: spent $270K × 6 months before cracking monetization — zero deals for six months, did not quit; sat intros, rewrote ads, fixed packaging/sales

Brandastic: do not copy $270K/mo. Copy patience + measurement + one primary channel you can afford for long enough to learn.

6) Webinar machine (pairs with ads)

  • Monthly webinar with people the client would actually work with if they hire you
  • AEs pitch signups; delivery team presents (not AE-only stage)
  • Example framing: “Q4 Panic Playbook…” ~45 min, limited seats
  • Meta video ads with real team faces
  • Rule: execution sustained creates differentiation — 16–24 months of world-class webinars beats one webinar

7) TAM + Primer (before spend)

  • Build account TAM; verify humans (+ optional AI site checks)
  • Compress fat lists (session theme: 20K → tighter year TAM so dollars aren’t diluted)
  • Primer-class list match tools; test matched lists vs native platform targeting
  • Only spend against TAM slices with real ACV/CAC room
  • Do not advertise “Services TAM” mush — pick the offer + accounts that can buy

8) Budget principles

  • Spend the least you can without stopping
  • Scale slowly
  • Never stop/start (kills learning + auction memory)
  • Don’t buy pressure with an oversized early budget
  • Measure held meetings + ACV, not vanity CPL

9) Brandastic play (what we actually do with this)

  1. Pick one primary paid channel we can sustain for 6–12+ months (likely LinkedIn for B2B ICPs; Meta when webinar is the wedge).
  2. Write convo ads with: charm time (29), charm price ($105 or AirPods/Kindle), ICP problem, active CTA, gift after call.
  3. Staff the meeting path (AE + delivery face on webinars).
  4. Track held intros and pipeline, not lead form vanity.
  5. Pair with events/content so Brandastic doesn’t look like pure DR spam.
