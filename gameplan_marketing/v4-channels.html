Video 04 / Channels
VIDEO 04

Meta · LinkedIn · gift cards — full detail

This is the channel section from the advertising training, expanded so you can build media plans and team tickets from it. Not a one-liner.

Rule: Meta owns webinars. LinkedIn is ABM/content + legacy customer-gen. Gift cards manufacture show-up intent on social. Non-incentive “free audit” retarget does not work.

1. Meta (primary for webinars)

  • He is blunt: “Meta is for webinars.” It is “absolutely crushing.”
  • You can use Meta for other things, but webinars are the standout use case.
  • Can take the agency upmarket on Meta (walkthrough quality: CMO-level leads).
  • Motion: monthly webinar featuring people the client will actually work with if they hire you.
  • Why that matters for brand: big agencies are not only the CEO’s face; small agencies often are founder-led. As you raise ACV, the experience must feel enterprise — webinars with delivery leaders help.
  • Integrates thought leadership + lead gen in one paid motion.
  • Website + funnel must match: he moved away from cheap CTAs like “book a control call” / “explore partnership” because he is trying to increase ACV — brand/experience should feel upmarket.
  • LinkedIn webinar CAC often fails first-principles math (CPM/CPC vs webinar lifecycle conversion) — that is why webinars live on Meta, not forced onto LinkedIn.

2. LinkedIn

  • Historic home of his gift-card customer generation machine at peak about $270K/mo.
  • Still used; still works in evolved form after the tactic got commoditized.
  • Include content clips so the brand does not look like pure direct response only.
  • List upload / matched audiences: uses tools like Primer to upload TAM lists; always tests list-based vs native algorithm audiences.
  • Do not assume LinkedIn webinars clear CAC — check the math before copying Meta webinar structure there.
  • Proof at peak scale: 10,000 target accounts; 6,800 sales calls in 18 months; $6M → $22M in 18 months off these types of ads.

3. Gift cards / incentives (activation theory)

  • Search problem: has intent, weak graphics for upmarket services monetization.
  • Social problem: has graphics, weak intent.
  • Solution: gift cards manufacture intent on social.
  • Critical rule: prospect cannot get the gift card unless they show up to the pitch.
  • Even with the weighted cost of the gift card, cost per intro call held went down.
  • All he needs is for them to hear the pitch — incentivizing attendance lowered cost per attended pitch including gift cost, enough that CAC improved vs not using incentives.
  • Zoom-out theory: invest in activation more than acquisition.
  • Method name for the broader system: Customer Generation (he called himself the grandfather of gift-card-for-demo ads for SaaS and his own agency).

4. What never worked (do not copy)

  • Non-incentive retarget offers like “unlock your audit” — he says they work like dog shit.
  • Even with really cool creative and good ideas, non-incentive versions never worked for him in his career.
  • Do not build the media plan around free-audit retarget as the hero offer.

5. How this fits the rest of Video 04

  • Before channels: real TAM + verified accounts + Primer match + positioning + Audience/Offer.
  • Alongside channels: HubSpot + Salesforce + Gong sales discipline; proposal-held win rate 20–40%.
  • After channels: CAPI on Meta + LinkedIn; optimize to influence revenue (Meta CTR ceiling ~4–6% means ~94% never click).
  • Creative target: VP Marketing change agent, not threatened practitioner (do not sell SEO to SEO manager).
  • Advertise division/department + outcome; only spend on TAM slices with CAC room (tech + industrial; skip low-spend services TAM).

6. Brandastic media plan tickets (from this section only)

  1. If running webinars → Meta first; write the monthly webinar + who appears on camera (delivery leads, not only Justin).
  2. LinkedIn = ABM/content clips + optional customer-gen tests; do not force webinar CAC there without math.
  3. If using incentives: gift only on show-up; track cost per held intro including gift cost.
  4. Kill “free audit” retarget as primary offer.
  5. Wire Primer (or equivalent) list match; A/B list vs native.
  6. Report influence pipeline/revenue, not only CPL.
