Revenue drivers

What “revenue drivers” means

These are the growth activities that should create money — new conversations, new proposals, more work from current clients, or leads from the website.

The five revenue drivers from Video 1

  1. Client events — small dinners / classes in Costa Mesa so people like us and want to work with us.
  2. Client gifts — thoughtful gifts through Goody when something real happens in their life or business.
  3. Stay in touch / reopen — notice job changes and old contacts, then reach out like a human.
  4. Website content (SEO) — clear pages on brandastic.com that answer buyer questions and ask them to talk to us.
  5. Ads (support only) — paid ads help fill the calendar while the relationship work grows. Ads are not the whole plan.

How to judge if it’s working

Not “likes” or vague brand awareness. We care about:

  • Meetings booked
  • Proposals sent
  • New work from current clients
  • Website form fills / calls
