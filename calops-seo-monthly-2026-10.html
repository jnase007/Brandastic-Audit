Opportunity

On Google AI Mode (California locale), California Online Public Schools / CalOPS holds 14.4% share of voice with 100 mentions (57.5% mention share), 139 own-domain citations (25.8%), and average answer position 2.06. CAVA leads at 21.6% SOV; Connections Academy is near-peer at 13.2%. CalOPS is repeatedly first on flexibility, SEL, parent visibility, and independent-study clarity questions, and californiaops.org is the top cited domain—but generic ‘California online publics’ framing still blurs brand identity, and ‘best for’ niches (advanced, credit recovery, STEM) often go to rivals. October organic clicks and Generative AI impressions both declined MoM while average position held steady.

Recommendations
  • Standardize CalOPS branding on every high-traffic explainer so AI stops treating the name as a generic category label.
  • Ship five authority hubs (SEL, independent study, college pathways, elementary structure, outcomes data) with fresh metrics.
  • Package FlexTrack/ThriveTrack-style journeys for bullying, health, and schedule-flexibility families AI already recommends CalOPS for.
  • Publish annual outcome snapshots (graduation, A-G, dual enrollment) so AI Mode has current, citable proof.

INTERPRETATION Traditional clicks vs Generative AI visibility
October organic clicks were 7,616 versus 9,861 in September (-22.8%), while Google Generative AI impressions fell to 80,076 from 94,991 (-15.7%). Average organic position held near steady (6.6 → 6.6). Both traditional clicks and Generative AI impressions moved lower in the same month, so AI exposure does not explain the click decline by itself. Calendar, graduation-requirements, and homepage pages still dominate Generative AI impressions. These AI figures are impressions only—not proof of enrollments or conversions.
  • Keep academics-calendar, graduation-requirements, independent-study, and SEL pages structured as quotable hubs so AI Overviews and classic results share the same proof.
  • Standardize the CalOPS brand descriptor (not generic ‘California online publics’) on high-AI-impression pages and authority hubs to convert visibility into preferred recommendations.
