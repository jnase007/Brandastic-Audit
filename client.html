RHOME × Brandastic04
Diagnosis

Why landlord demand loses inside a PM website

Renter side wins by default

  • “Homes for rent” / apply paths are high-intent and frequent
  • Cheaper CPC keywords (apartments, houses for rent + city)
  • Faster micro-conversions (views, saves, applications)
  • Remarketing audiences fill with browsers of listings

Landlord side needs design

  • Fewer searches, higher value (“property management company near me,” “hire property manager,” “landlord property management [city]”)
  • Longer trust cycle (fees, service level, local team)
  • Needs city pages + proof + clear “manage my property” CTA
  • Must be a primary conversion — not buried under resident portal traffic

If you don’t separate campaigns, creatives, landers, and conversion events, the platforms will always prefer the renter machine.

RHOME × Brandastic05
Paid search plan

Google / Microsoft: build a landlord engine, fence the renter engine

Landlord campaigns (priority budget)

  • Brand — protect RHOME / Associa-adjacent brand queries
  • Core PM intent by market — “property management [city]”, “hire property manager [city]”
  • Landlord problem terms — eviction help framing carefully, vacant rental, bad tenants → service pages (compliance-reviewed)
  • Competitor conquest (optional, controlled)
  • Exact/phrase heavy; RSA pinned headlines for investor outcomes

Renter campaigns (capped, not deleted)

  • Separate budget ceiling so it can’t eat landlord share
  • Only markets where vacancy / unit fill is a stated goal
  • Negatives shared upward so renter terms never bleed into landlord ad groups
  • Bid only to apply / qualified inquiry — not soft clicks

Negative keyword system (non-negotiable)

Shared lists: jobs, salary, DIY, free, template, apartment for rent (on landlord campaigns), student housing noise, pure tenant verbs on owner campaigns. Weekly expand from search terms. This is how you “shift the balance without writing off renters.”

Landing pages

Paid traffic should not share the homepage with everyone.

Today

  • Homepage serves investors, residents, renters, acquisitions
  • Contact often routes through Locations (extra step)
  • City pages exist but need paid-specific owner CTAs and message match

Recommended

  • Speed + mobile form first
  • Thank-you event fires once, hard
  • Renter ads → listing/apply paths only
