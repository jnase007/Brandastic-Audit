Opportunity

Brandastic holds only 0.2% AI share of voice with 5 mentions (0.7% mention share), 6 own-domain citations (0.1%), and average answer position 4.0 — well behind Ignite Visibility (3.4% SOV, 88 mentions, pos 2.64), Klientboost (2.7%), and Coalition Technologies (2.0%). Favorable sentiment is solid at 80%, and AI already recognizes Brandastic for mid-market integrated branding + digital, ROI focus, and SoCal presence. The gap is frequency and shortlist power: third-party directories (Clutch, Semrush Agencies, DesignRush, Reddit) dominate citations while brandastic.com is nearly invisible. Strategic priorities are ROI proof density, California AI query clusters, transparency packaging, and converting bare mentions into recommendations.

Recommendations
  • Lead every AI-visible page and case study with quantified revenue outcomes (120x / 18x style proofs) and publish 3–5 additional revenue-tied results.
  • Target 5–7 high-intent California AI query clusters (OC full-service, B2B lead gen, Shopify + performance, brand relaunch) to lift SOV from ~0.2% toward competitive parity.
  • Launch a named reporting / transparency framework so AI and prospects can defend premium pricing against 4/5 transparency and lock-in concerns.
  • Add “when to choose Brandastic” sections and neutral buyer guides; upgrade Clutch/DesignRush/Semrush Agency profiles so citations become shortlist recommendations, not name-drops.
