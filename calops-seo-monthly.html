Urgent strategic priorities

1
Beat Typical Online Tradeoffs
Quantify screen time, parent load, and social opportunities to outperform generic virtual competitors.
2

Build law-to-practice explainers so CalOPS, not CDE alone, defines independent study done right.

4
Clarify Grade-Band Stories
Translate TK-12 mentions into three crisp, reusable stage-specific elevator narratives.
Opportunity

On ChatGPT (CA), CalOPS leads this set with 24.4% share of voice, ~155 mentions, average position 1.56, 311 own-domain citations (15.8%), and 85% favorable sentiment — a top-two default with CAVA (21.4% SOV, pos 2.02). Connections Academy 10.3%, K12 4.6%, Pacific Charter 0.3%. cde.ca.gov still edges californiaops.org on citations (337 vs 311). ChatGPT already frames CalOPS as the middle ground between rigid virtual school and unstructured homeschool (LiveClasses, 1:1s, MTSS/SEL) but has no branded label for that model, thin academic-outcomes proof, and recurring parent-load/screen-time hedges. Priorities: name the model, independent-study explainers that out-cite CDE, honest fit profiles, and grade-band stories.

Recommendations
  • Name the model (guided independent study / structured-flex) in a few reusable sentences on homepage, enroll, and independent-study pages so ChatGPT stops calling it a vague compromise.
  • Publish law-to-practice independent-study explainers and a Results & Outcomes hub (graduation, A-G, CAASPP, AP/dual enrollment) so CalOPS, not only CDE/CAVA/Method, sets the quality bar.
  • Write honest grade-band day-in-the-life guides (screen time, parent load, 1:1 frequency) plus 4-8 learner-profile pages (athletes, IEP/ADHD, credit-deficient, gifted, travelers).
  • Keep Academic Calendar and enrollment URLs stable — they already dominate GSC; pair flexibility headlines with measurable support commitments (caseloads, check-in cadence, counselor response times).
