Owner Desk

How RHOME finds residential landlords — including DIY listings, Airbnb hosts, and an AI-assisted path from listing → title → owner.

RHOME Internal leave-behind Oct 20, 2026 SEO/PPC audit →
01 · The idea

Zillow is the fishing hole — not the boat

Landlords already show up when they list a house to rent. Zillow (and sites like it) do not sell a clean “landlord lead” pipe to PMs. The play is: spot DIY / STR activity → find the owner behind the address → offer management.

Not this

Buy ads inside Zillow hoping for owner leads. Scrape and spam. Optimize paid media to renter applications.

This

Treat each DIY listing as a deal on the owner. Qualify with AI. Match title/assessor. Sales or ads only on real owners.

Plus always

Google “hire PM / property management [city]” + Meta landlord campaigns remain the high-intent backbone (see main audit).

03 · Working those deals

Each DIY listing = an owner deal

Sales is not applying as a renter. Marketing is not sending landlord ads to “homes for rent.”

Listing typeOwner situationOffer
DIY long-term (Zillow etc.)Self-managing, often tiredLease + full manage
Vacant / long DOM / price cutsFrustrated“We’ll get it rented”
Airbnb / VRBOSTR fatigue or rulesFlip to steady LTR
Multi-listing same hostPortfolio painPortfolio PM

Pipeline

  1. Spot — DIY / STR in RHOME cities
  2. Qualify — SFR/small multi, owner-posted, in-area
  3. Owner — address → title/assessor → mailing/LLC
  4. Reach — call/text/email/mail or ads match (not tenant tour form)
  5. Land — owner LP or calendar only
  6. CRM — every listing is a deal stage

Example open

“Saw your rental in [area]. If you’re open to it, we manage long-term in [city] — screening, lease, maintenance — so you’re not fielding showings. Worth 10 minutes to compare?”

Airbnb: “If turns and platform fees are wearing you down, we can set it up long-term instead.”

AI scores

  • DIY owner vs big-PM branded listing
  • STR vs long-term signals
  • Stale / price-cut / vacant language
  • In RHOME service area
  • SFR / small multi vs large multifamily
  • Already in CRM? → suppress
  • Output: pursue / skip / needs human

Data stack (not scrape-first)

  • Listings: compliant intake, vendor feeds, ops CSV — full DIY scrape of Zillow/Airbnb often fights ToS
  • Title: assessor / PropStream-class property graph
  • Contact: licensed skip-trace only after AI filter
  • CRM: HubSpot / AppFolio-adjacent deal record

05 · Airbnb → long-term

Named campaign: STR Exit

Strong segment — not 100% of budget. Burned-out hosts want steady income without turns and platform fees.

Who

Owner-hosts (not pure co-hosts). Multi-listing hosts. Markets where RHOME already operates.

LP

“From Airbnb to steady long-term income in [City]” — timeline, screening, make-ready, CTA. Form: own vs co-host, # doors, city.

Media

Search: exit Airbnb, convert to long-term lease. Meta: host/investor angles geo-fenced. Plus Owner Desk on STR listings.

07 · Guardrails

So it doesn’t get ugly

  • No fake renter inquiries to “get the owner”
  • Respect listing-site terms — prefer compliant data paths
  • TCPA / DNC / email rules on every contact path
  • Fair housing on all LTR messaging
  • AI filters hard before you pay skip-trace
  • Never optimize landlord ads to portal logins or rental applies
  • Owner LPs only — not homepage “For Rent”
  • Suppress current clients and known managed doors
  • Document claim review for multi-state + Canada
