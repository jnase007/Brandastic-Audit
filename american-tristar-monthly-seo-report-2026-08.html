On Google AI Mode (CA), American Tri-Star currently holds 0% share of voice, 0 mentions, 0 own-domain citations, and n/a average position — it is not on the shortlist. State Farm (4.0% SOV), Progressive (3.0%), Travelers (2.7%), and Mercury (2.2%) occupy the visible carrier band; 86.7% sits in the long tail. When AI does describe Tri-Star, it is as a human, local, independent California agency — then it hedges on inconsistent service, claims, and digital convenience, and confuses the name with TRISTAR TPA. google.com / Yelp / insurance.ca.gov dominate citations. Priorities are visible SLAs, independent-agency-not-TPA language everywhere, basic digital tools, and turning Yelp/BBB/site citations into shortlist mentions. Recommendations
