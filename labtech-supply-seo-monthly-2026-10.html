Opportunity

On ChatGPT (California locale), LabTech Supply Company holds 4.4% share of voice with 53 mentions (30.3%), 54 own-domain citations (3.4%), ~70% favorable sentiment, and average answer position 4.49. Ezad is near-peer at 4.3% SOV; PSA and Labs USA trail. ChatGPT already surfaces LabTech on speed, custom instrument tables, and chemical-resistant tops, but still frames the brand as a flexible SoCal fabricator rather than the default operations-friendly mid-tier. Citation weight remains with google.com, hansonlab.com, and laboratorybydesign.com. October organic clicks declined while Generative AI impressions rose — pair commercial ranking strength with named quick-ship and multi-party project playbooks AI can quote.

Recommendations
  • Name and publish a 72-hour / quick-ship phenolic program with lead-time proof.
  • Publish SoCal first-call proof (references, delivery guarantees, project photos) for California lab RFQs.
  • Codify furniture-lead playbooks for multi-party GC/design-build projects so ChatGPT stops treating LabTech as a swappable component.
  • Close the trust stack: simplified warranty language, SEFA/durability summaries, and third-party reviews.

INTERPRETATION Traditional clicks vs Generative AI visibility
October organic clicks were 800 versus 954 in September (-16.1%), while Google Generative AI impressions rose to 28,985 from 26,544 (+9.2%). Average organic position improved slightly (13.5 → 12.5). Higher AI Overview/AI Mode visibility is an additional search surface, but these figures are impressions only and do not independently establish the cause of the click decline. Commercial bench/table pages remain the core traditional drivers.
  • Keep laboratory-tables, workbenches, and phenolic-resin guides answer-ready (specs, SEFA, lead times) so AI Overviews and classic results reinforce the same commercial proof.
  • Publish named quick-ship / SoCal delivery case notes so rising Generative AI visibility has quotable program language, not only product category pages.
