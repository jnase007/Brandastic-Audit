Favorable sentiment is 80%, and AI already associates CH with board education, developer transitions, master-planned expertise, and boutique high-touch service. The gap is frequency and shortlist power: competitors and directories (hoaunlimited.com, Reddit, Access, Action) dominate citations while ch-pm.com is nearly invisible. Service consistency and responsiveness complaints undercut the premium story. Strategic priorities are trust packaging, AI category ownership, reliability SLAs, and California specialist narrative.

Recommendations
  • Codify a sharp niche — governance-savvy, tech-forward partner for complex California communities and board-led transitions — and repeat it in proposals, bios, talks, and third-party articles.

  • Launch a reliability program (response SLAs, update cadences, escalation paths) and publish before/after turnaround metrics to own the responsiveness narrative.
  • Win 3 clear AI shortlist categories with cornerstone guides and California/SoCal/OC specialist content so models recommend CH beyond branded queries; reclaim review gateways (BBB, Birdeye, Reddit).
