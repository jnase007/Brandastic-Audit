BRANDASTIC 202714
06 · Content machine + sales OS

What “tight” means before we spend

Content / SEO machine (per locked ICP)

  • Service × market pages that a normal person gets
  • 1 case study that proves the outcome
  • 1 guide / listicle that earns links + trust
  • Proof library (before/after, numbers, quotes)
  • AI drafts OK · humans publish + own voice

Sales follow-up OS

  • Stages visible on one board
  • Every proposal logged
  • Every loss has a one-sentence reason
  • Weekly 20-minute pipeline (no exceptions)
  • Same-day reply standard on warm leads

Brandastic Google Ads / Meta for ourselves is Phase 4 — after markets lock, content ships, and follow-up is boringly reliable.
