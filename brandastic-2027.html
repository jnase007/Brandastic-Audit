Not “all businesses.” Three specific buyer pools where Brandastic already has proof or positioning.

Hungry for quotes but skeptical of “marketing.”

Combined addressable: ~1,000–2,000 companies across three markets. We need 6 net new to close the 2026 gap. The pool is not the problem — targeting and close rate are.

04 · Discovery snapshot
What we’ve learned about Brandastic

Strengths

  • Real year-over-year growth ($1.67M → ~$2.12M)
  • Search + referral engine creates most new clients organically
  • Deep delivery bench: SEO, paid, web, creative, content under one roof
  • Flagship proof: CalOPS-scale education work wins confidence
  • AI-assisted workflows speed content without adding headcount
  • OC roots — local trust in dental/med supplier cluster

What’s broken (honest)

  • 78 of 93 leads went nowhere — no systematic tracking of why
  • Churn rate invisible — no monthly number on any board
  • Channel attribution missing — can’t fund what we can’t measure
  • ICP too wide day-to-day — saying yes to anyone who calls
  • Follow-up uneven — leads go cold, nobody owns next step
  • Package pricing is a $5.5–7K range, not a clear tier
  • No formal referral system — asks happen randomly

Opportunities (this strategy addresses these)

  • Close rate: 16% → 22% on same leads = ~5 more contracts = ~$360K/yr. No extra ad spend.
  • Whitespace: growing existing clients is 3–5× cheaper than finding new ones.
  • Referral system: formalize the ask after every win — highest-quality leads at zero cost.
  • SEO compounds: pages we ship now earn traffic for years. Weekly ship cadence = compounding asset.
  • AI + process: use AI for draft speed, human for judgment + quality. More output without more people.

BRANDASTIC 202719
05 · Channel · Referral system
Highest-quality leads at zero cost

System (not random)

  • After every clear win: send the referral email within 48 hours
  • Quarterly check-in with happy clients: “who else should we help?”
  • Partner referrals: web dev, PR, accounting firms
  • Log every referral intro in CRM with channel tag

Expected volume

  • 17 current clients × quarterly ask = ~68 asks/year
  • If 15% produce an intro = ~10 warm referral leads/year
  • Referral close rate typically 2–3× cold = ~40%+
  • Expected: 4–6 new clients/year from referrals alone

That’s potentially the entire 2026 gap from one free channel.
