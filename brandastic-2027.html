07 · LinkedIn posts

Enrollment pain
Online schools don’t need “brand awareness.” They need families to find them and inquire. Search. Paid. Site trust. Aimed at enrollment conversations — not noise.
10-second test
Enrollment marketing fails in the first 10 seconds. Can a parent tell: • who the school is for • what happens next • why trust you If not, spend won’t save it.
Order matters
Fix the inquiry path before buying more ads. Most schools pour money into awareness while their site loses families at the front door. Get found. Make it clear. Then scale.
Proof
We run enrollment marketing for CalOPS-scale online schools. Same motion, different school. If families have to find you online, that’s the job — and we already know how.
