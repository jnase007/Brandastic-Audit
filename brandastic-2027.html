  • Service × market pages that a normal person gets

  • 1 guide / listicle that earns links + trust
  • Proof library (before/after, numbers, quotes)

  • Every proposal logged
  • Every loss has a one-sentence reason
  • Weekly 20-minute pipeline (no exceptions)
