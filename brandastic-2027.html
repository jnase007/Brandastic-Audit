BRANDASTIC02 / 10
Positioning

What we are · what we are not

What we are

  • A full-service digital marketing agency that grows real businesses
  • Strong at search, paid media delivery, retainers, and long client relationships
  • Orange County based · serves clients across ICPs (dental/medical, education, industrial, more)
  • Path to a bigger firm: clean growth, higher packages, less chaos

What we are not (yet)

  • Not a software company (Comply is a separate lane)
  • Not “get new clients only by running Meta ads for ourselves”
  • Not trying to be everything for everyone
  • Not growing by stacking tiny unprofitable packages forever

Strengths to run at

Search + referrals · delivery depth · CalOps-scale proof · Agency Academy learning · team that can execute

Gaps we must close

Clean win/loss tracking · sales rhythm · capacity before big paid push · average package size · less owner-only sales

How we win clients

Engines we fund in 2027

Primary

1. Search (Google)

Pages for the clients we want. Proof stories. Course / hub content. Keep ranking and converting.

Primary

2. Referrals

Ask on purpose. Partner list. Thank-you path. Make it easy for happy clients to send us work.

Support

3. Proof + content

Case studies by client type. Simple posts. Authority without empty noise.

Test only

4. Paid for the agency

Small LinkedIn or niche test only after sales follow-up is tight and capacity can take wins. Do not outspend our ability to close and deliver.

Phase 1 · next 30–60 days

Bookings from relationships. Client nights / gifts only if measured. Reopen warm doors. Protect delivery quality.

Phase 2 · days 30–120

Content machine on offers we already sell well. Proof pages. Not random posting.

Phase 3 · parallel

Sales OS hard enough for paid: who owns follow-up, speed to proposal, weekly pipeline review.

Phase 4 · only after 0–3

Advertise the agency. Message-ad style tests. One audience. One offer. Measure meetings and closed cash.

Source: strategic plan from Agency Academy Videos 1–4. Launch at most one new offer later (Phase 5) — optional.
