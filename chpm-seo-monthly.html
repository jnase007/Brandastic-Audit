INTERPRETATION Traditional clicks vs Generative AI visibility
October organic clicks were 776 versus 958 in September (-19.0%), while Google Generative AI impressions were essentially flat at 8,255 vs 8,310 (-0.7%). Average organic position improved (9.7 → 8.9). Brand queries still drive most clicks; informational HOA blog pages lead Generative AI impressions. AI Overviews can answer board questions without a site visit, but these AI figures are impressions only — not proof of citations, qualified leads, or new communities.
  • Refresh high-AI-impression governance posts (private board meetings, bare walls vs walls-in, transitions) with clearer board takeaways and CTAs so AI surfaces and classic results share the same proof.
  • Publish five flagship governance/transition/communication/technology explainers so AI systems cite ch-pm.com instead of competitor directories and lists.
