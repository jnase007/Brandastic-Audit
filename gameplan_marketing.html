Our go-to-market plan

This is not an audit and not “adopt their playbook” language. It is Brandastic’s go-to-market plan for the revenue motions we will run — events, customer marketing, content/SEO, team operating system, and paid as a bridge — informed by what a friend agency (~$30M) does well, sized for us (~$2M).

01 · Diagnosis

Why this GTM exists — and what it means at our size

The speaker’s pain: ads scaled pipeline but churn forced ever-higher spend until EBITDA died. Brandastic is smaller, but the same trap exists if growth = only paid media + Justin hustle.

Their problem

  • Up to ~$270K/mo ads to outpace churn
  • ~94–95% MRR looked fine until scale
  • Needed ~$1.2M/mo bookings just to break even
  • Ads = “crack cocaine” growth — no valuation-friendly margin

Brandastic version

  • Paid media is a core delivery skill (Sam’s lane) — good
  • Risk: new logos depend too much on Justin + ads + referrals luck
  • Client base is under-mined for expansion, referrals, job-change reopens
  • Content/SEO exists but isn’t a booking machine yet
  • No fixed monthly experiential pipeline event
🎯
Strategy north star
Build a bookings bridge that does not require ever-higher ad spend. Paid stays a channel. Relationships, events, customer marketing, and content become durable pipeline.

03 · Pillar 1 · Events

Brandastic monthly experiential pipeline

Copy the masterclass mechanic, size it to Brandastic. Not $8.5K Berlin dinners — OC experiences we can run at a fixed monthly cost.

Offer design

ItemBrandastic standard
Cadence1 event / month (lock on calendar year)
Budget capStart $2,500–$4,000 all-in (venue/activity + food + light travel). Scale toward $6–8.5K only after ROI proof.
FormatExperiential + meal: cooking class, wine tasting, pickleball + dinner, brewery private room with activity — not stiff private dining only
Headcount12–20 seats (sweet spot before restaurant private-room pricing explodes)
Mix50% clients / 50% prospects (prospects = decision-makers: owner, CMO, marketing director, founder)
Plus-oneRequired unlock — every invite allows +1 (spouse/partner/colleague). Cuts no-shows.
PitchZero pitch. No slide deck. No “quick 10 minutes on Brandastic.” Relationship only.
StaffJustin + 1 (Sandy or Melissa or AM). One ops owner runs logistics end-to-end.

Invite list machine

  1. Pull top 40 clients by health + expansion potential
  2. Pull 40 prospects: past proposals, warm intros, OC network, podcast guests, agency peers who buy services
  3. Score: can buy ≥$3–5K/mo or project ≥$15K
  4. Invite 25 to fill 15 (assume ~40% decline/no-show without plus-one; better with plus-one)
  5. CRM tag: event_YYYY_MM + attended/no-show

Invite copy (adopt structure)

Short. Human. No agenda PDF.

“We’re doing a small pasta night in OC on [date] — 15 people, clients + friends, plus-ones welcome. No pitch. Just a good night. Want two seats?”

Send from Justin. Follow-up once. Same-day reminder with address + parking.

Post-event booking path (without being sleazy)

  • T+1: Thank-you text/email + 1 photo. No CTA.
  • T+3: Justin personal note to each prospect: “Enjoyed meeting you — if useful, happy to look at [specific thing they mentioned].”
  • T+7: AM/Justin asks clients for one intro only if relationship is warm (never cold-ask night-of).
  • CRM: log who met whom; create tasks for open loops they volunteered.
  • Success metrics: show-up rate, # new meetings in 30 days, # referrals in 60 days, $ pipeline influenced — not “brand awareness.”

Owner + tools

Owner: Sandy (ops) + Justin (host/invite). Melissa helps prospect list + sales follow-up. Aimme tracks RSVPs in CRM.

Tools: simple spreadsheet or HubSpot/whatever CRM list · Luma/Partiful optional · Venmo/company card for venue.

B. Gifting OS (under $100, no approval theater)

Rules

  • Any AM/strategist can request a gift for a real human reason
  • Cap $100 default (exceptions need Justin/Sandy)
  • Personal > expensive (jersey, book, local favorite, baby gift)
  • Ops fulfills within 2 business days
  • Thank-you messages looped back to requester (keeps behavior alive)
  • Do not turn into spammy points program — kindness first

Brandastic workflow

  1. Slack channel #client-love
  2. Form: who / why / address / idea / budget
  3. Erin or Aimme buys + ships (ops lane)
  4. Log in CRM: gift_sent + reason
  5. Monthly spend review (not per-gift approval)

Starter monthly pool: $500–$1,000. Raise after proof.

D. Referral ask system

  • After a clear win (launch, lead goal hit, creative they love): ask for one named intro
  • Script: “Who is one peer who complains about [problem we just solved]?”
  • Make intro easy: 2-sentence blurb they can forward
  • Bonus: small internal spiff for closed-won referral (cash or gift card) — optional, keep ethical
  • Track: referral_asked / intro_made / won

E. Honest loss + sales debriefs

Lost client channel

Slack #client-offboarding. Within 5 days of notice:

  1. Run a fixed AI prompt on call notes/emails (root cause)
  2. Post prompt + output publicly to leadership
  3. AM adds context only — cannot delete AI findings
  4. One fix assigned with owner/date

Sales call debrief

If Justin or Melissa is on a pitch:

  • 5–10 min after every call
  • Pacing · multi-thread · pricing moment · missing DM · deck fix
  • Log 1 improvement to pitch library same day

06 · Pillar 4 · Team OS

How marketing runs week to week

adopt the operating system: autonomy, written updates, ship fast, craft feedback after live — without making Justin the approval bottleneck.

Every growth/marketing owner posts in public Slack by EOD Thursday:

  • Why it mattered (metric or learning)
  • Next week (3 bullets max)
  • Stuck (if any)

Ship rules

  • Missed feedback twice → performance conversation

07 · Pillar 5 · Paid media bridge

Keep paid — stop letting it be the only engine

What paid is for

  • Fill pipeline gaps while events/customer marketing ramp
  • Retarget event attendees + content readers
  • Prove offers quickly
  • Client delivery excellence (still a product line)

What paid is not for

  • Permanent substitute for weak retention/referrals
  • Ego spend without CAC vs LTV honesty
  • Covering broken sales follow-up

Brandastic paid GTM rules

  1. Separate client delivery paid from Brandastic new-business paid budgets
  2. New-business paid has a monthly cap reviewed against meetings booked from events + referrals
  3. Creative standards: short hooks, weekly kill list of losers
  4. Every paid landing page pairs with a content hub (SEO compounding)
  5. Sam owns craft; Justin owns offer + capacity to close
