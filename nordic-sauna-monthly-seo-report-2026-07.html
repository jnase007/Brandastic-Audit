Opportunity

Nordic Sauna holds 4.2% AI share of voice with 116 mentions (16.6% mention share), 152 own-domain citations (2.1%), and a strong 95% favorable sentiment — but average answer position (3.02) lags Finnleo (2.64) and Klafs (2.79). Finnleo leads the set at 7.3% SOV and 193 mentions. AI already recognizes Nordic for architect-led, code-critical, commercial, and custom residential work, yet the brand is often omitted from broad discovery answers and third-party buyer guides (YouTube, Reddit, Sun Home, Haven of Heat). The strategic tension is clear: excellent sentiment and #1 positioning when surfaced, insufficient frequency in the guides that shape shortlists. Name confusion with similarly named brands is an additional brand-safety risk.

Recommendations
  • Sharpen the portfolio around complex custom residential, architect-led projects, and commercial/accessibility builds — and explicitly qualify out low-fit kit demand.
  • Win citations on the third-party guides AI already uses (YouTube roundups, Reddit threads, havenofheat, Tahoe Sauna Company comparisons) rather than relying only on owned pages.
  • Publish independent reviews, TCO / 10-15 year durability proof, and clear not-affiliated disambiguation to protect brand safety and premium positioning.
  • Build a Nordic Authority Hub that centralizes turnkey, architect-integration, and ADA content so AI treats Nordic as the decision framework, not just one vendor option.
