01 · Goal
What success looks like
Connect on LinkedIn → earn the right to email (newsletter) → stay visible with useful social posts → book teardowns → start pilots.

1. LinkedIn

Grow and activate connections with consultants first (then lenders / developers). Conversations, not vanity follows.

2. Newsletter

Monthly brief to people who opted in or engaged — 2 useful reads + one clear pilot CTA.

3. Social posting

Steady LinkedIn posts from founders using your existing network — tips, pains, proof, soft CTA.

North star metric for 90 days: conversations → teardowns → pilots on 1–2 live loans.

04 · Communication rules
One spine, three accents
COMPLY helps you run construction draws as a system — cleaner packages, less chase, bank-ready trail. Humans still decide.

To consultants

Faster monthly monitoring without giving up judgment. Teardown → pilot.

To lenders

Consistent, bank-ready draws across the book. Joint pilot with your consultant.

To developers

Get draws through the first time. Fewer kicks, faster funding.

Voice

Peer, plain, short. Field words: pay-app, draw, G702/G703, lien waiver, monitoring. No “AI age” hype. No feature dumps.

BRANDASTIC11
06 · Social posts
What LinkedIn posts look like

2–3 posts / week from founders. Short. One idea. Soft CTA. Use your existing network.

Post A · pain
Most construction draw delays aren’t “bad GCs.” They’re incomplete packages — missing waivers, fuzzy G702 math, no single checklist. If you run monitoring for lenders, what’s the #1 thing that still lives in email?
Post B · point of view
Your product isn’t a PDF. It’s a defensible recommendation to the bank. Anything that makes that recommendation faster and cleaner — without removing your judgment — is worth testing on 1–2 live loans.
