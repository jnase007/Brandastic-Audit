Where COMPLY sits — plain truth

What we are

A workspace for people who live in construction draw packages every month — pay apps, waivers, plan & cost, bank-ready trail.

Pilot on comply.capital at about $300 / project / month.

What we are not (yet)

Not the full bank loan OS on day one. Not a feature war with Built. Not an “AI age” pitch. Not a developer-billed platform copy of Rabbet.

Strengths to run at

  • Real pain: monthly monitoring grind
  • CMAA warm track already running — don’t rebuild it
  • Room to scale with cold / network / Apollo sequences next
  • Low trial bar vs enterprise bank software
  • Consultant wedge bank-first vendors underserve
  • Founders who can do live package walkthroughs

Gaps we must close

  • Lock full price band with Tom/Jerod
  • Get 1–5 live pilot proof stories
  • Stay disciplined: consultants ~70% · developers ~20% · lenders third (not ideal outbound)
  • Don’t get pulled into bank RFP theater early
  • Write down time-saved / miss-reduced proof

02 · Rabbet deep dive
Their money model vs ours

Working owner numbers until a live sheet is pasted in:

Rabbet

Hook the bank · bill the builder

  • Lender ~$150 per deal — easy yes
  • Get software on real loans
  • Pull developers onto the system
  • Developers carry ~$300–$800 / deal / month

Smart bank-first SaaS. Not our first door.

COMPLY

Hook the consultant · earn the bank

  • Consultant trial ~$300 / project / month
  • 1–2 live jobs · real packages
  • Prove speed + fewer misses
  • Lender follows because their reviewer already runs cleaner
  • Developers only on the same loan path
Rabbet starts with the bank, and developers pay most of the bill. COMPLY starts with the construction consultant the bank already uses — then we show the bank it works.

Tom / Jerod line when they say “Rabbet”

“They run the loan platform and put most of the bill on developers. We help the firm the bank already hired get through monthly draws faster — with fewer misses — starting on 1–2 live jobs at about $300 a project a month. You still make every call.”

02 · Total addressable market
Commercial deals × $300 / draw

Goal: 3% of this market ≈ $3M (conservative). Full market at $300/draw ≈ ~$100M.

TAM is not “all of CRE.” It’s active U.S. commercial construction deals that run monthly draws — priced at COMPLY’s per-project fee.

How we size it

  • U.S. banks hold ~$450B+ construction & land-development loans (C&D).
  • Broader construction-loan book (banks + others) can run higher (~$600B+ class).
  • At ~$15–25M average active loan → rough ~20k–35k financed projects in motion.
  • Working band for draw-monitored commercial deals: ~15k–40k projects (labeled assumption).
  • COMPLY fee: $300–$600 / project / month → $3.6k–$7.2k / project / year.

Methodology · what changes the game
We know exactly who to sell to

Most competitors spray the market. We don’t. Before we spend time on anyone, we already know the names, numbers, and fit — double-checked.

Our edge

  • Exact target market — not a vague industry list
  • Names + numbers — real firms and people, not guesses
  • Apollo + human review — pulled the data, then double-checked every row
  • Ideal-fit only — Fit A consultants first; reject GC/mega platforms/competitor SW
  • Time is protected — no outreach until the account clears the bar

How we use it

  • Build the list once → company-first, not title-only
  • Score fit (A-core / secondary / no)
  • Only then: LinkedIn · email · walkthrough
  • CMAA warm track stays separate; cold lists are cleaned the same way
  • Result: every hour Tom & Jerod spend is on a real buyer

Why this beats spray-and-pray software sales

Rabbet and bank platforms sell wide. We sell narrow on purpose: the firms that already live in monthly draw packages. The list is the product of the methodology — so the motion stays sharp and the $300/draw path to 3% / $3M stays honest.
