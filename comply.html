FAQ

Who is this pilot for?

Lender’s construction consultants — firms that do monthly pay-app / draw inspection, plan & cost review, and related monitoring for banks. Not general contractors.

Does AI approve draws?

No. COMPLY helps organize, cross-check, and flag. Your team reviews and decides.

What do we need for the teardown?

Ideally one redacted draw package (pay app + supporting docs). If you can’t share one yet, we’ll still walk the flow with a sample structure.

How long is the pilot?

Typically about 30 days on 1–2 active loans, with a short weekly check-in.
