Opportunity

JB White, DDS holds 0.2% Google AI Mode share of voice (Nevada locale) with 2 mentions, 2 own-domain citations (0.2%), 100% favorable sentiment, and average answer position 3.0. Smile Oasis leads at 10.8% SOV; Sierra Smiles, Summerlin Dental Solutions, and Vegas Choice Dental fill the next tier. AI already respects clinical quality and natural aesthetics, but almost never recommends JB White on high-intent cosmetic and implant queries — presence is mostly by-name, not category. Weekday-only / single-location constraints read as a gap unless reframed as boutique focus. Citation weight sits with Google, Modento, Adit, and competitor booking sites — not jbwhitedds.com.

Recommendations
  • Own the boutique model explicitly: fewer patients, more focus, protected complex-reconstruction blocks — schedule and setting as design choices, not weaknesses.
  • Publish Doctor & Philosophy, Complex Case Center, Natural Aesthetic Makeovers, and Fly-In Program pages AI can cite for redo veneers, full-mouth work, and destination patients.
  • Bring finance into the clinical narrative with phased treatment pathways and a clear ‘Your Options at a Glance’ visual so premium pricing feels controlled, not upsold.
  • Seed Q&A-style guides and case stories that mirror Google AI Mode questions so JB White moves from name-search only into category recommendations.
