Opportunity

On ChatGPT (CA), Rogers | Beltran holds 1.0% share of voice (~0.96% in the narrative), 7 mentions (4% mention share), average position 2.29, 5 own-domain citations (0.2%), and 100% favorable sentiment. Quality is not the gap — presence is. Eisner Gorin leads this peer set at 1.6% SOV / 13 mentions; Jacoby & Meyers, Hadsell Stormer, and Burg & Brock sit at 0.4 / 0.4 / 0.3. 94.4% of voice sits in the long tail (Panish, Callahan, Compass, BD&J, Wikipedia-class sources). ChatGPT already frames the firm as a hands-on bilingual trial boutique for serious SoCal PI + criminal + civil-rights overlap, then hedges on mega trucking/national-scale matters. Priorities: pick boutique vs catastrophic-elite, a trucking hub, a Client Experience Charter, and more fact-pattern pages to keep the 2.29 rank working.

Recommendations
  • Publish a one-line brand promise (partner-led, bilingual trial boutique for serious SoCal cases where one incident creates multiple legal problems) on the site, AI Instructions page, third-party profiles, and intake.
  • Add an honest right-fit guide (typical case size, when to use small-claims/PD, when to co-counsel mega-firms) so AI stops parking the firm in an undefined middle band.
  • Build a trucking/catastrophic hub and overlapping PI+criminal+civil-rights fact-pattern pages so ChatGPT can reuse the 2.29 average rank.
  • Keep the AI Instructions page as a living brief (ideal clients, declined matters, preferred phrasing, recent results) and earn more third-party listings beyond the 5 own-domain citations.
