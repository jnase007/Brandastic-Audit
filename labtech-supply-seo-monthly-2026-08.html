Opportunity

On ChatGPT (CA), LabTech holds 5.7% share of voice, 36 mentions (20.6% mention share), average position 3.33, 56 own-domain citations (3.4%), and 78% favorable sentiment. That is enough to be on the list, not enough to be first: Hanson (14.13% SoV, pos 2.19) and Laboratory by Design (8.88%, pos 2.69) still own California lab-furniture shortlists, and hansonlab.com leads citations (181 / 11%) versus labtechsupplyco.com at 56. Versus the export peer set LabTech still leads PSA (3.1%), EZAD (3.9%), Labs USA (2.2%), and Norlab (0.8%). ChatGPT already praises customization, heavy-duty benches, speed, and California service — then hedges on turnkey delivery, SEFA/seismic proof, and large regulated builds. Priorities are an explicit GC/MEP partnership story, niche ownership vs Hanson, certification proof to lift sentiment toward 90%, and documented turnkey furniture scopes.

Recommendations
  • Move from always-on-the-list to frequently-first in 2-3 use cases: custom heavy-duty benches for analytical instruments, fast-turn casework for California startups, and custom instrument tables with cutouts and supports.
  • Publish load ratings, lead times, California references, SEFA/seismic summaries, and partner case studies on pages ChatGPT can quote; keep product URLs stable.
  • Segment GTM: win mid-scale custom/schedule-sensitive work; for flagship architect-led buildings, position as the nimble furniture specialist partnering with the GC and spec brand.
  • Name speed programs (e.g. QuickBench / QuickCase) and document a repeatable turnkey-furniture scope plus GC/MEP partnerships so AI can describe breadth beyond benches.
