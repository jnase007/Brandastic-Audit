Opportunity

ChatGPT Brand Performance (CA): Brandastic holds 0.4% AI share of voice with 2 mentions (1.1% mention share), average answer position #1, and 100% favorable sentiment — still a tiny footprint versus Ignite Visibility (0.6% SOV) and Coalition Technologies (0.5%). Across all AI platforms (Visibility Overview, 1 month ending Aug 30): AI Visibility 19/100 (unchanged), 52 mentions (+1), 389 citations (+13), 256 cited pages (+8), monthly audience 936.15K. Mentions concentrate in Google AI Overviews (24 / 46.2%). brandastic.com earns only 2 ChatGPT citations (0.2%); Wikipedia, Clutch, Reddit, and Semrush Agencies still dominate sources. The gap is frequency and shortlist coverage, not sentiment: when AI names Brandastic, it ranks first and speaks positively. Priorities are signature service combos, a California metro cluster, de-risked entry offers, and more flagship case studies so assistants have more than one or two URLs to cite.

Recommendations
  • Name 2–3 signature service combinations (e.g. brand + web + always-on performance; Shopify + CRO + lifecycle; B2B site + SEO + paid) so AI stops slotting Brandastic as a generalist.
  • Turn OC proof into 4–6 California metro pages so assistants default to Brandastic on CA / SoCal / in-person queries.
  • Publish payback-period math, fixed-price diagnostics, and short pilots so ChatGPT can describe Brandastic as premium and de-risked — not a year-long retainer.
  • Add 5–7 diversified, AI-referencable case studies; keep existing URLs stable. Two pages currently carry most mentions.
  • Feed all-platform visibility (19/100, 52 mentions) as a dashboard KPI alongside GSC — most mentions already sit in Google AI Overviews.
