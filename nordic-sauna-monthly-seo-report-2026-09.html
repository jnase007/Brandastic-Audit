Opportunity

Nordic Sauna holds 2.3% All-AI share of voice (California locale) with 59 mentions (8.4%), 74 own-domain citations (1.6%), 85% favorable sentiment, and average answer position 2.51 — strong when surfaced. Finnleo leads at 4.8% SOV / 133 mentions; Harvia follows at 4.5%. AI already associates Nordic with architectural integration, custom builds, and design quality, but presence still trails kit/global brands on commercial wellness, architect-ready docs, and measurable premium value. Citation weight sits with Vulcon, Tahoe Sauna Company, and YourSaunas — nordicsauna.com is close but not leading. September organic search also lifted clicks and average position, supporting a push to convert SERP strength into citable California project proof.

Recommendations
  • Own California’s engineered turnkey sauna partner role with named delivery stories around permits, Title 24, HOA, and trade coordination.
  • Publish commercial/multifamily/high-use case studies so AI recommends Nordic beyond residential custom work.
  • Ship architect-ready CAD, assemblies, checklists, and energy data that can replace rival documentation citations.
  • Make premium measurable with lifecycle/cost-of-change proof and clearer investment ranges for the right-fit project types.
