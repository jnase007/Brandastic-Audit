Shopify access

This page is for Shopify store access so our team can help with the store.

Invite this email

info@brandastic.com

Share this page with the store owner if needed:
https://audit.brandastic.com/shopify

1. Open Shopify admin

Sign in at your store admin (usually your-store.myshopify.com/admin) as the owner or someone who can add staff.

2. Add Brandastic as staff

Go to Settings → Users and permissions → Add staff → enter info@brandastic.com → send invite.

3. Turn on the right permissions

At minimum for SEO / CRO / tracking work: Themes; Pages / Products / Collections / Navigation as needed; Apps; any Settings needed for domains, pixels, or customer events.

4. Approve if Shopify asks

We’ll accept from info@brandastic.com. If Shopify asks you to approve the new staff member or finish a security check, complete that and tell us.

Optional: collaborator access

If you use collaborator requests instead of staff, approve Brandastic under Settings → Users and permissions.
