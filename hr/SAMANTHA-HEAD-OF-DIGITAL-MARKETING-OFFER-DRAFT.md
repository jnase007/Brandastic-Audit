**Title:** Head of Digital Marketing

## Opening
Samantha O'Leary,

We are pleased to update your role to **Head of Digital Marketing** at Brandastic. This change reflects the evolution of your responsibilities toward department leadership, strategic oversight, and the highest-value client relationships, while continuing to personally engage with select accounts.

## Primary role
As Head of Digital Marketing, you will lead the digital marketing function, setting strategy and direction for paid media and SEO, managing team capacity and performance, and owning executive-level relationships on priority accounts. Your focus shifts from day-to-day account management across the full client list to department leadership and the highest-complexity/priority client work.

## Responsibilities

### Department
- Paid media strategy and direction
- SEO oversight
- Resource and capacity planning
- Managing Jonathan, CJ, and future hires/contractors
- QA standards
- Processes and documentation
- New platform capabilities
- Technical/tracking escalations
- Own digital marketing end-to-end: paid media, SEO, and how those channels work together for clients
- Lead hiring for the digital team (paid, SEO, and contractors), with final offer approval from Justin
- Work with Sandy (Director of Operations) so digital work follows Brandastic marketing SOPs and client-delivery standards
- Set clear digital and channel strategy (especially SEO + paid), and manage Jonathan, CJ, and future digital hires to that standard

### Accounts
- Own executive/client relationships on priority accounts
- Set strategic direction
- Review performance and recommendations
- Handle escalations
- Personally involved only in the highest-complexity/priority accounts
- Identify upsell and cross-sell opportunities

You will continue to personally touch accounts, with your workload oriented toward department leadership and the highest-value client relationships rather than day-to-day management of the full client list.

### How this pairs with Account Managers (Stacy & Maxwell)
Stacy and Maxwell are both **Account Managers**. They own client strategy and relationship. You own digital strategy and the digital team. You collaborate ongoing; they do not report into you for relationship ownership, and you do not replace them as the client strategy owner on the full book.

## Requirements
- Proven experience managing high-volume paid media campaigns with strong ROI results
- Expertise in Google Ads, Paid Social (Meta, LinkedIn, etc.), and digital marketing platforms
- Strong understanding of the full marketing funnel and biddable media
- Proficiency with Google Tag Manager, analytics (GA4), keyword research tools, and advanced Excel/Sheets
- Experience managing budgets of $10,000–$50,000+/month
- Excellent communication, analytical, and leadership skills
- Preferred: Google Ads and Google Analytics (GA4) certifications

## KPIs
All KPIs are determined by Brandastic ownership and are subject to change based on company needs and performance. Performance will be evaluated during quarterly and annual reviews. Key expectations include:
- Measurable improvements in department efficiency, team output, and client ROI
- Effective leadership of the digital marketing function (strategy, capacity, processes, and quality)
- Strong ownership of priority client relationships and identification of growth opportunities
- Ongoing progress in strategic alignment with Brandastic's goals
