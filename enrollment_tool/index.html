The goal

Give the school a campus and channel view of inquiries, applications, and enrolled students, with a sample cost per associated enrollment. The figure is an association. It is not causal proof and it is not a performance promise.

The gap

A lead tracker answers who inquired. The admissions office answers who applied and who enrolled. Those records live in different places. This sample is the reporting layer in between, not a second lead form and not a replacement for call tracking.

What this page is

A proof of concept with built-in fictional rows. It shows the screen. It does not pull WhatConverts, read FACTS, or upload anything to Google Ads. Conversion feedback on this page is a simulation, not a live sync.

The path

Sources, school-approved outcomes, then the insight.

WhatConverts stays in place. A school-approved admissions status is the only thing that can move an inquiry into an application or an enrollment in this concept. An ads feedback record is optional, and on this page it is not sent.

How a pilot would work

Manual and narrow before any connection.

The first step in the concept is a conversation and a minimum file, not an integration project. This page is the picture of the report so the team can react before anyone commits.

  1. 1Keep WhatConverts.

    Lead and call capture stay where they are. This report reads a sample shaped like that export. It does not replace the tracker.

  2. 2Leave admissions in FACTS.

    Staff keep working in the school’s system of record. No FACTS module or API is assumed to be turned on.

  3. 3Start with one campus and a CSV.

    Someone the school authorizes exports the minimum fields: an application or family id, campus, status, date, and a shared key if the school agrees. Names and sensitive student fields stay out of the file.

  4. 4Reconcile, then show aggregates.

    Rows join when the shared key is on both sides. Missing keys, inquiries with no status yet, and enrollments with no inquiry stay visible. The result is a campus and channel report, not a claim that an ad caused an enrollment.

  5. 5Let ads learn only after the school confirms.

    WhatConverts keeps tracking. The new piece is the school’s outcome. Google or Meta could be told later. This page does not send it and does not promise better results.

Still to confirm with the school

  • Which FACTS products are licensed, and who can export admissions status.
  • Which identifier the school will allow on a minimum file.
  • Whether click identifiers are present on the existing WhatConverts leads.
  • The real online eligibility list. The states in the sample are fictional.

Public product pages were used only to shape the sample fields. They are not a connection and they are not a statement of what this school has enabled.
