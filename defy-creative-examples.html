1Targeted audiences & messaging

Who we talk to, what they care about, and the message each channel leads with. Same brand world — different entry points.

A1 · Primary

Outdoor regulars (trail / hike / camp)

Who: 25–45, weekend adventurers, already buy outdoor gear, hate logo tax.

Job-to-be-done: Reliable polarized shades that survive real use without $200+ markup.

Primary message: Premium trail performance. Real-world price.

Proof: HD polarized · bio-based durability · lifetime warranty · 30-day trial · $50–70

Channels: Meta prospect · Reels · Welcome email · Google outdoor intent

A2 · Primary

Water + drive glare fighters

Who: Boating, fishing, paddle, long highway commute — glare is the enemy.

Job-to-be-done: Cut glare, keep clarity, don’t fog/slip drama.

Primary message: Built for water, road, and hard light — not just Instagram trails.

Proof: Polarized HD · use-case lifestyle · trial removes fit fear

Channels: Meta interests · TikTok use-case · Cart email · SMS abandon

A3 · Secondary

Value-premium switchers

Who: Own Ray-Ban / Costa / Oakley / goodr energy — overpaid or underwhelmed.

Job-to-be-done: Same (or better) utility without status tax or toy feel.

Primary message: $250 energy. $70 reality. Stop paying for the logo.

Proof: Price framing · warranty/trial stack · Aspen hero

Channels: Prospecting ads · Hook tests · careful conquest

A4 · Secondary

Sustainability-minded buyers

Who: Care about materials, not greenwash. Want strength + planet-minded story.

Job-to-be-done: Feel good about the buy without fragile “eco” product.

Primary message: Sustainable shouldn’t mean fragile. 58% bio-based Grivory. Built to take a beating.

Proof: EMS Grivory Greenline · durability demos · packaging story

Channels: Organic education · Welcome email 2 · Meta broad/interest

A5 · Always-on

Site visitors / ATC (retarget)

Who: Viewed PDP or added to cart — warm interest, residual fear.

Job-to-be-done: Resolve fit / quality / price hesitation.

Primary message: Your pair is waiting — 30 days risk-free + lifetime warranty.

Proof: Trust stack · ships ~2 days · free ship $55+

Channels: Meta retarget · Cart flow · Browse abandon · SMS if consented

A6 · Growth

Buyers → second pair / advocates

Who: Purchasers inside/after trial window.

Job-to-be-done: Second use-case pair + UGC.

Primary message: Different light, different frame. Stress-test them — then show us.

Proof: Post-purchase care · warranty save · UGC repost · bundle later

Channels: Post-purchase email · UGC organic · Winback / bundle

AI creative production notes

You’re already using AI imagery on-site (e.g. mountain hero). Lean into it — with guardrails.

Do

  • Consistent frame geometry / colorways matching real SKUs
  • Adventure environments: ridge, alpine lake, desert highway, overcast trail
  • Mix AI plates + real UGC faces for trust
  • Batch 10–20 variants per territory weekly
  • Disclose if/when platform or legal requires

Don’t

  • Impossible product morphing (wrong temples, logo drift)
  • Generic “stock happy hiker” with no product clarity
  • Over-glamor soft lighting that hides lens/frame detail
  • Claim performance AI can’t prove — keep claims product-true

Operator line: AI creative is already on-brand on-site. This pack turns it into a testing machine across paid, organic, and email — so every dollar learns faster.

Cadence & kill/keep

Operating rules

  • Territories first — every post/ad/email maps to one of the 5 pillars (+ UGC hybrid).
  • Test weekly — 3 hooks × 2 bodies × 2 formats; kill bottom quartile; scale only on repeat signal.
  • Flows before campaigns — welcome, cart, post-purchase live before promo blasts.
  • AI + UGC — Imagen/Veo for speed plates; real humans for trust. Same brand world.
  • SKU lock later — mockups are strategy comps; swap real Aspen geometry when assets land.

  • IG/TikTok organic: 4–6 posts/week + Stories daily
  • Paid: always-on Meta + weekly creative refresh
  • Email: flows first · 1–2 campaigns/mo after flows
