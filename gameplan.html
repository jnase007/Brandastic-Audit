Why this

Opens are not the problem. Replies are. Asking them to book a free audit is still a pitch. Sending the audit is proof.

What we are doing now

  • Apollo ICP into Instantly
  • Email says get a free audit, talk to someone
  • High open rate
  • Almost no replies

What we are switching to

  • We already ran SEO + AEO on their domain
  • Email is a live URL with their numbers
  • They can see where they are failing
  • It is real before they ever talk to us

The loop

People come in from Apollo. Work happens at the domain. Everyone at that company gets the same link.

05 Instantly

Lead gets {{audit_url}} plus 3 gaps.

06 Send

Instantly sequence emails the URL. Replies come back to us.

What they click

Not Elevate. Not Fountain Life. Those are sales-room builds. Cold gets a private SEO + AEO scorecard they can read in a minute. No PPC module.

On the page

  1. SEO health from Semrush: traffic, keywords, rank, top terms, competitors, referring domains
  2. AEO / extractability from the site: schema, FAQPage, Organization NAP, H1, llms.txt, indexability
  3. Three SEO/AEO failures, with their numbers
  4. Three SEO/AEO moves
  5. CTA to talk. Paid media is a call question, not a cold-page claim

URL rules

  • Live path: https://audit.brandastic.com/p/{slug}
  • noindex, nofollow. Not in sitemap. Not on /portal. Not on the homepage.
  • Same URL for every contact at that company
  • UTM: utm_source=instantly&utm_campaign=seo-aeo-audit&utm_content={slug}

Guardrails

ICP lock. $3–20M. Retain $5–20k/mo. Advertise / close only $10k+/mo. Founder, Owner, CEO first. Then Marketing Director / Head of Marketing / VP Marketing. Skip CMO, creatives, specialists, coordinators.
SEO + AEO only. Semrush is accurate enough on organic. It is not the source of truth for Google Ads. No PPC audit, no tool ad-spend number, no “you are wasting $X on paid.” Paid media comes up after they are on a call.
AEO is extractability, not ChatGPT rank. We cannot score AI mentions cheaply at scale. We can score whether a model can read who they are from the site.
Quality over volume. First batch 10–25. Then 50/week. A thin audit is worse than “book a call.”
