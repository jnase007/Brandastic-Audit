INTERPRETATION Traditional clicks vs Generative AI visibility
October organic clicks were 44 versus 48 in September (-8.3%), while Google Generative AI impressions rose to 204 from 97 (+110.3%). Higher AI exposure is an additional form of search visibility, but it does not independently prove that AI Overviews caused the modest click change. Site-wide average position softened (21.3 → 27.1). Homepage and information-page AI impressions led the Generative AI set. These are impressions only — not citations, qualified visits, or new patients.
  • Keep homepage, information, and complex-care pages tightly aligned to decision-stage questions (revision, second opinion, conservative vs comprehensive) so AI Overviews and classic results share the same proof.
  • Expand documented reconstruction / redo case stories so rising Generative AI visibility has citable clinical proof, not only brand pages.
