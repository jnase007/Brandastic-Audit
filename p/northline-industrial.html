Where you are failing

1. Traffic is down 31% with 86 terms stuck on page 2

Google still knows the domain. It is not awarding the manufacturer / supplier queries that sit one page away. Brand is fine. Category is not.

2. AI systems cannot extract who you are

No Organization or FAQ schema. H1 is a slogan. No llms.txt. Footer NAP does not match About. Models have to guess the company, category, and location.

3. Category pages lose to thinner, clearer competitors

Money pages read like product dumps. Competitors own “industrial conveyor manufacturer” intent with entity-clear titles and FAQs.

What to do

1. Recover the page-2 cluster

Rewrite the 8 highest-intent category pages around buyer language: manufacturer, food-grade, stainless, pallet. Not slogans.

2. Make the company extractable
