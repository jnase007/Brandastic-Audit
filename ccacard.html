  • Official handles exist on IG, TikTok, and X under ccacard.

What “bot trends” means on this audit

We measure

  • Fake or low-quality followers
  • Inorganic likes / comments / saves patterns
  • Sudden spikes that don’t match content quality
  • Spam, scraper, and “grading DM” bot behavior
  • Whether bots are inflating vanity metrics
  • Hobby-wide bot waves that hit card accounts

We deliver

  • Channel-by-channel snapshot
  • Organic vs suspicious engagement view
  • Timeline of notable spikes / dips
  • Cleanup list (block, delete, ignore, change)
  • 30-day monitor plan
  • Optional monthly bot watch

Method (how Brandastic will run it)

  1. Account map — confirm every official CCA property (including Discord/FB if any).
  2. Baseline pull — 90-day posting, growth, and engagement shape.
  3. Bot signals — follower cohorts, comment samples, spike anomaly checks.
  4. Hobby context — TCG/grading spam patterns vs real collector engagement.
  5. Score + fixes — plain-English scorecard and prioritized actions.
  6. Monitor — optional weekly/monthly bot watch so metrics stay honest.
