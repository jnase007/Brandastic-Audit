$10,000 on Google is not enough to buy 10 meetings at today’s efficiency. 3 meetings × $10,000 means each Clarity-ready meeting costs about $3,333. Ten meetings at the same rate is about $33,000 / month in Google. We either lower cost per meeting (tracking, page, offer, creative) or we raise spend. Paid social does not replace that math — it only helps if it books meetings cheaper than $3,333 or adds meetings Google cannot.

What the close rate does to the year

36 meetings / year ÷ 6 = 6 signed jobs today. $1.7M ÷ 6 ≈ $260,000 average job (use this, not $260). $3M needs about 11 jobs = about 66 meetings = about 5–6 qualified meetings / month if close stays 1 in 6 and the ticket stays $260,000. That 11-job count is the old ticket. The deal-size ramp above keeps about 6 jobs and reaches $3M by raising the ticket instead. The “10 meetings” goal is how you overshoot $3M and start the $8M path (~20 jobs × $283k ≈ $5.7M).

12-month picture — leads, meetings, closes

Lead = inquiry (form/call). About 4 inquiries per qualified meeting. Spend held at $10,000 / month (what Enclave is spending on Google now). Cost per lead = $10,000 ÷ leads that month. Cost per meeting = $10,000 ÷ meetings. Today that is ~$3,333 / meeting because only 3 meetings come in. The later months only work if tracking and creative actually produce more leads on the same $10k.
