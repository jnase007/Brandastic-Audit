Opportunity

American Tri-Star Insurance holds only 0.1% AI share of voice with 3 mentions (0.4% mention share), 3 own-domain citations (0.1%), and average answer position 3.0 — against national carriers State Farm (2.5% SOV, 103 mentions), Progressive (1.9%), Travelers (1.9%), and Mercury (1.8%). Favorable sentiment is perfect at 100%, and AI already associates the brand with high-touch, relationship-first service and California SME fit (contractors, trades, hospitality, trucking, bilingual businesses). The gap is frequency and shortlist power: insurance.ca.gov, directories, and national carriers dominate citations while americantristarinsurance.com is nearly invisible. Strategic priorities are codifying the relationship-insurance promise, seeding citable profiles for 10–12 new mentions, publishing claims outcome proof, and launching niche vertical pages so AI recommends Tri-Star for specific California SMB segments.

Recommendations
  • Codify a clear relationship-first / independent multi-carrier promise and publish it consistently across site, profiles, directories, and partner content.
  • Seed 10–12 structured public profiles and citable pages to lift SOV from ~0.1% and get into AI shortlists on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and AI Overviews.
  • Add 5 concrete claims case studies and service standards (including 24-hour response SLAs) so advocacy and outcomes are visible, not assumed.
  • Launch 3–5 proof-rich vertical pages (contractors, trucking, landlords, SMBs) and standardize bilingual English–Spanish positioning; add schema/FAQs so the site is AI-readable.
