Opportunity

On ChatGPT (California locale), Rogers | Beltran holds 0.4% share of voice with 5 mentions (2.9% mention share), 6 own-domain citations (0.4%), 80% favorable sentiment, and average answer position 3.8. Named competitors Eisner Gorin and Hadsell Stormer each sit near 1.1% SOV with far denser mentions. The firm surfaces on fit-specific prompts (prior record + injury; PI + criminal under one roof; working-class flexible communication) but is largely absent from broad civil-rights and catastrophic-injury shortlists. Citation weight remains with google.com, gbw.law, panish.law, and hadsellstormer.com — not rogersbeltran.com. October organic clicks and Generative AI impressions both declined MoM, reinforcing the need for proof-dense content rather than generic AI marketing copy.

Recommendations
  • Treat the AI Instructions page as a living briefing: who you serve, multi-front problems you solve, concrete trial examples, and bilingual support.
  • Publish 8–12 crash/trial and multi-front case capsules so ChatGPT and Google AI have quotable proof.
  • Build scenario pages for injury+criminal, police violence+criminal, and felony+civil-rights query archetypes.
  • Keep sentiment proof fresh (reviews/testimonials) while chasing >1% ChatGPT share of voice on high-intent prompts.

INTERPRETATION Traditional clicks vs Generative AI visibility
October organic clicks fell to 171 from 225 (−24.0%), while Google Generative AI impressions also declined to 7,962 from 9,795 (-18.7%). Both traditional and Generative AI visibility moved lower in the same month, so increased AI exposure does not explain the click drop. Average organic position also softened (12.9 → 14.2). AI Overviews/AI Mode can still answer informational questions without a site visit, but these figures are impressions only — not proof of citations, qualified traffic, or cases.
  • Refresh high-AI-impression practice guides (driving without a license / VC 12500, ghost gun, castle doctrine, stand your ground) with clearer answers, FAQs, and updated California citations so pages stay useful inside AI experiences and classic results.
  • Publish concrete multi-front case capsules (injury + criminal, civil rights + defense) so both Google AI surfaces and ChatGPT have documentable proof beyond generic firm bios.
